# Route B — Seoul → Cheonan → Busan → Seoul

Five sample itineraries for the **Cheonan** middle leg. Cheonan is the calmer, closer-in middle stop: the Independence Hall of Korea, walnut-pastry (hodu-gwaja) and sundae-soup food traditions, Gakwonsa's giant Buddha, and quick access to the Onyang/Asan hot-spring strip — with shorter, cheaper rail hops than Daejeon.
