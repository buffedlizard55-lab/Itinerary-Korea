## Who this suits

Travelers who want the rite, not just the roof: robes, pre-dawn chanting, monastic lunch (balwoo), meditation, and tea chat. You’ll keep the regular hotels for most nights and slot one templestay overnight where it fits the research window.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Hotel arrival **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Hotel Skypark Myeongdong 3** — dataset $95–135, Myeongdong. **Confirm 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number. Standard check-in 15:00.**
- **Alternates (also verify 24-hour desk):** Ibis Styles Myeongdong ($90–130) · Nine Tree Myeongdong 1 ($100–145) · L7 Myeongdong ($150–210).
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** CU/GS25 tonight — monastic meals are mindful, not midnight.

- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); Ibis Styles Myeongdong continuation.

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · **Templestay overnight (Option: Hwagyesa for foliage) OR one-day if overnight full**

- **Morning option A — Overnight Hwagyesa (Bukhansan foothills — foliage!):** Check in **Hwagyesa Temple** templestay via `eng.templestay.com` — day program ~15:00–next day 11:00 typically: temple tour, 108 prostrations, tea chat with sunim, evening bell tolling, pre-dawn chanting ~04:30, monastic balwoo breakfast. Autumn pine-maple behind the hall per fun/seoul.md Hwagyesa note — book 2–4 weeks ahead, ~₩70k–90k.
- **Morning option B — One-day Jogyesa/Myogaksa:** If overnight is full, do a **one-day templestay** at **Jogyesa or Myogaksa** (central, 09:00–16:00 variants via same portal) and sleep at **Ibis Styles** tonight — you still get robes/tea/108 bows.
- **Overnight stay:** **Hwagyesa Temple** (night 3/6 counted as temple, not hotel) — or **Ibis Styles** (night 3/6) if one-day program.
- **Plan B:** No slot this week → slot it into Cheonan week return or do **International Seon Center** (Yangcheon) one-day in English — same portal.
