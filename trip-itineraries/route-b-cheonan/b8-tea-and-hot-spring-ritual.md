## Who this suits

Visitors who want a reset, not a resort. You’ll learn how a Korean bath is actually done, sit through a proper tea service, and pair it with Independence Hall and Gakwonsa between soaks. Cheonan gets 6 nights — pace is the point.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Expect hotel **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun** — full-service desk, City Hall edge, calm recovery. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Korean address saved offline.
- **Late supper:** Convenience-store tea tonight — save the tea houses for tomorrow.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30).
- **Stay:** Seoul — Courtyard Namdaemun (night 1/6 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to **Ibis Styles Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then tea.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Tea opener
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to Ibis Styles Myeongdong.

- **Late morning:** Jogyesa Temple — lantern lane + incense courtyard (quiet circuit).
- **Afternoon:** Bukchon tea house — long sit; Gyeongbokgung outer gardens only today.
- **Evening:** Insadong early dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Ibis Styles Myeongdong (night 2/6).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Palaces, seated

- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung + National Folk Museum (both seated-friendly).
- **Afternoon:** Changdeokgung + Huwon Secret Garden pre-booked tour — Seoul’s stillest walk.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon dusk tea.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/6.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Herbal lane & bath

- **Morning:** Seoul Hanbang Jinheung Center (Jongno herbal center) — herbal foot-bath + sachet craft.
- **Afternoon:** Classic Seoul jjimjilbang session — Dragon Hill Spa Yongsan is the full-size classic (verify current hours/status) — learn the towel-hat, rice-drink ritual properly. Budget 3–4 hours.
- **Evening:** Light dinner after sauna; early bed.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/6.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Forest breathing

- **Morning:** Namsangol Hanok Village + Deoksugung Stonewall stroll.
- **Afternoon:** Seoul Forest — flat loop, deer corral, cafe bench.
- **Evening:** Seongsu non-alcohol tea bar.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/6.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Museum quiet

- **Morning:** National Museum of Korea (free).
- **Afternoon:** War Memorial of Korea — pick one hall, sit when needed.
- **Evening:** Pack for Cheonan.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/6.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul → Cheonan (transfer)

- **Morning:** Mugunghwa Seoul → Cheonan (~55–60 min) or Line 1 (~1 hr 20). Check in Sono Belle.
- **Afternoon:** Resort pool/sauna soft open (verify what’s included in stay) or local tea house if outside soak not included.
- **Evening:** Quiet Cheonan dinner; early night.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — Sono Belle Cheonan (night 1/6).

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Cheonan · Tea & hall

- **Late morning:** Independence Hall of Korea (free) — but today go for the tea house on campus and one exhibition hall only, not the full sprint.
- **Afternoon:** Hakwha hodu-gwaja origin shop — walnut pastry with tea, seated.
- **Evening:** Resort bath or hotel soak.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 2/6.

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Cheonan · Onyang history

- **Full day:** Historic **Onyang Hot Spring district** (Line 1): old bathhouses for day-bathing, soup alley lunch, Onyang Folk Museum for the town’s 600-year hot-spring story. This is the cultural bath, not the water-park.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 3/6.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Cheonan · Spavis modern

- **Full day:** **Asan Spavis** (modern theme-spa) — the contrast day to yesterday’s history. Allow half-day + travel.
- **Evening:** Back to Cheonan; light dinner.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 4/6.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Cheonan · Temple & tea

- **Morning:** Gakwonsa + giant Buddha (bus/taxi hill; seated courtyard tea).
- **Afternoon:** Taejosan Park gentle ridge or Arario Gallery seated show.
- **Evening:** Early night; Pepero Day (Nov 11) street stalls if you want the silly photo.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 5/6.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Cheonan · Buffer / soak

- **All day:** Free soak day — repeat favorite bath or just hotel rest + packing for Busan.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 6/6.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Cheonan → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Cheonan-Asan → Busan (~2 hr). Local hop to Cheonan-Asan confirmed.
- **Afternoon:** Haeundae beach flat + Dongbaek pine loop.
- **Evening:** Shinsegae Centum food hall.
- **Stay:** Busan — Grand Josun Busan (night 1/7).

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Spa Land marathon

- **Full day:** **Spa Land Centum City** — 22 themed baths, 2–3 hour circuit, then department store food hall. No other anchor today — let the spa be the plan.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli drone show (Sat, verify time) if you want night air; otherwise hotel bath.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/7.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Haedong early

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa **at opening (~07:30)** — sea cliffs before buses.
- **Afternoon:** Songjeong beach cafe tea.
- **Evening:** Early night.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/7.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Huinnyeoul calm

- **Morning:** Huinnyeoul Culture Village — cliff hamlet, hanok cafes with sea windows.
- **Afternoon:** UN Memorial Cemetery — pine rows, spare stillness.
- **Evening:** F1963 quiet edit cafe.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/7.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Coast by train

- **Morning:** Blueline Sky Capsule Mipo → Cheongsapo (book) + Daritdol skywalk.
- **Afternoon:** Cheongsapo fish + tea.
- **Evening:** Jeonpo tea row.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/7.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Flex

- **All day:** Taejongdae Danubi train *or* Beomeosa mountain bowl — pick one, seated versions exist for both.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/7.
- **Note:** Fireworks/G-STAR mid-Nov windows — swap if official 2026 dates land here.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Full rest

- **All day:** CSAT day — deliberately off. Beach sit, bath, fish-cake (Samjin) souvenir.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 7/7.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50). Check in Ibis Styles Myeongdong — daytime arrival, 15:00.
- **Afternoon:** Namdaemun slow sweep; herbal-tea buy.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon + Kimjang Festival at aT Center (Nov 20–22).
- **Stay:** Seoul — Ibis Styles Myeongdong (night 7 of 8 total).

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Last tea

- **Morning:** Jogyesa tea or Bukchon tea house repeat; weigh bags.
- **Afternoon:** Final ceramics.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner near Seoul Station; two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8 of 8.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:15; taxi to Seoul Station → **AREX Express → ICN** (~43 min, depart ~09:00, arrive ~09:45) — 3h+ before 13:00 flight.
- **Afternoon:** Fly home 13:00.
