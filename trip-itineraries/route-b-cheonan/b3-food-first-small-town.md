## Who this suits

Eaters who like small-town specialties as much as big-city markets. Cheonan is a secret food weapon: sundae-guk lore (Byeongcheon sundae town nearby), hodu-gwaja walnut pastries baked since 1934, and a lived-in old market — all cheaper and slower than Seoul.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**; hotel arrival realistically **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): GLAD Mapo** — big, late-arrival-friendly full-service hotel on the AREX line (Gongdeok/Mapo edge). **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email your flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Hotel Manu Seoul · Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 3 PM check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop (last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70,000–100,000.
- **Late supper:** midnight gukbap joints cluster around Gongdeok/Mapo — an ideal food-first first meal.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → 24-hour check-in (~23:45 target) → midnight gukbap (24-hour joints near Gongdeok).
- **Stay:** Seoul — GLAD Mapo (night 1/7 — arrival night; the only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop (5-minute taxi / one subway stop) to **Ibis Ambassador Insadong** in Insadong / Jongno — drop the bags, sightseeing runs as written, check in from 15:00.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Market lunch, soft start
- **First move:** check out of the arrival hotel (~10:00); 5-minute taxi or one subway stop to **Ibis Ambassador Insadong** — bags stored at the desk until 15:00 check-in, then the day below runs as planned.

- **Late morning:** Sleep in; Mapo coffee.
- **Afternoon:** **Gwangjang Market** long lunch (bindaetteok, yukhoe, mayak gimbap).
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon walk; early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 2/7.

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Royal + dosirak

- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung + Guard Changing Ceremony.
- **Afternoon:** **Tongin Market coin lunchbox**; Samcheong tea house.
- **Evening:** Insadong hanjeongsik (traditional set menu) dinner — reserve.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/7.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Old alleys feed

- **Morning:** Ikseon-dong cafes.
- **Afternoon:** Euljiro old-school lunch alleys (kalguksu, bindaetteok in print-shop backstreets).
- **Evening:** Myeongdong street-food crawl + chicken & beer.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/7.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Mangwon day

- **Morning:** **Mangwon Market** grazing breakfast-lunch.
- **Afternoon:** Hongdae dessert cafes; Hapjeong record shops.
- **Evening:** Hongdae Korean BBQ; coin noraebang.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/7.
 
### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Kimchi & tower

- **Morning:** Museum Kimchikan kimchi class (pre-book English session).
- **Afternoon:** Bukchon viewpoints; tea.
- **Evening:** N Seoul Tower dusk; Namsan hanok-village-area dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/7.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Noryangjin & the river

- **Morning:** **Noryangjin Fish Market** buy-and-eat sashimi brunch.
- **Afternoon:** The Hyundai Seoul food halls + Han River park.
- **Evening:** Event slot (My Chemical Romance Nov 7 / Korean Series watch party) or riverside chimaek picnic.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/7.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul → Cheonan (transfer)

- **Morning:** Mugunghwa Seoul Station → Cheonan (~55–60 min); grab station-bakery snacks for the ride; check in by Cheonan Station.
- **Afternoon:** **Cheonan Jungang Market** first pass — lunch grazing through the arcade aisles; buy hodu-gwaja warm off the press.
- **Evening:** Sundae-guk alley dinner near the station.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — Brown Dot Cheonan Station (night 1/5).

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Cheonan · Hodu-gwaja day

- **Morning:** **Hakwha Hodugwaja** original bakery — walnut pastries since 1934, buy mixed boxes (they travel well; some for the suitcase, some for now).
- **Afternoon:** Independence Hall of Korea (free; even a food itinerary deserves the area's big sight).
- **Evening:** Bulgogi or grilled-meat alley downtown.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 2/5.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Byeongcheon sundae town

- **Full day:** **Byeongcheon Sundae Town** (Cheonan's Byeongcheon-myeon, ~30–40 min by bus/taxi) — the country's most famous blood-sausage-soup quarter: whole restaurants serving sundae-guk from generations-old recipes across the market street. Lunch is the point of the day.
- **Evening:** Return to Cheonan city; light dessert cafe; early night.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 3/5.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Onyang soup & soak

- **Morning:** Line 1 to Onyang Oncheon station — classic soup-alley breakfast near the station.
- **Afternoon:** Asan Spavis or a traditional bathhouse soak; Onyang Folk Museum if preferred.
- **Evening:** Back in Cheonan; downtown cafe-street coffee and dessert.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 4/5.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Cheonan · Slow food prep

- **Morning:** Market breakfast; picnic-supply shopping (rice cakes, fruit, hodu-gwaja boxes for the train).
- **Afternoon:** Gakwonsa temple & giant Buddha approach walk (works up an appetite).
- **Evening:** Pack; quiet noodle dinner.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 5/5.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Cheonan → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** Cheonan-Asan KTX → Busan (~2 hr); grab station kimbap for the ride; ASTI check-in.
- **Afternoon:** **Jagalchi Fish Market** late lunch (grilled eel street after).
- **Evening:** BIFF Square seed-hotteok + Gukje market lanes.
- **Stay:** Busan — ASTI (night 1/7).

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Nampo food loop

- **Morning:** Gukje + Bupyeong day markets.
- **Afternoon:** Gamcheon Culture Village + viewpoint coffee.
- **Evening:** **Bupyeong Kkangtong night market** dinner crawl; Busan Tower night view.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/7.
- **Note:** If the Busan Fireworks Festival lands this Saturday at Gwangalli (usual early/mid-Nov slot — verify), tonight pivots to the bridge show + Millak hoe-center dinner.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Haeundae coast eats

- **Morning:** Blueline Sky Capsule; Cheongsapo **grilled clam** lunch (the town's specialty).
- **Afternoon:** Haeundae market-street snacks; beach walk.

- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/7.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Soup alleys & milmyeon

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa early (sea air builds appetite).
- **Afternoon:** Seomyeon **dwaeji-gukbap alley** + a **milmyeon** (wheat noodle) shop — Busan's two default lunches, both today.
- **Evening:** Jeonpo cafe desserts; snack-souvenir run.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/7.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Cable car & eomuk

- **Morning:** Songdo Marine Cable Car round trip.
- **Afternoon:** Nampo fish-cake crawl — Samjin eomuk history hall, Goraesa flagship; fish-cake DIY class if slots allow (check booking windows).
- **Evening:** Pojangmacha tent-bar area dinner (verify current cluster locations) or Seomyeon izakaya row.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Taejongdae & stew
