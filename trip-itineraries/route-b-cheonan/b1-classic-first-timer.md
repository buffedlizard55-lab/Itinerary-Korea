## Who this suits

The balanced greatest-hits plan with the calmer middle city. Cheonan trades Daejeon's scale for shorter rail hops, the country's flagship Independence Hall, walnut pastries, and access to the Onyang hot-spring strip — without giving up the clean Seoul–Busan arc.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**; expect the hotel between **23:00 and 01:00** — later if delayed.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Hotel Manu Seoul** — between Seoul Station and City Hall, easy walk or 2-minute taxi from the AREX terminus, accustomed to past-midnight arrivals. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email your flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
- **Late transit, in order of preference:** (1) AREX all-stop ICN → Seoul Station (~60 min; last services ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi/Kakao T ~60–75 min, ₩70,000–100,000. Save the hotel's Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** 24-hour convenience store or Seoul Station-area gukbap.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX or taxi → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30–00:30) → bed.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Hotel Manu (night 1/7).
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001 night bus; heavy delay → taxi. The 24-hour desk is exactly why this hotel was chosen.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Palace core, gently

- **Morning:** Late start (jet lag); Cheonggyecheon Stream → Gwanghwamun Square.
- **Afternoon:** Gyeongbokgung + Royal Guard Changing Ceremony (usually 10:00/14:00 — verify; hanbok rental = free entry).
- **Evening:** Insadong dinner; early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 2/7.

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Hanok & garden

- **Morning:** Bukchon Hanok Village viewpoints (before 10:00 for quiet).
- **Afternoon:** Changdeokgung + Huwon Secret Garden tour (pre-book; fall moonlight tours run to ~Nov 8).
- **Evening:** Ikseon-dong alley dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/7.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Markets & tower

- **Morning:** Gwangjang Market brunch; Kimjang Festival (Nov 1–3) overflow events if any linger.
- **Afternoon:** DDP design plaza → Myeongdong sweep.
- **Evening:** N Seoul Tower dusk by cable car; Seoul Plaza free concert if scheduled (Nov 4–18).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/7.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · North loop

- **Morning:** Seoul City Wall walk (Naksan easy section) or Bugaksan (ID checkpoint — verify access).
- **Afternoon:** Namsangol Hanok Village; War Memorial of Korea if you'd rather be indoors.
- **Evening:** Hongdae evening walk.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/7.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · River & Seongsu

- **Morning:** COEX Starfield Library + Gangnam lunch.
- **Afternoon:** Seoul Forest autumn color → Seongsu cafes/pop-ups.
- **Evening:** Han River evening (cruise option) or Banpo rainbow-light park.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/7.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Free day / big event slot

- **Morning:** Tongin Market coin-lunchbox brunch.
- **Afternoon:** Suwon Hwaseong Fortress half-day (ITX ~35 min) or DMZ tour (book far ahead).
- **Evening:** Big-ticket slot: My Chemical Romance (Nov 7), KGMA (Nov 7–8), or a Korean Series watch party (through ~Nov 8).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/7.
- **Plan B:** Low-energy → this becomes laundry + souvenir day.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul → Cheonan (transfer)

- **Morning:** Check out. Take the easy ride: Korail Mugunghwa Seoul Station → Cheonan (~55–60 min) or Subway Line 1 all the way (~1 hr 20, T-money, no booking).
- **Afternoon:** Check in; **Independence Hall of Korea** first pass (free entry; sprawling campus — allow half a day; closes ~17:00–18:00 seasonally, so go straight out if you want it today, else push to Day 9).
- **Evening:** Shinsegae-belt dinner; Cheonan Jungang Market snack preview.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — Shilla Stay Cheonan (night 1/5).

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Cheonan · Independence Hall day

- **Full day:** Independence Hall of Korea properly — seven exhibition halls tracing Korea's independence movement; the grounds and Grand Hall of the Nation are the centerpiece of the Cheonan leg. Weekday = school groups taper by early afternoon.
- **Evening:** Hodu-gwaja walnut-pastry bakery stop (Cheonan's signature — Hakwha is the classic original brand) + quiet dinner.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 2/5.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Cheonan · Buddha & gallery

- **Morning:** Gakwonsa Temple + giant bronze Buddha on the foothills (bus/taxi; good maple color).
- **Afternoon:** Arario Gallery + the small-city sculpture walk downtown — keep today compact and walkable.
- **Evening:** Local pocha dinner; espresso at a roastery cafe.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 3/5.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Onyang/Asan hot-spring day

- **Full day:** Line 1 north to Onyang Oncheon station area: **Onyang Hot Spring district + Onyang Folk Museum**, or the big modern **Asan Spavis** water-spa complex (swimwear). Classic onsen-hopping logic, Korean style.
- **Evening:** Back to Cheonan; sundae-guk (blood-sausage soup) dinner — this region's dish.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 4/5.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Cheonan · Small-town rhythm

- **Morning:** Jungang Market breakfast grazing; market-street arcade antique quest.
- **Afternoon:** Cafe at a hanok-ish old house conversion; laundry; pack.
- **Evening:** Early night before the longest transfer.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 5/5.
- **Sports option:** Cheonan City FC or Hyundai Capital Skywalkers volleyball if a home fixture lands.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Cheonan → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** Taxi/temp bus to **Cheonan-Asan KTX station** → KTX to Busan (~2 hr). Subway Line 1 connects Cheonan ↔ Cheonan-Asan directly.
- **Afternoon:** Check in Haeundae; beach + Dongbaek loop.
- **Evening:** Haeundae night market street.
- **Stay:** Busan — L7 HAEUNDAE (night 1/7).

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Sky capsule coast

- **Morning:** Blueline Park Sky Capsule Mipo → Cheongsapo; Daritdol skywalk.
- **Afternoon:** Cheongsapo cafes; boardwalk return.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli — Saturday M Drone Light Show (verify schedule); bridge lights.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/7.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Sea temple

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa early.
- **Afternoon:** Spa Land Centum City session or Shinsegae browsing.
- **Evening:** Centum food-hall dinner.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/7.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Old town west

- **Morning:** Gamcheon Culture Village early.
- **Afternoon:** Jagalchi Fish Market lunch → BIFF Square + Gukje Market.
- **Evening:** Bupyeong Kkangtong night market; Busan Tower night view (Yongdusan).
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/7.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Cliffs & cable cars

- **Morning:** Taejongdae Danubi loop to the lighthouse cliffs.
- **Afternoon:** Songdo Marine Cable Car round trip.
- **Evening:** Seomyeon + Jeonpo cafe street.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/7.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Flex day

- **Morning:** Beomeosa temple mountain air (Line 1).
- **Afternoon:** Oryukdo Skywalk + Igidae segment, or F1963 if raining.
- **Evening:** Sports night options (KCC Egis basketball, V-League) — check fixtures.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/7.
- **Note:** Busan Fireworks Festival (usually early/mid-Nov Saturday at Gwangalli) and G-STAR 2026 (BEXCO, usually mid-Nov) — reshuffle evenings if official 2026 dates land in this leg.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Repeat-favorites

- **Morning:** Favorite coast/makeup stop.
- **Afternoon:** Fish-cake and dried-seafood souvenir run (vacuum packs travel well).
- **Evening:** Farewell-to-the-coast dinner.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 7/7.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50); check in Hotel Manu.
- **Afternoon:** Namdaemun Market souvenir pass.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon night walk; Korea Kimjang Grand Festival at aT Center runs Nov 20–22 nearby.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8 of 9 total.

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Last full day

- **Morning:** Haneul Park silver grass (tail season) or a favorite-neighborhood repeat.
- **Afternoon:** Final shopping; pack; weigh bags.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner near Seoul Station; two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 9 of 9.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:30; **AREX Express ~09:00 → ICN ~09:45** (3h+ buffer). City Airport Terminal early check-in exists for select airlines only — verify.
- **Afternoon:** Fly 13:00.
