## Who this suits

Families with kids ~4–12 who want the gentlest city moves. Seoul does aquariums and palaces in hanbok; Cheonan does the free Independence Hall as a picnic day and Onyang warm soaks; Busan does beach trains and sea cliffs by bus-train, not hikes.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. With kids, expect **23:00–01:00+** to hotel.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): GLAD Mapo** — AREX-line (Gongdeok/Mapo), relaxed about late families. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number + kids.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points Seoul Station · Hotel Manu Seoul · Courtyard Namdaemun.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Gongdeok/Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k with seat request. Save Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** CU/GS25 milk + kimbap; nothing timed tonight.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → bags → AREX all-stop → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:45). Juice + bed.
- **Stay:** Seoul — GLAD Mapo (night 1/7 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to **Nine Tree Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, gentle start.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Palace in hanbok
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to Nine Tree Myeongdong.

- **Late morning:** Cheonggyecheon flat stroll.
- **Afternoon:** Gyeongbokgung — rent hanbok (free entry) + Guard Changing Ceremony (10:00/14:00 verify) — the show kids remember.
- **Evening:** Insadong early dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Nine Tree Myeongdong (night 2/7).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Lotte World

- **Full day:** Lotte World Adventure (indoor, strollers) + Folk Museum inside. Lockers for coats; weekday = shorter lines.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/7.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Aquarium & forest

- **Morning:** COEX Aquarium + Starfield Library stairs.
- **Afternoon:** Seoul Forest — deer + leaf piles + playground.
- **Evening:** Myeongdong night-market eye-spy.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/7.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Wall treasure walk

- **Morning:** Namsangol Hanok Village (compact) + Naksan wall mural hunt (easy segment).
- **Afternoon:** DDP lawn run-around.
- **Evening:** Hongdae dance spot.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/7.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Museum choose-one

- **Afternoon:** Seongsu cafe/hot-chocolate + Han River kits.
- **Evening:** Laundry/pack-light night.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Coin lunchbox

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul → Cheonan (transfer)

- **Late morning:** Easy ride: Mugunghwa Seoul → Cheonan (~55–60 min) or Line 1 (~1 hr 20, T-money, no booking) or KTX to Cheonan-Asan (~35–40 min). No seat stress.

- **Evening:** Quiet Shinsegae-belt dinner.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — Shilla Stay Cheonan (night 1/5).

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Cheonan · Picnic monument

- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 2/5.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Cheonan · Buddha hill easy
