## Who this suits

Return visitors who want a new Seoul. Palaces get one respectful half-day total, not five. The rest is neighborhoods locals actually moved to in the last five years — plus the shortest Cheonan leg so Busan gets 8 full nights for islands, vinyl bars, and late streets.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Expect hotel **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): GLAD Mapo** — AREX-line, easy Hongdae/Seongsu hop, late-night tolerant. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points Seoul Station · Hotel Manu Seoul · Courtyard Namdaemun.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Gongdeok/Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** CU/GS25 + hotel bar close-down — save the eating tour for tomorrow.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:45). No neighborhood tonight — sleep debt matters.
- **Stay:** Seoul — GLAD Mapo (night 1/8 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to **L7 Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then new Seoul.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Euljiro after dark (start slow)
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to L7 Myeongdong.

- **Late morning:** Sleep in (jet lag). Afternoon: Euljiro printing alleys → Bangsan tool lanes → neon cafe where you’d never expect one.
- **Evening:** Jongno makgeolli lane micro-brew + tiny plates.
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 2/8).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Mullae steel village

- **Morning:** Mullae-dong (Line 2 Mullae) — steel workshops by day, murals between them; outdoor welders then gallery doors.
- **Afternoon:** Mullae cafe row + Dangsan park edge.
- **Evening:** Hongdae vinyl-bar row (Modern Vinyl + Gopchang alley light).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/8.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Seongsu warehouses

- **Full day:** Seongsu-dong — factory conversions, flagship roasteries, November pop-up houses (free to enter 90%). Seoul Forest mid-break for air.
- **Evening:** Seongsu brewery dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/8.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Mangwon market lunch

- **Afternoon:** Mangwon Market (not Gwangjang) — the local’s market: perilla-oil makguksu, fried camellia-flower buys, market sake. **Less English, more regulars.**
- **Evening:** Mangwon riverside + Mapo backstreets → Hongdae late walk optional.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/8.
- **Note:** Gwangjang is intentionally skipped — you’ve done it last trip.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · One-palace courtesy

- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung only outer courts + Guard Ceremony quick view (10:00 or 14:00 verify) — the one traditional beat, done quickly.
- **Afternoon:** Seochon alleys + Sejong bookstore row; Tongin lunchbox if you skipped Mangwon.
- **Evening:** Ikseon-dong light dessert bar — new behind old facades.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/8.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Record & stage

- **Afternoon:** Itaewon record shops + Hannam hill walk.
- **Evening:** Big-ticket slot: My Chemical Romance (Nov 7) or KGMA (Nov 7–8) — your returners’ live night; if nothing grabs you, coin noraebang proper.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/8.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul · A last new block

- **All day:** Yeonnam-dong Gyeongui Line Forest + Yeonhui-dong bakery hop — the block most first-timers miss.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8/8.

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Seoul → Cheonan (transfer) — short leg on purpose

- **Late morning:** Mugunghwa Seoul → Cheonan (~55–60 min) or Line 1 (~1 hr 20). No booking stress.
- **Afternoon:** Check in Brown Dot; quick Shinsegae-belt walk; Jungang Market preview.
- **Evening:** Hodu-gwaja warm box — Hakwha original press-mold line is a good second-visit watch.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — Brown Dot Cheonan (night 1/3).

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Cheonan · Independence Hall + sundae

- **Morning:** Independence Hall of Korea (free) — Grand Hall as architecture, then one hall deep (not all seven — you’ve earned choosing).
- **Afternoon:** Byeongcheon sundae-soup town (bus/taxi ~20–30 min) — lunch-focused so arrive before 12:30; back to Cheonan for Arario night if energy.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 2/3.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Cheonan · Bath town cameo

- **Morning:** Onyang Hot Spring district quick soak (Line 1) — just the free foot baths if you’d rather save spa money for Busan’s Spa Land.
- **Afternoon:** Return, pack for Busan; Pepero Day stalls.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 3/3.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Cheonan → Busan (transfer) — 8-night Busan starts

- **Morning:** KTX Cheonan-Asan → Busan (~2 hr). Confirm hop to Cheonan-Asan.
- **Afternoon:** Haeundae beach + Dongbaek Island flat.
- **Evening:** Haeundae night street.
- **Stay:** Busan — Ramada Encore Haeundae (night 1/8).

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Busan · Jeonpo + Seomyeon night

- **Full day:** Jeonpo Cafe Street by day (Busan’s cafe-density peak) → Seomyeon by night — neon, underground mall, late food. This is Busan’s new-city beat, distinct from Seoul’s.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/8.

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Blueline coast

- **Morning:** Blueline Sky Capsule Mipo → Cheongsapo + Daritdol skywalk.
- **Afternoon:** Cheongsapo clam grill / cafe balcony; beach train back.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli drone show (Sat verify) — Gwangan Bridge lit.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/8.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Islands

- **Morning:** Taejongdae Danubi train to lighthouse cliffs + pine-framed sea.
- **Afternoon:** Songdo Marine Cable Car + Songdo Cloud Walkway.
- **Evening:** Yeongdo Huinnyeoul Culture Village — cliff atelier houses at dusk.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/8.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Port roots

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa **early** (~07:30) before buses.
- **Afternoon:** Jagalchi knife-work downstairs, eat upstairs + BIFF Square hand-prints.
- **Evening:** Nampo Gukje Market lanes + Bupyeong Kkangtong night-market lights.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/8.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan ·gamcheon + factory

- **Morning:** Gamcheon Culture Village early (before 09:30) — pastel layer.
- **Afternoon:** F1963 eco-complex — wire factory → cultural site.
- **Evening:** Jeonpo re-do for the shop you missed.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/8.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Buffer coast

- **All day:** Oryukdo Skywalk + Igidae partial *or* UN Memorial Cemetery quiet — your choice. Optional KBL/V-League night.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 7/8.
- **Note:** Fireworks/G-STAR mid-Nov windows — swap if official 2026 dates land here.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Full rest

- **All day:** CSAT day — deliberately off. Revisit beach, fish-cake shop (Samjin) re-stock.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 8/8.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50). Check in L7 Myeongdong — daytime arrival, 15:00.
- **Afternoon:** Last new-block repeat: Mullae or Seongsu for the missed pop-up.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon + Kimjang Festival at aT Center (Nov 20–22).
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 9 of 10 total).

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Last crates

- **Morning:** Budget vinyl + stationery hunt; weigh bags.
- **Afternoon:** Pack — done by 16:00, then one more makgeolli.
- **Evening:** Farewell near Seoul Station; two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 10 of 10.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:15; taxi to Seoul Station → **AREX Express → ICN** (~43 min, depart ~09:00, arrive ~09:45) — 3h+ before 13:00 flight.
- **Afternoon:** Fly home 13:00.
