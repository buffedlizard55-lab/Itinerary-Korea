## Who this suits

Travelers who read every museum placard. Seoul's palaces get full depth, Cheonan exists in this itinerary *because of* the Independence Hall, and Busan carries the 20th-century layer (UN Memorial Cemetery, wartime capital remnants) plus its seaside temples.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**; hotel arrival realistically **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station** — at the AREX terminus, so even a 1 AM arrival is simple. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email your flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Hotel Manu Seoul · GLAD Mapo · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 3 PM check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70,000–100,000. Korean address saved offline.
- **Late supper:** Seoul Station 24-hour options; no plans beyond bed.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (~23:30 target) → bed.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Four Points (night 1/8 — arrival night; the only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop (5-minute taxi / one subway stop) to **Ibis Ambassador Insadong** in Insadong — drop the bags, sightseeing runs as written, check in from 15:00.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Founding palaces
- **First move:** check out of the arrival hotel (~10:00); 5-minute taxi or one subway stop to **Ibis Ambassador Insadong** — bags stored at the desk until 15:00 check-in, then the day below runs as planned.

- **Late morning:** Gyeongbokgung + Royal Guard Changing Ceremony + National Palace Museum.
- **Afternoon:** National Folk Museum (on the grounds).
- **Evening:** Seochon quiet-lane dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 2/8.

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Changdeokgung & Jongmyo

- **Morning:** Changdeokgung full tour incl. Huwon Secret Garden (pre-book; moonlight finale ~Nov 8).
- **Afternoon:** Jongmyo Shrine (UNESCO royal-ancestral rites site; guided slots — check days/times).
- **Evening:** Ikseon-dong dinner; early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/8.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · War & memory

- **Morning:** War Memorial of Korea (big; budget 3+ hours).
- **Afternoon:** Leeum Museum of Art (permanent traditional collection is superb).
- **Evening:** Itaewon/Hannam dinner; Seoul Plaza free concert if one fits (Nov 4–18).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/8.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Bukchon & crafts

- **Morning:** Bukchon Hanok Village + Seoul Museum of Craft Art (SeMoCA).
- **Afternoon:** Donuimun Museum Village / Independence Gate (Seodaemun) + Seodaemun Prison History Hall — sobering, essential.
- **Evening:** Myeongdong light pass.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/8.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Deoksugung & the city wall

- **Morning:** Deoksugung (unique East-meets-West palace mix) + Jeongdong historic quarter.
- **Afternoon:** Seoul City Wall (Sungnyemun→Inwang? use the Naksan or Bugaksan segment; Bugaksan needs ID).
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon night walk.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/8.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · DMZ or fortress day

- **Full day (choice):** DMZ/JSA tour (book early; passports required) **or** Suwon Hwaseong Fortress (UNESCO walls, ITX ~35 min).
- **Evening:** Rest; Korean Series watch party if the season finale is on (runs to ~Nov 8).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/8.
- **Note:** DMZ closures happen without notice — Hwaseong is the guaranteed fallback.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul · National Museum + packing

- **Morning:** National Museum of Korea (free; the Silla gold crowns).
- **Afternoon:** Laundry, pack for the move south.
- **Evening:** Early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8/8.

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Seoul → Cheonan (transfer)

- **Morning:** Mugunghwa Seoul Station → Cheonan (~55–60 min) or Line 1 all-stop (~1 hr 20); check in.
- **Afternoon:** **Yu Gwan-sun Memorial** (Byeongcheon, Cheonan — birth home & memorial of the 1919 March 1st heroine) — pairs perfectly with tomorrow.
- **Evening:** Cheonan Jungang Market area dinner.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — Ramada Encore (night 1/5).

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Cheonan · Independence Hall, the core day

- **Full day:** **Independence Hall of Korea** — free admission, seven vast halls of colonial-era and independence-movement history; the Grand Hall of the Nation with its tiled roofline is the national-scale "we are here" monument. Give it a full day without guilt.
- **Evening:** Sundae-guk dinner (regional specialty) + hodu-gwaja dessert.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 2/5.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Cheonan · Temple Buddha & city art

- **Morning:** Gakwonsa Temple + the 15 m bronze seated Buddha; maple-lined approach.
- **Afternoon:** Arario Gallery + downtown sculpture walk; Cheonan Samgeori Park brief pause.
- **Evening:** Local pocha dinner.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 3/5.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Onyang heritage & hot spring

- **Full day:** Onyang area (Line 1): **Onyang Folk Museum** (the old provincial hotel-turned-museum tradition—museum tells the onsen-town story) + a soak in the historic Onyang hot-spring district (public baths or a hotel day-use jjimjil).
- **Evening:** Quiet dinner; street of night-lit motels/onsen inns is its own retro scene.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 4/5.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Cheonan · Slow day & pack

- **Morning:** Jungang Market breakfast; walnut-pastry stock-up for gifts.
- **Afternoon:** Cafe reading; pack for Busan.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 5/5.

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Cheonan → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** Line 1 to Cheonan-Asan → KTX to Busan (~2 hr); ASTI check-in.
- **Afternoon:** Busan modern-history intro: the 40-Step Stairway and Jungang-dong wartime-capital blocks (the evacuee-era alleys when Busan was the nation's temporary capital).
- **Evening:** BIFF Square + Gukje market night edge.
- **Stay:** Busan — ASTI Hotel Busan Station (night 1/6).

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · UN Memorial & coastal temples

- **Morning:** UN Memorial Cemetery (the only UN cemetery in the world; quiet, immaculately kept).
- **Afternoon:** Busan Museum (next door) for Gaya/Busan archaeology.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli bridge night view.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/6.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Beomeosa temple mountain

- **Full morning:** Beomeosa (Line 1 north, founded year 678) — temple-stay teas, maple-valley gate walk.
- **Afternoon:** Geumjeong Fortress lower gate / fortress-wall segment if legs allow.
- **Evening:** Seomyeon dinner.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/6.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Old port & Gamcheon

- **Morning:** Gamcheon Culture Village (post-war refugee hillside turned art maze).
- **Afternoon:** Jagalchi market lunch, then the Jagalchi-to-BIFF colonial-port blocks walk.
- **Evening:** Yongdusan Park + Busan Tower; Bupyeong night market.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/6.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Sea temples loop

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa (seaside temple, 1376 origin) early.
- **Afternoon:** Cheongsapo & the Blueline coast OR Gyeongju day-trip option (KTX 35 min to Singyeongju — Bulguksa/Seokguram world-heritage temples; this itinerary supports that swap with an early start).
- **Evening:** Farewell-feast seafood.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/6.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Buffer & make-up

- **All day:** Missed-museum/makeup day (Busan Modern History Museum? keep names simple: Busan Modern History Museum block, Provisional Capital Memorial if open), plus souvenir logistics.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/6.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50); Ibis Insadong check-in.
- **Afternoon:** Seodaemun? already done — Namdaemun Market souvenirs instead.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon; Kimjang Grand Festival (aT Center, Nov 20–22) option.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 9 of 10 total.

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Final full day

- **Morning:** Bukchon or a palace re-visit in different light; Seoul City History Museum if weather is poor.
- **Afternoon:** Pack; weigh bags; print/confirm anything needed.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner near Seoul Station; two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 10 of 10.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:15; 5-minute taxi (or Myeongdong airport-limousine bus) to Seoul Station, then AREX Express ~09:00 → ICN ~09:45.
- **Afternoon:** Fly 13:00.
