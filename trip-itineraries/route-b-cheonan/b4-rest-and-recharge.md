## Who this suits

Anyone treating this trip as actual recovery. Cheonan is the best hot-spring gateway of the two middle cities (the historic Onyang oncheon district and Asan's modern spa resorts are a short Line 1 ride away), so this plan soaks there, adds Busan's Spa Land Centum, and keeps sightseeing to one calming anchor a day.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**; hotel arrival realistically **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun** — full-service desk, 2-minute taxi from the AREX terminus; late arrivals are routine here. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email your flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop (last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70,000–100,000 — this itinerary's default is the taxi: spa trip energy starts tonight.
- **Late supper:** hotel lounge or convenience store. Bed is the program.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → taxi or AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:45) → bed.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Courtyard Namdaemun (night 1/7).

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Nothing before noon

- **Late morning:** Wake naturally; brunch near City Hall.
- **Afternoon:** Deoksugung Stonewall Walkway + Jeongdong cafe window.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon short stroll; early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 2/7.

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · One palace, one tea house

- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung grounds, seated touring style.
- **Afternoon:** Seochon hanok tea house long break.
- **Evening:** Insadong early dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/7.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Jjimjilbang afternoon

- **Morning:** Free morning (sleep, read, hotel gym).
- **Afternoon:** Classic Seoul jjimjilbang session (Dragon Hill Spa Yongsan is the famous full-size one — verify current hours/status; alternates: itaewon spas, hotel spas).
- **Evening:** Post-sauna ramen/snack hall; lights out early.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/7.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Park air

- **Morning:** Seoul Forest flat loop + deer corral.
- **Afternoon:** Seongsu large-format cafe with armchairs (recovery seating matters).
- **Evening:** Optional seated Han River cruise at golden hour.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/7.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Museum + massage

- **Morning:** National Museum of Korea (free, generous seating).
- **Afternoon:** Foot-massage hour (Myeongdong/City Hall areas; walk-ins common).
- **Evening:** Light dinner; Seoul Plaza free concert if one drifts by (Nov 4–18).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/7.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Buffer day

- **All day:** Unscheduled. Laundry, favorite cafe, nap. Pack for Cheonan.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/7.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul → Cheonan (gentle transfer)

- **Late morning:** Mugunghwa Seoul → Cheonan (~55–60 min) or Line 1 (~1 hr 20); check in Sono Belle.
- **Afternoon:** Resort rest day — on-site pool/sauna facilities if available (verify what's included).
- **Evening:** Quiet resort-area dinner.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — Sono Belle Cheonan (night 1/6).

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Cheonan · Independence Hall, calmly

- **Late morning:** Independence Hall of Korea (free) — the one big cultural anchor of the Cheonan stay; take the free campus at whatever pace feels good.
- **Afternoon:** Hakwha hodu-gwaja bakery on the way back; hotel rest.
- **Evening:** Simple dinner; soak if the room/resort allows.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 2/6.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Onyang oncheon day

- **Full day:** Historic **Onyang Hot Spring district** (Line 1): the old onsen hotels offer day-bathing; lunch at a soup alley; Onyang Folk Museum for the town's 600-year bathing story.
- **Evening:** Back to Cheonan; warmed-through early night.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 3/6.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Asan Spavis day

- **Full day:** **Asan Spavis** (big swim-spa complex near Onyang; swimwear required in main pools — pack one) — outdoor hot pools in cool November air is the whole point of this itinerary.
- **Evening:** Dinner near Asan; return.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 4/6.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Cheonan · Temple & tea

- **Morning:** Gakwonsa temple + giant Buddha approach (flat, contemplative).
- **Afternoon:** Downtown cafe; light gallery stop (Arario) if it pleases.
- **Evening:** Sundae-guk comfort dinner; pack.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 5/6.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Cheonan · Free soak/buffer

- **All day:** Unscheduled — sleep, on-site spa, or a second Onyang round. Nothing is owed today.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 6/6.

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Cheonan → Busan (transfer to the sea)

- **Morning:** Line 1 to Cheonan-Asan → KTX to Busan (~2 hr); taxi to Haeundae.
- **Afternoon:** Grand Josun check-in; beach chairs and the boardwalk at whatever distance feels good.
- **Evening:** Room-view sunset; nearby seafood dinner.
- **Stay:** Busan — Grand Josun (night 1/6).

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Spa Land marathon

- **Full day:** **Spa Land Centum City** — 20+ themed baths and saunas inside Shinsegae Centum; a whole seated/soaked day with cafe breaks inside.
- **Evening:** Centum food-hall dinner; fall asleep early and happy.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/6.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Gentle coast

- **Morning:** Haeundae beach + Dongbaek island flat loop.
- **Afternoon:** Blueline Park sky capsule slow ride (seated views, minimal steps) to Cheongsapo for coffee and clams.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli night view from a seated cafe terrace.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/6.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Sea temple morning

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa early (waves, then withdraw before crowds).
- **Afternoon:** Hotel pool/spa or a F1963 bookstore-cafe afternoon.
- **Evening:** Quiet dinner; maybe the hotel bar.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/6.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Port side, seated versions

- **Morning:** Songdo Marine Cable Car round trip (floating armchair over the sea).
- **Afternoon:** Jagalchi lunch (upstairs tables, no queuing); skip the stair-heavy sights — taxi to a Nampo coffee with a harbor view.
- **Evening:** Bupyeong Kkangtong short pass; early taxi home.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/6.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Full buffer

- **All day:** Unscheduled. Beach, book, bath. Pack calmly for Seoul.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/6.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (gentle transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50); Courtyard check-in.
- **Afternoon:** Nap + short Myeongdong pass.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon evening walk, last-lantern energy.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7 of 9 total.

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Pack & polish

- **Morning:** Small souvenir loop (gim, tea, bath salts/soap gifts — on-theme).
- **Afternoon:** Final jjimjilbang hour or hotel spa; pack; weigh bags.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner near Seoul Station; two alarms; AREX time confirmed.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 9 of 9.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:30; AREX Express ~09:00 → ICN ~09:45 (3h+ buffer).
- **Afternoon:** Fly 13:00, rested.

## Booking checklist (in order)

1. Flights.
2. **Night 1 hotel (Courtyard Namdaemun) — 24-hour desk confirmed + flight number emailed.**
3. Grand Josun Busan early (premium inventory thins fast for November weekends).
4. Sono Belle Cheonan (resort weekends book up) + final Seoul nights.
5. KTX ×2 long segments (~1 month out).
6. Spa reservations: Dragon Hill status check, Spavis ticket window, Spa Land hours/age rules.

## If plans slip

- **Flight delay:** taxi + 24-hour desk; Day 2 starts at noon by design.
- **Rain all week:** honestly fine — this itinerary is 60% indoor by default (spas, museums, cafes, department stores).
- **Too-tired days:** buffer days (7, 13, 19) absorb anything; never cut the spa anchors, cut the sights around them.
