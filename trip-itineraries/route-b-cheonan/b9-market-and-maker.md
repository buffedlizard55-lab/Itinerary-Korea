## Who this suits

People who like to watch hands work — printers, welders, potters, fishmongers, needle workers. You’ll bargain less and observe more. Market mornings, workshop afternoons, and a souvenir bag that’s actually made by the person you met.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Expect hotel **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station** — AREX terminus, full-service desk, no midnight stress. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** CU/GS25 tonight — markets open in the morning.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30).
- **Stay:** Seoul — Four Points Seoul Station (night 1/7 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to **L7 Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then metal and paper.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Euljiro metal morning
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to L7 Myeongdong.

- **Morning:** Euljiro Bangsan printing & tool alleys — metal shops, neon-bending, stamping. Watch-first, buy-second.
- **Afternoon:** Cheonggyecheon rest + Ikseon-dong hanok cafe (tiles made by hands you just saw).
- **Evening:** Jongno makgeolli lane — the makers’ dinner mile.
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 2/7).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Mullae steel village

- **Morning:** Mullae-dong steel village (Line 2 Mullae) — outdoor welders + mural lanes; ask before shooting.
- **Afternoon:** Seongsu leather & sneaker maker row + pop-up window.
- **Evening:** Seoul Forest meadow break; Seongsu dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/7.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Paper & cloth

- **Morning:** Gwangjang Market textile mezzanine — sewing rows, fabric bolts, button trays.
- **Afternoon:** Dongdaemun fabric buildings DDM/BangSan — find the four-floor fabric towers + DDP exterior ramps.
- **Evening:** N Seoul Tower dusk if feet allow; otherwise Myeongdong supply run.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/7.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Ceramic hands

- **Morning:** National Museum of Korea craft wing or Amorepacific ceramics if you want hands-on.
- **Afternoon:** Insadong — pottery wheel demos + Ssamziegil maker stalls (not chain souvenirs).
- **Evening:** Buckchon tea house sit; off early.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/7.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Tongin lunch you made

- **Morning:** Tongin Market dosirak — you load the brass tray yourself (the act is the souvenir).
- **Afternoon:** Sewoon Sangga electronics salvage + used-tool floors — maker-bric-a-brac.
- **Evening:** Hongdae small-club listening night (instrument shops daytime, band night).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/7.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Free / big event

- **Afternoon:** Free market re-run or DMZ maker contrast (the DMZ line’s tool-grid and barbed craft is its own making) — must book DMZ far ahead. Evening My Chemical Romance (Nov 7) or KGMA (Nov 7–8) if music > making tonight.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/7.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul → Cheonan (transfer)

- **Morning:** Mugunghwa Seoul → Cheonan (~55–60 min) or Line 1 (~1 hr 20, T-money) or KTX to Cheonan-Asan (~35–40 min). Easy hop.
- **Afternoon:** Cheonan Jungang Market + Arario Gallery — small-maker stalls + modern sculpture yards.
- **Evening:** Hodu-gwaja bakery — watch the walnut-press mold stamp (Hakwha original line).
- **Stay:** Cheonan — ON City Hotel (night 1/5).

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Cheonan · Independence as craft

- **Full day:** Independence Hall of Korea (free) — the craft here is monumental: roof carpentry, stone, and exhibition carpentry at national scale. Allow half-day; campus picnic.
- **Evening:** Quiet market dinner.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 2/5.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Cheonan · Byeongcheon tool food

- **Half day:** Byeongcheon sundae-soup town (bus/taxi ~20–30 min) — the lunch you watch being filled, sliced, and filled again. Lunch-focused, so get there before 12:00.
- **Afternoon:** Back in Cheonan — Taejosan park walk or folk-hall craft demo.
- **Evening:** Pocha supper.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 3/5.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Cheonan · Onyang warm hands

- **Full day:** Onyang Hot Spring district (Line 1) — bathhouses and Onyang Folk Museum: the making here is bath — tile, wood, and water.
- **Evening:** Pepero Day (Nov 11) stalls.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 4/5.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Cheonan · Slow make

- **All day:** Free — revisit the stall you liked, pack for Busan.
- **Stay:** Cheonan — night 5/5.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Cheonan → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Cheonan-Asan → Busan (~2 hr). Confirm hop to Cheonan-Asan.
- **Afternoon:** Nampo-dong: Jagalchi fish-floors (watch the knife work downstairs, eat upstairs) → BIFF Square hand-print lane.
- **Evening:** Gukje Market — shoe, bag, and eyewear makers in tight lanes.
- **Stay:** Busan — ASTI Busan Station (night 1/7).

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Market coast

- **Morning:** Jagalchi dawn re-run if you like the work-light, otherwise Blueline Sky Capsule pastel ride (book) for coast craft.
- **Afternoon:** F1963 eco-complex — factory → bookstore + workshop row.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli drone show (Sat verify) — sky craft.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/7.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Sea temple & fish cake

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa at opening (~07:30) — the water-temple carpentry.
- **Afternoon:** Samjin/Goraesa fish-cake (eomuk) factory shop — watch the fry line, box your gifts (travel-safe to wrap).
- **Evening:** Seomyeon snack pass.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/7.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Huinnyeoul painters

- **Morning:** Huinnyeoul Culture Village — cliff houses turned galleries and ateliers.
- **Afternoon:** Gamcheon Culture Village (early still) — mural-step craft.
- **Evening:** Bupyeong Kkangtong night-market light-craft — tarp kitchens.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/7.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Cable & cliffs

- **Morning:** Taejongdae Danubi train to lighthouse; Songdo Marine Cable Car + Cloud Walkway.
- **Afternoon:** Jeonpo Cafe Street — barista craft mile.
- **Evening:** Seomyeon neon.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/7.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Flex / make gap

- **All day:** UN Memorial Cemetery quiet (craft as restraint) *or* Oryukdo Skywalk + Igidae (coast carpentry).
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/7.
- **Note:** Fireworks/G-STAR mid-Nov windows — swap if official 2026 dates land here.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Buffer

- **All day:** CSAT day — deliberately off. Revisit the market you’d actually buy from.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 7/7.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50). Check in L7 Myeongdong — daytime arrival, 15:00.
- **Afternoon:** Euljiro/Myeongdong last buys — get the maker’s card if you missed it; ICN tax-refund stack ready.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon + Kimjang Festival at aT Center (Nov 20–22).
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 8 of 9 total).

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Wrap & ware

- **Morning:** Haneul Park silver-grass boardwalk — no making, just air — then wrap, weigh, tape.
- **Afternoon:** Final stationery wrap.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner near Seoul Station; two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 9 of 9.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:15; taxi to Seoul Station → **AREX Express → ICN** (~43 min, depart ~09:00, arrive ~09:45) — 3h+ before 13:00 flight.
- **Afternoon:** Fly home 13:00.
