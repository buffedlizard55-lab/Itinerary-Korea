## Who this suits

Shoppers who want a plan, not a haul panic. You’ll do Myeongdong and Olive Young with a list, hit Seongsu pop-ups while they’re fresh, use Daejeon as a low-pressure market rehearsal, then size the big Busan buys before a generous 3-night Seoul finish with time for exchanges and tax-refund processing.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Arrival at hotel **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun** — full-service desk, City Hall/Seoul Station edge, easy next-day hop to Myeongdong. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** Convenience-store tonight — save the shopping budget for tomorrow.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30). No buys tonight; rest your feet.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Courtyard Namdaemun (night 1/7 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to **Nine Tree Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then shopping.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Myeongdong recon
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to Nine Tree Myeongdong.

- **Late morning:** Myeongdong street grid + **Olive Young flagship** first sweep (make your skin-care list tonight; don’t buy all today).
- **Afternoon:** Cheonggyecheon rest + Gwanghwamun window shopping.
- **Evening:** Early dinner; open shopping spreadsheet.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Nine Tree Myeongdong (night 2/7).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Seongsu pop-up day

- **Morning:** Seongsu-dong — November pop-up row (brand houses cluster here); DPR pop-ups free to enter 90% of the time — try before you buy.
- **Afternoon:** Seoul Forest break; Seongsu cafe/flagship hop.
- **Evening:** Hongdae side — K-fashion micro-brands.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/7.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Beauty lab

- **Morning:** Apgujeong / Garosu-gil beauty : cafe → Olive Young LAB edit + Amore Seongsu or Apgujeong flagship.
- **Afternoon:** COEX Starfield + Gangnam Station underground mall — trend scan.
- **Evening:** Banpo Han River dusk if you need air.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/7.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Dongdaemun after dark

- **Morning:** Bukchon photo pause or Naksan wall easy walk.
- **Afternoon:** Dongdaemun History & Culture Park + **DDP night market** prep (1000+ stalls; late energy).
- **Evening:** Dongdaemun 20:00–24:00 loop — wholesale blocks, try-on, late tteok.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/7.
- **Plan B:** If crowds overwhelm → Myeongdong night-market re-run (wider lanes).

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Jongno vintage & Gwangjang

- **Morning:** Jongno vintage lanes (Ikseon-dong second-hand) + Gwangjang Market fabric hunt.
- **Afternoon:** Tongin Market lunchbox + Insadong crafts (ceramics, hanji).
- **Evening:** Seoul Plaza free concert if on (Nov 4–18).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/7.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Big-day buffer

- **Afternoon:** Rest + Myeongdong second-buy pass (you now know what’s worth it). My Chemical Romance (Nov 7) / KGMA (Nov 7–8) if you need a style-break concert.
- **Evening:** Early night before transfer.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/7.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Seoul → Daejeon (~50–60 min). Check in near Daejeon Station.
- **Afternoon:** Sungsimdang HQ tasting + Eunhaeng-dong + Jungang Market lanes — market-price cosmetics and socks rehearsal.
- **Evening:** Daejeon Skyroad LED ceiling — night-market glow.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — Ramada Daejeon (night 1/4).

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Daejeon · Market & style

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Expo & edit

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Easy send

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan → Seoul (transfer) — the 3-night buffer starts

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50). Check in Nine Tree Myeongdong — daytime arrival, standard 15:00 — **three nights to sort baggage**.

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Pack & polish

- **Afternoon:** Final weigh; roll, don’t fold.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner near Seoul Station; two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 10 of 10.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:15; taxi to Seoul Station → **AREX Express → ICN** (~43 min, depart ~09:00, arrive ~09:45) — 3h+ before 13:00 flight. Tax-refund kiosk post-security — allow 20 extra minutes if claiming.
- **Afternoon:** Fly home 13:00.
