## Who this suits

First-timers who want the loud side of Korea: drum lines, cheerleaders, chant sheets, and stadium street food — without needing Korean. All venues are metro-reachable and tickets are typically ₩10,000–25,000 per the research calendar; fixtures for November release late Sep / early Oct 2026.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Expect immigration + bags to take 45–90 minutes, so hotel arrival lands **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): L7 MYEONGDONG by LOTTE HOTELS** — central Myeongdong, 2-min walk to Myeongdong Station. Dataset price range **$150–210** (standard queen/king). **Confirm the 24-hour front desk and late-check-in policy at booking; email flight number. Standard check-in 15:00.**
- **Alternates (also verify 24-hour desk):** Nine Tree by Parnas Seoul Myeongdong 1 ($100–145) · Ibis Styles Ambassador Seoul Myeongdong ($90–130) · Hotel Skypark Myeongdong 3 ($95–135).
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit, in order of preference:** (1) AREX all-stop ICN → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi/Kakao T ~60–75 min, ₩70,000–100,000. Save the hotel’s Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** CU/GS25 or 24-hour soup near Myeongdong — no timed dinner tonight.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Evening/Late:** Land 21:00 → immigration, bags, eSIM → AREX all-stop → check in at 24-hour desk (target ~23:30).
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 1/7 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop (one subway stop / 5-min taxi) to **Ibis Styles Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then sports scouting starts.
- **Plan B:** Missed last AREX → N6001 night bus; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Easy opener, ticket hunt
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to Ibis Styles Myeongdong.

- **Late morning:** Seoul Tourism Info Center at City Hall — pick up fixture printouts for V-League/KBL while on free Wi-Fi.
- **Afternoon:** Gyeongbokgung outer + Cheonggyecheon stroll — light legs for jet lag.
- **Evening:** Open NOL / Interpark / KOVO on hotel Wi-Fi; shortlist one volleyball and one basketball home game for the Seoul week. No ticket yet = still a good day.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Ibis Styles Myeongdong (night 2/7).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Volleyball debut

- **Afternoon:** Free Han River History Tour (through Nov 30 per Visit Seoul calendar) or Seoul Outdoor Library final day overlap (Apr 23–Nov 1) — both free, reserved, at the Han/Seoul Plaza.
- **Evening:** **V-League volleyball — Seoul Woori Card Woori WON (men, Jangchung Arena, Line 3 Dongguk Univ. Stn)** or **GS Caltex Seoul KIXX (women, Jamsil Students Gymnasium)** — per events.csv, matches run 2–3×/week in November, tickets ~₩10,000–25,000 at `kovo.co.kr`. Expect drums, chants, 90-min show.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/7.
- **Plan B:** No home fixture this exact night → PC bang LoL Worlds watch warm-up (V-League and KBO overlap early Nov) at a Hongdae PC bang (₩1,500–2,500/hr per research).

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Music bank or baseball shadow
