## Who this suits

Travelers who prefer a base camp. You’ll sleep in the same Myeongdong room for 8 nights straight and collect the classic Seoul day trips (all verified in `research/sources/transport/data/destinations.json` with official sites, transit, and two-traveler KRW estimates). No packing every other day.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Hotel arrival **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Ibis Ambassador Insadong (newly refurbished)** — Insadong/Jongno historic arts district per hotels.json, 3★ mid, area “Insadong / Jongno (historic & arts district)”, price **$110–155** (Standard/Superior). **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number. Standard check-in 15:00.**
- **Alternates (also verify 24-hour desk):** L7 Myeongdong ($150–210) · Nine Tree Myeongdong 1 ($100–145) · Ibis Styles Myeongdong ($90–130).
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** CU/GS25 tonight — day trips start early, so sleep matters.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30).
- **Stay:** Seoul — Ibis Insadong (night 1/8 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to **L7 Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then light.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Gentle palace opener
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to L7 Myeongdong.

- **Late morning:** Cheonggyecheon to Gwanghwamun.
- **Afternoon:** Gyeongbokgung outer + Bukchon before-09:30 quiet (residents ask for quiet).
- **Evening:** Insadong dinner; early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 2/8).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Suwon day trip

- **Full day:** **Suwon Hwaseong Fortress** — per destinations.json: take **ITX-Saemaeul 30 min from Seoul Station** (fast) or **Subway Line 1 60 min** to Suwon Station (KORAIL/Line 1), then Bus/Foot to Paldalmun Gate. Walk the 5.7 km stone walls with autumn red ginkgos + silver grass, archery pavilion. **Official site: `swcf.or.kr`** — check which Suwon gate you start at.
- **Evening:** Back to Myeongdong.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/8.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Local Seoul day

- **Morning:** DDP Dream in Light 18:00–22:00 preview + Euljiro printing alleys.
- **Afternoon:** Seongsu-dong warehouses + Seoul Forest ginkgo lane (early Nov peak per events.csv foliage window Oct31–Nov15).
- **Evening:** Culture Flowing Through Seoul Plaza — **Wed Nov 4 18:30 free** at Seoul Plaza per events.csv.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/8.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Nami Island foliage

- **Full day:** **Nami Island & Gapyeong** — per destinations.json: **ITX-Cheongchun 50 min from Yongsan or Cheongnyangni Station** — book ticket on **KorailTalk app**; Nami Island `namisum.com` ginkgo/metasequoia golden canopy. Per research: “Korea’s most famous autumn destination — 2026 foliage forecast ~late Sep (KMA)”.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/8.
- **Plan B:** If island ferry queues look heavy, pivot to Gapyeong Petite France / Garden of Morning Calm (same line).

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · DMZ or museum flex

- **Full day — pick one:** **DMZ & Imjingak Peace Park** (via panmuntour.go.kr portal) — 60–75 min from Seoul Station, third tunnel/Dora Observatory overlooking North Korea, civilian access requires passport + booking per destinations.json; note events.csv warns **general tours suspended since 2023, status fluid** — verify. If DMZ is still suspended, use **National Museum of Korea** (free, museum.go.kr) + War Memorial indoor day.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/8.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Market + marathon-eve flex

- **Morning:** Tongin Market dosirak + Gwangjang mezzanine.
- **Afternoon:** Yeouido Han River air or JTBC Seoul Marathon course preview (Marathon Sun Nov 1 already passed, but World Cup Stadium park remains good).
- **Evening:** KGMA (Nov7–8 Gocheok) or My Chemical Romance (Paradise City, Nov7) per events.csv if ticketed; otherwise Seoul Plaza concert backup (Nov 4/11/18 series).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/8.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul · Re-collect

- **All day:** Revisit the day-trip you’d repeat, or stay local: Changdeokgung Huwon Secret Garden timed tour (garden ₩5,000 + palace ₩3,000, EN 10:30/11:30/14:30/15:30 via `cdg.go.kr`, book 6 days ahead at 10:00) — autumn moonlight tours run through ~Nov 8 per fun/seoul.md.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8/8.

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer) — light 3-night break

- **Morning:** KTX Seoul → Daejeon (~50–60 min). Check in Benikea Daelim near Daejeon Station.
- **Afternoon:** Sojaedong Cafe Street (3 min East Exit) + Sungsimdang bakery — hot fried soboro per `sungsimdang.co.kr` official hours Daily 08:00–22:00 verified in sources-and-notes.md.
- **Evening:** Skyroad LED canopy free Tue–Sun 18:00–21:00 per #79.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — Benikea Daelim (night 1/3).

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Single anchor

- **All day:** National Science Museum (free main hall per walking-maps) + Expo Science Park + Hanbit Tower — all within 10 min bus/taxi Gapcheon. One anchor, then cafe.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 2/3.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Arboretum + departure prep

- **Afternoon:** Pack for Busan; Pepero Day street stalls.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 3/3.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Daejeon → Busan (transfer)

- **Afternoon:** Haeundae Beach + Dongbaek Island boardwalk (APEC House).

## Booking checklist (in order)

1. Flights (ICN round trip).
2. **Night 1 hotel — verify 24-hour front desk + late check-in; email flight number.**
3. Remaining hotels in leg order.
4. KTX: Seoul→Daejeon, Daejeon→Busan, Busan→Seoul (book ~1 month ahead at 07:00 KST).
5. Day-trip tickets: ITX-Saemaeul to Suwon (KorailTalk), ITX-Cheongchun to Gapyeong (KorailTalk), DMZ via `panmuntour.go.kr` (passport, 2–4 weeks ahead); Nami ferry via `namisum.com`. Also Huwon Garden if you keep it (6 days ahead).
6. AREX Day 22 — recheck week before.

## If plans slip

- **Delayed flight:** 24-hour desk absorbs it; day trips slip half-day — Suwon is still the easiest to move.
- **Rain swap bank:** DMZ and Nami are weather-exposed — swap to National Museum (free), MMCA, Leeum, COEX Aquarium, Shinsegae indoor, F1963.
- **Energy swap bank:** Buffers Day 7 / Day 11 / Day 19 — raid these first; no single day trip is the trip.
