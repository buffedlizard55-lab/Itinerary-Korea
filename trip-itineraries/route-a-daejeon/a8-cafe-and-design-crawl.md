## Who this suits

Cafe lovers, slow travelers who collect neighborhoods not landmarks, and design-curious visitors who’d rather sit in a well-made room than queue. Minimal palace protocol; maximal window seats and bakery science.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Hotel arrival **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Hotel Manu Seoul** — Seoul Station/City Hall edge, reliable 24-hour desk, easy taxi from AREX. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Courtyard Namdaemun.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Korean address saved offline.
- **Late supper:** Save the cafe tour — tonight is CU/GS25 or a 24-hour bakery near City Hall.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30).
- **Stay:** Seoul — Hotel Manu (night 1/8 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to **L7 Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then cafes.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Seongsu warehouse row
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to L7 Myeongdong.

- **Late morning:** Seongsu-dong — converted factories → pop-up street (Nov pop-ups peak here) → flagship roastery window seat.
- **Afternoon:** Seoul Forest ginkgo lane + cafe bookshop pause.
- **Evening:** Seongsu dinner — casual pasta or hand-drip set.
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 2/8).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Euljiro & Jongno textures

- **Morning:** Euljiro printing-alley metal workshops + tool-shop cafes (grind, neon, tile).
- **Afternoon:** Ikseon-dong hanok cafes — small houses, high taste.
- **Evening:** Jongno 3-ga makgeolli lane light dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/8.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Hannam & Leeum line

- **Morning:** Hannam-dong hill — design shops + Leeum Museum of Art (verify exhibition & photo rules).
- **Afternoon:** Itaewon second-wave coffee row + Yongsan Park overlook.
- **Evening:** Myeongdong night walk if you need supplies; otherwise hotel lounge.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/8.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Yeonnam & Hongdae edge

- **Morning:** Yeonnam-dong Gyeongui Line Forest park — hanok cafes, flat park benches.
- **Afternoon:** Yeonhui-dong / Yeonnam bakery hop; record-shop pause.
- **Evening:** Hongdae small-gig or busker bench — sit, don’t sprint.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/8.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Bukchon with breaks

- **Morning:** Bukchon before 09:30 (quiet lanes) — then tea house sit.
- **Afternoon:** Changdeokgung outer park only (skip Huwon if you’d rather cafe) → Samcheong-dong gallery cafe.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon by night; early bed.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/8.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · DDP & market graphics

- **Morning:** Dongdaemun Design Plaza — interior ramps.
- **Afternoon:** Gwangjang Market mezzanine + Tongin Market lunchbox.
- **Evening:** Optional concert slot: My Chemical Romance (Nov 7) or KGMA — otherwise keep it cafe-slow.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/8.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul · A last long brunch

- **All day:** Seochon/ Tongin repeat — one more long brunch, stationery shop, pack for Daejeon.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8/8.

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Seoul → Daejeon (~50–60 min). Check in near Daejeon Station.
- **Afternoon:** Sojaedong cafe street — small-house renovations at their most dense; Sungsimdang bread run.
- **Evening:** Skyroad LED canopy stroll.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — Aank Air Daejeon Station (night 1/3).

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Museum & bread

- **Morning:** Daejeon Museum of Art + Lee Ungno Museum (two compact pairings).
- **Afternoon:** Hanbat Arboretum greenhouse sit + Jungang Market noodle alley snack.
- **Evening:** Yuseong foot-bath + evening roastery.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 2/3.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Slow roastery day

- **Morning:** Free morning — bakery redo, favorite cafe window.
- **Afternoon:** Daejeon Arts Center surrounds or O-World cafe hill if you want a park view.
- **Evening:** Early night before Busan.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 3/3.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Daejeon → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Daejeon → Busan (~1 hr 20–1 hr 40).
- **Afternoon:** Check in Haeundae; Jeonpo Cafe Street by metro — Busan’s densest cafe belt.
- **Evening:** Jeonpo long dinner; beach wind down.
- **Stay:** Busan — Ramada Encore Haeundae (night 1/8).

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Busan · Jeonpo deep dive

- **All day:** Jeonpo — cafes edit by day, Seomyeon behind them. Bookshop cafe, roaster, bar hop if you want.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/8.

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Haeundae coast, seated

- **Morning:** Blueline Sky Capsule Mipo → Cheongsapo (book ahead) + Daritdol skywalk.
- **Afternoon:** Cheongsapo cafe balcony + beach train.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli drone light show (Sat, verify time) + Gwangan Bridge.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/8.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Port & print

- **Morning:** F1963 wire-factory conversion — bookstore + cafe in concrete.
- **Afternoon:** Huinnyeoul Culture Village cliff houses — window-light portraits.
- **Evening:** Shinsegae Centum food hall pick.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/8.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Gamcheon, gently

- **Morning:** Gamcheon Culture Village early (before 09:30) — pastel steps then out.
- **Afternoon:** BIFF Square + Gukje Market quick pass; Yongdusan Park view.
- **Evening:** Bupyeong Kkangtong night-market lights.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/8.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · South coast

- **Morning:** Taejongdae Danubi train to lighthouse; pine cliffs.
- **Afternoon:** Songdo Marine Cable Car + Songdo beach deck.
- **Evening:** Seomyeon neon walk home.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/8.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Islands or indie

- **Morning:** Oryukdo Skywalk + Igidae coastal trail short piece.
- **Afternoon:** UN Memorial Cemetery quiet or F1963 return edit cafe.
- **Evening:** Optional V-League/KBL fixture if one runs — otherwise bar-cafe.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 7/8.
- **Note:** Fireworks/G-STAR windows mid-Nov — swap if official dates land here.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Buffer

- **All day:** CSAT day — keep loose. Revisit favorite cafe or Souvenir fish-cake shops.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 8/8.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50). Check in L7 Myeongdong — daytime arrival, 15:00 check-in.
- **Afternoon:** Namdaemun + Euljiro last buys.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon + Kimjang Festival at aT Center (Nov 20–22).
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 9 of 10 total).

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Last roast

- **Morning:** Hannam or Seongsu one-last-roast; weigh bags.
- **Afternoon:** Pack + final stationery stock.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner near Seoul Station; two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 10 of 10.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:15; taxi to Seoul Station → **AREX Express → ICN** (~43 min, depart ~09:00, arrive ~09:45) — 3h+ before 13:00 flight.
- **Afternoon:** Fly home 13:00.
