## Who this suits

Travelers who read wall text. You’ll trade queues for timed entries and use Daejeon’s compact museums as palette cleansers between Seoul and Busan’s large halls. Evenings are for after-hours openings (Wed/Sat 21:00 closes).

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Hotel arrival **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Nine Tree by Parnas Seoul Myeongdong 1** — standard business-hotel reliability, 2-min walk to Myeongdong Station Exit 8 per hotels.json, airport-limousine steps away. Dataset price **$100–145** (Standard/Double/Deluxe). **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number. Standard check-in 15:00.**
- **Alternates (also verify 24-hour desk):** L7 Myeongdong ($150–210) · Ibis Styles Myeongdong ($90–130) · Hotel Skypark Myeongdong 3 ($95–135).
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** Convenience-store tonight — shows start tomorrow.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30).
- **Stay:** Seoul — Nine Tree Myeongdong 1 (night 1/8 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to **L7 Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then art.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Banksy final days
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to L7 Myeongdong.

- **Late morning:** Late start (jet lag) — Cheonggyecheon to City Hall.
- **Afternoon:** **BANKSY: Still Here** at The Hyundai Seoul ALT.1 — last two days before Nov 3 close; book online before landing per research (tickets sold fast in summer). Mon–Thu 10:30–20:00 (last 19:00) · Fri–Sun 10:30–20:30 per listing.
- **Evening:** Seoul Outdoor Library final overlap (Apr 23–Nov 1 at Gwanghwamun/Seoul Plaza per #7) — free open-air reading on your first evening if landing-day timing missed it.
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 2/8).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · BANKSY last call + palace light

- **Morning:** If Day 2 BANKSY sold out, catch the final day (Tue Nov 3) early slot — same hours.
- **Afternoon:** National Museum of Korea (free, museum.go.kr) — colossal, seated, no ticket stress.
- **Evening:** Changgyeonggung Mulbit Yeonhwa palace night media art (Sep8–Nov8 per events.csv, ~19:00–21:00, closed Mon per fun/seoul.md #25/Seoul Route notes) — 8 light scenes around Chunjangji pond, ₩1,000 palace admission.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/8.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Leeum

- **All day:** **Leeum Museum of Art** (Hannam/Itaewon) — Inside Other Spaces through Nov 29 + KOO JEONG A solo from September; permanent collection is collection-free, special exhibition ticketed (hours ~10:00–18:00 closed Mon per #6). Pair with Amorepacific Museum or SeMA Public Space Project (through Dec 31) if energy.
- **Evening:** Free hanbang center break if needed (Jongno herbal foot-bath).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/8.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · MMCA OLED night

- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung + Huwon Secret Garden timed tour (garden ₩5,000 + palace ₩3,000 per events.csv, EN sessions 10:30/11:30/14:30/15:30; book 6 days ahead at 10:00 via `cdg.go.kr`).
- **Afternoon:** Bukchon hanok tea house sit.
- **Evening:** **MMCA Seoul — MMCA × LG OLED Series: Christine Sun Kim** at Seoul Box through Nov 29 (museum entry ₩2,000 per listing) — giant sound/OLED in Samcheong-dong, wedged between palaces. Wed & Sat until 21:00 per gate page — good night museum.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/8.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Artist of the Year + SeMA

- **All day:** MMCA Korea Artist-of-the-Year (Jul24–Dec6, 4 artists) + Full of Colors: Regions Shaping exhibition (through Dec 31) — same MMCA Seoul building, one ticket covers both wings.
- **Evening:** Seochon bookstore row; early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/8.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Flex / watch party

- **All day:** Unscheduled edit/revisit — pick the show you’d repeat, or rest for the K-concert weekend. Evening **Culture Flowing Through Seoul Plaza** (if you skipped Nov 4) is the second Wednesday (Nov 11) not today — so today is open.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/8.
- **Note:** KGMA (Nov7–8 at Gocheok) and MCR (Paradise City, Nov7) are music-program tonight but this itinerary keeps visual art primary — swap if music pulls.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul · Re-shoot

- **All day:** Revisit favorite gallery (Leeum or MMCA) or add Changdeokgung Moonlight Tour fall run (Sep–Nov watch per events.csv; Thu–Sun 19:10–20:10 via `royal.khs.go.kr`, lottery via Ticketlink — 2025 fall ran Sep4–Oct26, so late Oct/early Nov 2026 fall should still cover your dates if announced).
