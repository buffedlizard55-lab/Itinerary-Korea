## Who this suits

Walkers, leaf-peepers, and museum people. Early November is late-autumn color season, and this plan spends maximum time among parks, arboretums, a national park, and Busan's coastal trails — with Daejeon's museums and Expo park as the rainy-day spine.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**; hotel arrival realistically **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station** — attached to the AREX terminus; the fastest bathroom-to-bed late-arrival play. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email your flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
- **Late transit:** AREX all-stop (last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of) → N6001 night bus → taxi ₩70,000–100,000.
- **Late supper:** Seoul Station-area 24-hour soup or convenience store.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30) → bed. Trails start tomorrow, sleep matters.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Four Points (night 1/6).
- **Plan B:** Delay → N6001 or taxi; the desk is staffed either way.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · City-wall warm-up

- **Morning:** Late wake; Naksan section of the Seoul City Wall (Ihwa Mural Village side) — easy 2-hour loop with views.
- **Afternoon:** Changdeokgung + Huwon Secret Garden (book the garden tour slot; fall moonlight finale ~Nov 8).
- **Evening:** Insadong tea house; early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 2/6.

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Peak-foliage palaces

- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung grounds + Guard Changing Ceremony — palace ginkgo maples peak early November.
- **Afternoon:** Seochon lanes; optional short Bugaksan wall segment (passport/ID required for the checkpoint — verify).
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon dusk walk.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/6.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Seoul Forest & Seongsu

- **Morning:** Seoul Forest Park (deer corral, ginkgo lane) peak color window.
- **Afternoon:** Seongsu cafe/lifestyle pop-ups (rest break built in).
- **Evening:** DDP light show if you're out; otherwise early night for the hike tomorrow.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/6.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Bukhansan day hike

- **Full day:** Bukhansan National Park — Baegundae peak classic (8–9 km, allow 5–6 h; subway Line 3 to Gupabal → bus) or the gentler Bukhansanseong fortress route. Late foliage + granite views. Start by 08:30.
- **Evening:** Well-earned jokbal/samgyetang dinner near the hotel; stretch.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/6.
- **Plan B:** Knees say no → Haneul Park silver-grass fields + World Cup Park instead.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · National Museum & park

- **Morning:** National Museum of Korea (free) — the indoor science/history fix.
- **Afternoon:** Ichon Hangang riverside stroll; pack for Daejeon.
- **Evening:** Hongdae casual night (or skip and rest — big week ahead).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/6.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Seoul → Daejeon (~50–60 min); check in near Government Complex.
- **Afternoon:** **Hanbat Arboretum** long afternoon (it's next door; late-autumn leaves).
- **Evening:** Skyroad LED canopy; soup dinner.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — Toyoko Inn Govt Complex (night 1/6).

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Daejeon · Gyeryongsan main hike

- **Full day:** **Gyeryongsan National Park** — Donghaksa → temple valley loop or Gwanumbong ridge (choose by energy). Fall color + temple-town lunch after.
- **Evening:** Yuseong hot-spring district: free public foot baths for trail-tired feet.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 2/6.

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Daejeon · Science Monday

- **Morning:** National Science Museum (quiet on weekdays).
- **Afternoon:** Expo Science Park + Hanbit Tower; The Art Space 193 if an exhibit is up.
- **Evening:** Old-downtown noodle alley.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 3/6.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Daecheong Lake side

- **Morning:** Daecheong Lake/dam outlook drive or bus ride (autumn water views).
- **Afternoon:** Daejeon Astronomical Observatory area or a KAIST-adjacent cafe; Sungsimdang bread run.
- **Evening:** Sojaedong cafe street.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 4/6.
- **Plan B:** Lake logistics feel floppy → O-World Chrysanthemum Festival window instead.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Museum pair

- **Morning:** Daejeon Museum of Art.
- **Afternoon:** Lee Ungno Museum; coffee; laundry.
- **Evening:** Yuseong jjimjilbang session.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 5/6.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Daejeon · Buffer & pack

- **All day:** Unscheduled — revisit a favorite, rest, pack for Busan.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 6/6.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Daejeon → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Daejeon → Busan (~1 hr 20–1 hr 40); check in Haeundae.
- **Afternoon:** Dongbaek Island coastal-loop warm-up.
- **Evening:** Beachfront dinner; listen to the surf.
- **Stay:** Busan — Toyoko Inn Haeundae 2 (night 1/7).

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Dalmaji ridge & coast

- **Morning:** Blueline Park Sky Capsule to Cheongsapo; walk the Daritdol skywalk; beach train back.
- **Afternoon:** Dalmaji-gil hill cafes (moon-viewing road) with ocean overlooks.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli + Saturday M Drone Light Show (verify weekly schedule) — bridge over the water.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/7.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Sea temple sunrise walk

- **Early:** Haedong Yonggungsa at opening — waves against temple stone, best morning of the trip.
- **Afternoon:** Songjeong beach & surf-town cafes; nap.
- **Evening:** Quiet; prep for the big trail day.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/7.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Igidae coastal trail

- **Full morning:** **Igidae Coastal Walk** (Oryukdo Skywalk → Igidae suspension bridge section; ~4–5 km of cliff-hugging boardwalk, allow 3 h).
- **Afternoon:** Recovery lunch near the trail end in Kyungsung/Busan National University area; hotel rest.
- **Evening:** Seomyeon dinner.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/7.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Taejongdae cliffs

- **Morning:** Taejongdae — Danubi train between cliff lookouts; lighthouse descent optional.
- **Afternoon:** Songdo Marine Cable Car + coastal walkway.
- **Evening:** Nampo-dong market dinner.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/7.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Beomeosa forest temple

- **Morning:** Beomeosa (Line 1) — mountain temple among maples; Geumjeongsan lower slopes if legs want more.
- **Afternoon:** UN Memorial Cemetery park (sober, beautifully kept grounds) — gentle flat walking.
- **Evening:** Farewell-coast seafood night; fireworks-festival check (their usual window is this stretch — reshuffle if confirmed).
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/7.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Rain-proof science day

- **Full day:** Busan National Science Museum (Suyeong) or F1963 exhibits + big bookstore cafe; Spa Land if you'd rather soak.
- **Evening:** Final Gwangalli bridge night.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 7/7.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50); Four Points check-in.
- **Afternoon:** Haneul Park silver grass (tail-end season) or Seoul Outdoor Library/autumn plaza.
- **Evening:** Seoul Plaza free concert window (ends Nov 18 — check leftovers) or Cheonggyecheon; light dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7 of 8 total.

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Last walk

- **Morning:** Namsan loop or Seoullo 7017 elevated walk — pick a favorite repeat.
- **Afternoon:** Souvenirs; pack; weigh bags.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner near Seoul Station; two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8 of 8.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:30; AREX Express ~09:00 → ICN ~09:45 (3h+ buffer).
- **Afternoon:** Fly 13:00.

## Booking checklist (in order)

1. Flights.
2. **Night 1 hotel (Four Points) — confirm 24-hour desk + email flight number.**
3. Hotels (Daejeon arboretum-side, Busan beach-side are first-come inventory).
4. KTX ×3 segments (~1 month out).
5. Huwon Secret Garden slot; holiday/weekend trail-head planning (Gyeryongsan is popular on autumn weekends — Day 8 is a Sunday, start early).
6. Seasonal items: Busan drone-show schedule, fireworks-festival confirmation.

## If plans slip

- **Bad weather on hike days (5 & 8):** swap with the museum days (6 & 9) — both pairs are adjacent by design.
- **Trail closures/typhoon remnant:** all coastal walks downgrade to Spa Land + markets + science museums.
- **Flight delay Day 1:** absorbed by the 24-hour-desk rule; Day 2 is intentionally the easiest walking day.
