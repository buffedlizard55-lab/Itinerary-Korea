## Who this suits

Travelers who plan days around meals. Extra Seoul night for market depth, a compressed Daejeon leg focused on the Sungsimdang bakery pilgrimage and Jung-market noodle alleys, then a full Busan seafood week.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**; hotel arrival realistically between **23:00 and 01:00**, later if delayed.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): GLAD Mapo** — big full-service property by Gongdeok/Mapo on the AREX line, relaxed about very late arrivals. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email the hotel your flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Hotel Manu Seoul · Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 3 PM check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit, in order of preference:** (1) AREX all-stop ICN → Gongdeok/Seoul Station (last services ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) night bus N6001; (3) official/Kakao T taxi ~60–75 min, ₩70,000–100,000. Save the hotel's Korean address offline.
- **Late supper (food-first angle):** CU/GS25 triangle kimbap and hot bar, or Mapo-gu's 24-hour gamjatang/gukbap joints near Gongdeok — a legitimately good first meal at midnight.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → 24-hour check-in at GLAD Mapo (target ~23:45) → midnight convenience-store or 24-hour gukbap supper.
- **Stay:** Seoul — GLAD Mapo (night 1/8 — arrival night; the only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop (5-minute taxi / one subway stop) to **Ibis Styles Ambassador Seoul Myeongdong** in Myeongdong — drop the bags, sightseeing runs as written, check in from 15:00.
- **Plan B:** Delay → N6001 night bus or taxi; the desk is staffed regardless.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Soft landing, market lunch
- **First move:** check out of the arrival hotel (~10:00); 5-minute taxi or one subway stop to **Ibis Styles Ambassador Seoul Myeongdong** — bags stored at the desk until 15:00 check-in, then the day below runs as planned.

- **Morning:** Sleep in; Mapo neighborhood coffee.
- **Afternoon:** **Gwangjang Market** long lunch — bindaetteok, yukhoe (beef tartare), mayak gimbap.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon digestif walk; early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 2/8.

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Royal table day

- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung + Royal Guard Changing Ceremony.
- **Afternoon:** **Tongin Market dosirak cafe** (buy old-style brass coins, build a lunchbox) → Samcheong-dong tea.
- **Evening:** Insadong traditional-set-menu dinner (hanjeongsik) or temple-cuisine restaurant.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/8.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Old neighborhoods feed

- **Morning:** Ikseon-dong alley cafes (soufflé pancakes, salt bread).
- **Afternoon:** Euljiro "Hip-jiro" walk → old-school kalguksu/bindaetteok shops tucked in print-shop alleys.
- **Evening:** Myeongdong street-food crawl (lobster tails, egg bread, hotteok) + fried chicken.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/8.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Mangwon & Hongdae

- **Morning:** **Mangwon Market** — locals' market; snack your way down the main aisle.
- **Afternoon:** Hapjeong/Hongdae dessert cafes; record shops.
- **Evening:** Hongdae Korean BBQ with all the banchan; noraebang (coin karaoke) after.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/8.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Kimchi day

- **Morning:** **Museum Kimchikan** kimchi class (book English session ahead).
- **Afternoon:** Bukchon viewpoint walk (work off lunch) or National Museum if tired.
- **Evening:** N Seoul Tower dusk; Namsan-area chef's-table or Korean-fine-casual dinner (reserve).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/8.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Noryangjin & river

- **Morning:** **Noryangjin Fish Market** — buy sashimi downstairs, eat upstairs with spicy stew finish.
- **Afternoon:** The Hyundai Seoul mall food-hall reconnaissance + Han River parks.
- **Evening:** Eland Han River cruise with a light on-board dinner, or riverside chimaek (chicken + beer).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/8.
- **Optional loud slot:** My Chemical Romance (Nov 7) or a Korean Series watch party (through ~Nov 8). Eat late after.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul · Free-feed day

- **Morning:** Bakery-hopping (Seongsu or Anguk) — salt bread, croissants, Korean rice cakes.
- **Afternoon:** Suwon half-day (Hwaseong Fortress + **Suwon galbi** — the city's famous rib) or stay local with a Seongsu pop-up lunch.
- **Evening:** Pack for Daejeon; casual jokbal (pigs' trotters) street dinner in Gongdeok/Mapo area.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8/8.

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer + bread pilgrimage)

- **Morning:** KTX Seoul → Daejeon (~50–60 min); check in near station.
- **Afternoon:** **Sungsimdang main store** — arrive early-afternoon to beat sellouts: fried soboro, chive bread, mammoth bread; Daejeon Jung-ang old-town walk.
- **Evening:** Daejeon Skyroad LED canopy + Sojaedong cafe street dessert.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — Aank Air (night 1/4).

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Noodle alley

- **Morning:** Expo Science Park stroll (walk off yesterday's bread).
- **Afternoon:** Jungang Market area kalguksu alley lunch; Seongsimdang Cake Boutique/Culture Space if the flagship ran out.
- **Evening:** Yuseong foot-bath soak; spicy daeji-galbi dinner.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 2/4.
- **Note:** Daejeon hosts a Noodle festival most years — check if the 2026 dates overlap; if yes, restructure today around it.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Mountain & temple lunch

- **Full day:** Gyeryongsan/Donghaksa — the temple-town approaches are lined with simple sanchae (mountain-vegetable) set-menu restaurants; eat where hikers queue.
- **Evening:** Back to town for a light soup dinner; pack.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 3/4.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Daejeon · Slow market morning

- **Morning:** Daejeon local market breakfast (hotteok, tteok, soup) + last Sungsimdang run for suitcase bread.
- **Afternoon:** Coffee at Sojaedong; early rest before transfer.
- **Evening:** Quiet; easy dak-hanmari or samgyetang.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 4/4.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Daejeon → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Daejeon → Busan (~1 hr 20–1 hr 40); check in ASTI.
- **Afternoon:** **Jagalchi Fish Market** late lunch (grilled eel row, or pick-a-fish service).
- **Evening:** BIFF Square snack pass (seed hotteok!) → Gukje Market lanes.
- **Stay:** Busan — ASTI Hotel Busan Station (night 1/7).

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Nampo food day

- **Morning:** Gukje + Bupyeong day markets (yubu pockets, glass noodles).
- **Afternoon:** Gamcheon Culture Village (coffee with the view).
- **Evening:** **Bupyeong Kkangtong night market** grazing dinner; Busan Tower view.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/7.
- **Note:** If the Busan Fireworks Festival 2026 lands on this Saturday (usual season at Gwangalli) — everything moves around tonight; go early for bridge-side space.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Haeundae coast

- **Morning:** Blueline Park Sky Capsule Mipo → Cheongsapo; grilled clams seafood lunch in Cheongsapo (the town is famous for it).
- **Afternoon:** Beachfront coffee, Haeundae market street snacks.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli M Drone Light Show (Saturdays most weeks — verify) + raw-fish (hoe) center dinner at Millak waterfront.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/7.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Soup-alley Busan

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa early temple walk.
- **Afternoon:** Seomyeon pork-rice-soup (dwaeji-gukbap) alley + wheat-noodle (milmyeon) shop double-header.
- **Evening:** Jeonpo cafe street dessert; grocery-store snack souvenir run.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/7.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Markets of the west

- **Morning:** Songdo Marine Cable Car (appetite builder) + Songdo beach walkway.
- **Afternoon:** Old-port walk around Ami-dong and the Yeongdo bridge area, then back to Nampo for a fish-cake bakery crawl (Samjin eomuk history hall, Goraesa fish cakes).
- **Evening:** Seomyeon youth-zone izakaya-style dinner or pojangmacha tent-bar row near KBS hall area (go with a local guide vibe; check current clusters).
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/7.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Taejongdae & seafood stew

- **Morning:** Taejongdae loop (Danubi train to the cliffs).
- **Afternoon:** Taejongsa/Jagalchi-adjacent haemultang (spicy seafood stew) lunch; nap window.
- **Evening:** Bay 101 yacht-side night photos + nearby pub dinner.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/7.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Repeat & restock

- **Morning:** Favorite-market revisit + dried-seafood souvenir packing (vacuum-sealed gim/eomuk travel well).
- **Afternoon:** Spa Land jjimjilbang rest (you've eaten a lot by now).
- **Evening:** Final Busan feast — grilled shellfish (jogae-gui) or blowfish soup nightcap row.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 7/7.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50); check in at **Ibis Styles Myeongdong** again; the airport limousine also stops nearby.
- **Afternoon:** **Korea Kimjang Grand Festival (aT Center, Nov 20–22)** — hands-on kimchi making if slots remain.
- **Evening:** Namdaemun Market alley dinner (kalguksu alley behind shutter rows) + souvenir street food.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 9 of 10 total.

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Last bites

- **Morning:** Gwangjang return lap or Hongdae brunch.
- **Afternoon:** Olive Young / snack supermarket sweep; pack, weigh bags.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner — Korean BBQ or hanwoo splurge near Mapo; set two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 10 of 10.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:15; 5-minute taxi to Seoul Station, then AREX Express ~09:00 → ICN ~09:45; **grab one final gimbap for the plane.**
- **Afternoon:** Fly 13:00.

## If plans slip

- **Flight delay:** absorbed by the 24-hour desk + 24-hour soup restaurants near Gongdeok.
- **Sold-out Sungsimdang:** Cake Boutique or Cupid branches usually have stock later in the day; go at opening for the flagship.
- **Full day of rain:** markets stay fine under roof; swap coastal walks for Spa Land + department-store food halls.
