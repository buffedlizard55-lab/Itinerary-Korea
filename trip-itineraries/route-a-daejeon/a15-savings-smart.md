## Who this suits

Budget travelers who want the research to do the work. You’ll use the `research/sources/korea/docs` savings vault (all links official) and skip anything that needs Korean phone verification (`본인인증`). Prices are research estimates — verify live.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Hotel arrival **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Hotel Skypark Myeongdong 3** — Myeongdong budget-base per hotels.json, price **$95–135** (check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00, official `skyparkhotel.co.kr`). **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.**
- **Alternates (also verify 24-hour desk):** Ibis Styles Myeongdong ($90–130) · Nine Tree Myeongdong 1 ($100–145) · L7 Myeongdong ($150–210).
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus (cheapest of all); (3) taxi ₩70k–100k only if both gone. Save Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** CU/GS25 budget feast — the savings tour starts with a convenience-store dinner (~₩8,000 per trip-itineraries/b5 style).

**Money posture (all verified per research/sources/korea/docs/tourist-promotions.md + verification-log.md, 2026-08-03 snapshot):**
- **Korea Sale Festa** — national autumn festival **late Oct–mid Nov** (2025 ran Oct 29–Nov 16 per `koreasalefesta.co.kr` in docs; 2026 expected to cover Oct 31–Nov 22). Look for official banners at F&B chains, food halls, convenience stores — instant coupon/scan, **no ID**.
- **Airline boarding pass** — Asiana Magic / Korean Air Excellent Boarding Pass → **10–20% off** at participating Seoul restaurants/tea houses/hotel buffets for **7 days to 1 month after arrival** (source: `flyasiana.com` per docs). Bring passport (name match).
- **Tongin Market** — coin lunchbox **₩10,000 for 20 brass coins** + tray, Tue–Sun 11:00–16:00, closed Mondays/3rd Sunday per `ivisitkorea.com` video-verified link.
- **Dookki tteokbokki buffet** — AYCE adult **₩11,900** (8 rice-cake styles, fried, ramen, soft drinks — per NamuWiki 2026 TOS link); **Myeongryun Jinsa Galbi** pork BBQ AYCE **₩21,900** with soft drinks (same source tier) — both **zero app/ID needed**.
- **Convenience stores** CU/GS25/7-Eleven/emart24 — **1+1 and 2+1 on drinks/snacks**, promos reset 1st of each month per `cu.bgfretail.com` · `gs25.gsretail.com`.
- **Depart-mart immediate tax refund** — at Lotte Mart & E-Mart, **immediate ~6–7% tax exemption at checkout ≥₩15,000 on edible gifts/groceries** with passport per `lottedepart` guides (docs). No airport kiosk needed for these.

**Skip (needs Korean phone `본인인증` — blocked for US tourists):** Starbucks Korea Rewards, Outback/Burger King tier birthdays, most native delivery apps — use Shuttle+Klook or Baemin Guest Checkout (`비회원 주문` with foreign card per `apps.json` note) instead.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX all-stop (~₩5,000 range per B5 budget note — verify on `letskorail.com`) → 24-hour check-in (~23:30) → CU/GS25 supper (watch for 1+1).
- **Stay:** Seoul — Hotel Skypark Myeongdong 3 (night 1/6 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop (one stop) to **Ibis Styles Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then cheap eats.
- **Plan B:** Missed train → N6001 night bus; delay → the desk is staffed, that's the point.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Free palace + Korea Sale Festa scout
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); Ibis Styles Myeongdong continuation.

- **Late morning:** Cheonggyecheon free + Gwanghwamun.
- **Afternoon:** Gyeongbokgung — **rent hanbok ₩15,000–25,000 and palace entry is free** (per B5 budget note) + Guard Changing Ceremony 10:00/14:00 free per `royal.khs.go.kr` in events.csv.
- **Evening:** Scout Myeongdong food halls for **Korea Sale Festa banners** — instant F&B bundle meals per `koreasalefesta.co.kr`.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Ibis Styles Myeongdong (night 2/6).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Dookki + BanK libraries

- **Morning:** National Museum of Korea free (museum.go.kr) — half day, no ticket.
- **Afternoon:** **Dookki tteokbokki buffet ₩11,900 AYCE** — zero phone, zero ID per verification table — good cheap anchor before the Festa.
- **Evening:** War Memorial of Korea free.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/6.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Tongin coin-lunch

- **Morning:** Seoul City Wall Naksan free (Ihwa murals).
- **Afternoon:** **Tongin Market coin lunchbox** — 20 brass coins ₩10,000 Tue–Sun 11:00–16:00 per table (closed Mon/3rd Sun) — load the tray yourself, cheap lunch + show.
- **Evening:** Culture Flowing Through Seoul Plaza free concert **Wed Nov 4 18:30** at Seoul Plaza per `festival.seoul.go.kr` (events.csv) — free.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/6.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Myeongryun + boarding-pass test

- **Morning:** Bukchon viewpoints free.
- **Afternoon:** Test **Asiana/Korean Air boarding pass dining discount** — 10–20% at participating Seoul restaurants per `flyasiana.com` (bring passport for name match, valid 7 days to 1 month — docs tourist-promotions §2). Use it at one listed partner (tea house or hotel buffet).
- **Evening:** N Seoul Tower plaza free (observatory paid, plaza free per B5).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/6.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Market cheap day

- **Morning:** Seoul Forest free (autumn color).
- **Afternoon:** Seongsu pop-ups free to enter 90% per previous itineraries — browse, not buy.
- **Evening:** Convenience-store 1+1 late shop — reset is Nov 1 per docs, so early-month deals are fresh.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/6.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer) — cheap hop

- **Morning:** **Mugunghwa Seoul → Daejeon** or **Subway Line 1** free-ish? Actually Mugunghwa ~₩~8k–10k or Line1 with T-money (cheaper than KTX per `transport/04-intercity-travel-guide.md`) — does not need advance booking — per README shared logistics.

## Booking checklist (in order)

1. Flights (ICN round trip).
2. **Night 1 hotel — verify 24-hour front desk + late check-in; email flight number.**
3. Remaining hotels in leg order (cheapest-first wins this plan).
4. KTX: book Seoul→Daejeon, Daejeon→Busan, Busan→Seoul at Korail open (~1 month ahead 07:00 KST) for early fares.
5. Recreation-only tickets: Blueline capsule (only real splurge), any Huwon slot (6 days ahead via cdg.go.kr) — everything else is free/AYCE.
6. AREX Day 22 — recheck week before.

- **Delayed flight:** 24-hour desk absorbs it; savings festivals run weeks-long — shift Festa/Q1+1 catches.
- **Rain swap bank:** All indoor free/AYCE: National Museum free, War Memorial free, LOTTE Mart indoor, Shinsegae Centum indoor, COEX Mall, F1963.
- **Energy swap bank:** Buffers Day 6 / Day 11 / Day 19 — raid these first; nothing queued is the point.
