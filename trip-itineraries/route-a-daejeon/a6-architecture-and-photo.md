## Who this suits

Photographers, architecture lovers, and anyone who plans a day around golden hour. You’ll shoot palace wood and DDP curves in the same afternoon, then trade city geometry for Daejeon’s Expo relics and Busan’s painted hills and sea cliffs. Mornings start early for empty streets; midday is for cafes, edits, and indoor backup.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Expect immigration + bags to take 45–90 minutes, so hotel arrival lands between **23:00 and 01:00** — later if delayed.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station** — at the AREX terminus, so a late arrival is a flat walk to the elevator. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk and late-check-in policy at booking; email the hotel your flight number.**
- **Alternates (also verify 24-hour desk):** GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit, in order of preference:** (1) AREX all-stop ICN → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi/Kakao T ~60–75 min, ₩70,000–100,000. Save the hotel’s Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** CU/GS25 or a 24-hour gukbap near Seoul Station — don’t plan a timed dinner tonight.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Evening/Late:** Land 21:00 → immigration, bags, eSIM → AREX all-stop → check in at the 24-hour desk (target ~23:30).
- **Stay:** Seoul — Four Points Seoul Station (night 1/7 — arrival night; the only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop (one metro stop / 5-min taxi) to **L7 Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then shooting starts.
- **Plan B:** Missed last AREX → N6001 night bus; heavy delay → taxi. The 24-hour desk absorbs it.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Empty palaces at blue hour
- **First move:** check out of arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to L7 Myeongdong — bags stored, check in from 15:00.

- **Morning:** Bukchon Hanok Village **before 09:00** for empty lanes (quiet-please zone) → Gyeongbokgung outer walls.
- **Afternoon:** Gyeongbokgung + Huwon Secret Garden (book timed Huwon entry; with tripod off, shoot details). Backup: National Palace Museum indoors.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon at dusk → Euljiro neon — tripod reflections after 18:00.
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 2/7).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Concrete and curve

- **Morning:** Dongdaemun Design Plaza — Zaha Hadid curves at 08:30 light; rooftop steps with few crowds.
- **Afternoon:** Euljiro printing-alley textures + Sewoon Sangga brutalism walk.
- **Evening:** N Seoul Tower — cable car up, shoot Seoul grid at blue hour (17:15–18:00). Verify sunset/tower hours.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/7.
- **Plan B:** Rain → Seoul City Hall exhibition + DDP interior galleries.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Han River geometry

- **Morning:** Seoul Forest — ginkgo avenue + mirrored ponds (autumn peak early Nov).
- **Afternoon:** Seongsu-dong — cafe architecture and converted warehouses; bakery break as light softens.
- **Evening:** Yeouido Han River park — Banpo Rainbow Bridge, long-exposure bridge lines (tripod zone past the crowd).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/7.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Market graphic day

- **Morning:** Gwangjang Market — textile mezzanine and food-stall grids (ask before shooting vendors).
- **Afternoon:** Jongno / Ikseon-dong hanok cafes — tiled roof detail work.
- **Evening:** Insadong Ssamziegil layers + Jogyesa lanterns by night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/7.
- **Plan B:** Overcast = perfect market light — keep it.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · North-Seoul walls

- **Morning:** Naksan Seoul City Wall (Ihwa Village murals) — wall leading line to skyline.
- **Afternoon:** Bugaksan segment if ID-check slots open (passport required) — otherwise Changdeokgung stone walls.
- **Evening:** Hongdae late — street portraits and busker silhouettes (ISO-friendly).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/7.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Flex / event light

- **Morning:** Tongin Market coin-lunchbox for a graphic lunch; restock batteries.
- **Afternoon:** Free edit / print-shop walk (Euljiro); DMZ bus option if you want contrast landscapes — book ahead.
- **Evening:** My Chemical Romance (Nov 7) stage-light practice or Seoul Plaza free concert (Nov 4–18) for crowd-light work.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/7.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Seoul → Daejeon (~50–60 min). Check in near Daejeon Station.
- **Afternoon:** Sungsimdang HQ (the bread institution itself) — shoot the trays and 1970s signage, then Eunhaeng-dong old storefronts.
- **Evening:** Daejeon Skyroad LED canopy after dark — long-exposure ceiling.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — LOTTE City Hotel Daejeon (night 1/4).

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Daejeon · Expo & river

- **Morning:** Expo Science Park + Hanbit Tower — 1993 Expo geometry; wide shots from Gapcheon riverside.
- **Afternoon:** National Science Museum (weekday = empty) + Uam Historical Park stone walls.
- **Evening:** Gapcheon riverside at sunset; Yuseong foot-bath reflections.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 2/4.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Leaves and glass

- **Morning:** Hanbat Arboretum — free, huge, late-autumn color still in the greenhouse glass.
- **Afternoon:** Daejeon Museum of Art + Lee Ungno Museum (calligraphy-meets-modern — no photos inside, sketch instead).
- **Evening:** Sojaedong cafe street — small-house conversions for night window shots.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 3/4.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Gyeryongsan day

- **Full day:** Gyeryongsan National Park — Donghaksa approach, temple eaves and maple. Taxi or Bus 107.
- **Evening:** Back in town; minimal edit night.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 4/4.
- **Plan B:** Knees or rain → O-World Chrysanthemum hill + interior greenhouse.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Daejeon → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Daejeon → Busan (~1 hr 20–1 hr 40).
- **Afternoon:** Check in Haeundae; Dongbaek Island APEC House circle + coastal pines.
- **Evening:** Haeundae beach promenade — first ocean long-exposure.
- **Stay:** Busan — L7 Haeundae (night 1/8).

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Busan · Pastel villages

- **Morning:** Gamcheon Culture Village **before 09:30** for empty alleys; layered pastel blocks.
- **Afternoon:** F1963 eco-cultural complex — wire factory → architecture conversion.
- **Evening:** Yeongdo Huinnyeoul Culture Village — cliff houses at dusk.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/8.

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Blueline coast

- **Morning:** Haeundae Blueline Park — Sky Capsule Mipo → Cheongsapo; Daritdol skywalk.
- **Afternoon:** Cheongsapo fishing-port details + beach-train back.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli Myeongji Bridge lit + weekly M Drone Light Show (Saturday, verify time) over Gwangan Bridge.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/8.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Temple by the sea

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa **at opening (~07:30)** — tide rocks before buses arrive (~10:00).
- **Afternoon:** Songjeong surf-beach cafes; edit break.
- **Evening:** Shinsegae Centum City atrium + night facade.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/8.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Old port grids

- **Morning:** Jagalchi + BIFF Square + Gukje Market — tarp grids and fish-crate color.
- **Afternoon:** Yongdusan Park + Diamond Tower — framing the port grid from above.
- **Evening:** Bupyeong Kkangtong night-market lights.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/8.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · South coast cliffs

- **Morning:** Taejongdae — lighthouse cliffs via Danubi train; pine-framed sea.
- **Afternoon:** Songdo Marine Cable Car + Songdo Cloud Walkway.
- **Evening:** Seomyeon neon + Jeonpo Cafe Street windows.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/8.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Edge islands

- **Morning:** Oryukdo Skywalk + Igidae coastal trail partial (tripod light, wind watch).
- **Afternoon:** UN Memorial Cemetery — spare, graphic headstones (quiet photography, no tripod inside hall).
- **Evening:** Optional V-League or KBL night for indoor sports light — check fixtures.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 7/8.
- **Note:** Busan Fireworks Festival (usually early-mid Nov Sat) & G-STAR at BEXCO (mid-Nov) — if official 2026 dates land here, swap evenings.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Buffer & re-shoot

- **All day:** CSAT/Suneung day — keep it deliberately loose (traffic controls, late openings). Re-shoot your favorite block or print at a photo cafe.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 8/8.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50). Check in L7 Myeongdong — daytime arrival, standard 15:00.
- **Afternoon:** Namdaemun Market grid shopping; backup SD-card buy if needed.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon lanterns; Kimjang Festival at aT Center (Nov 20–22) — kimchi-crowd color.
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 8 of 9 total).

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Last light

- **Morning:** Haneul Park silver-grass fields at World Cup Park — dawn haze (mid-Nov is tail season, still graphic).
- **Afternoon:** Final edit / souvenir prints; weigh bags.
- **Evening:** Early dinner near Seoul Station; set two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 9 of 9).

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:15; taxi to Seoul Station → **AREX Express → ICN** (~43 min, depart ~09:00, arrive ~09:45) — 3h+ before 13:00 flight. City Airport Terminal at Seoul Station only for select airlines — verify.
- **Afternoon:** Fly home 13:00.
