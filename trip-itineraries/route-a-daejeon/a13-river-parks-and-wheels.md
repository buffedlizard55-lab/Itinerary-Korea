## Who this suits

Walkers who’d rather roll: gentle cycling on flat riverside paths, seated boardwalks, and park-to-park links. No mountain hikes — the two active days are handlebar days, with taxi backups every time.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Hotel arrival **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Ibis Styles Ambassador Seoul Myeongdong** — central Myeongdong, airport-limo steps away, research price **$90–130** (Standard/Superior, 15:00 check-in, 12:00 check-out). **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.**
- **Alternates (also verify 24-hour desk):** L7 Myeongdong ($150–210) · Nine Tree Myeongdong 1 ($100–145) · Hotel Skypark Myeongdong 3 ($95–135).
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** CU/GS25 tonight — save the Han River picnic for daylight.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30).
- **Stay:** Seoul — Ibis Styles Myeongdong (night 1/6 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to same-hotel continuation (or nearby) — bags stored until 15:00, then wheels.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Palace then park
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); Ibis Styles Myeongdong continuation — bags stored.

- **Late morning:** Cheonggyecheon Stream flat + Gwanghwamun Square.
- **Afternoon:** Gyeongbokgung outer + National Folk Museum (both seated-friendly) — huwon limited today for rest.
- **Evening:** Seoul Plaza free concert window (Nov 4–18 per events.csv, *Wed* Nov 4 specifically) — tonight is stroll only, save legs.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Ibis Styles Myeongdong (night 2/6).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Ttareungyi Han River debut

- **Full day:** **Ttareungyi Han River loop** — rent via T-money GO app (₩1,000/hr per bikeshare.json) — Yeouido Park → Banpo Bridge Rainbow Fountain boardwalk → Seoul Forest gated deer corral. Best route: Yeouido loop to Banpo per bikeshare.json `best_route`. Return bike at any station (check app map for docks).
- **Evening:** Seongsu-dong flat cafe break — bakery, hot chocolate.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/6.
- **Plan B:** Rain → Eland Han River Cruise (≈₩20,000–30,000 via `elandcruise.com` per walking-maps Seoul Route B) — seated, rain-proof.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Forest & arbor

- **Morning:** **Seoul Outdoor Library** final day overlap (Apr 23–Nov 1 per fun/seoul.md #7, free at Gwanghwamun/Seoul Plaza) — morning reading lawns.
- **Afternoon:** Seoul Forest ginkgo avenue + deer + ponds — late-autumn peak window (early Nov per events.csv foliage 10:31–11:15).
- **Evening:** Culture Flowing Through Seoul Plaza free outdoor concert **Wed Nov 4 18:30** at Seoul Plaza per events.csv (free, official `festival.seoul.go.kr`).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/6.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · North path, low bike

- **Morning:** Namsangol Hanok Village + Seoul City Wall Naksan easy mural segment (flat, 2-hour loop).
- **Afternoon:** DDP Dream in Light nightly free show 18:00–22:00 hourly ~25 min per events.csv (`ddp.or.kr`). Seated viewing.
- **Evening:** Early night; prep for Busan?No, still Seoul tomorrow.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/6.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Han River History Tour

- **Morning:** **Han River History Tour** — guided heritage walk Apr 3–Nov 30 2026 per `festival.seoul.go.kr` (events.csv DDP block), free with reservation via `festival.seoul.go.kr`.
- **Afternoon:** Yeouido Han River Bus commuter boat (`hgbus.co.kr` per walking-maps Seoul Route B/Yeouido section) along the riverbank — seated city from water.
- **Evening:** Pack for Daejeon.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/6.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Seoul Station → Daejeon (~50–60 min). Check in Toyoko Inn Government Complex.
- **Afternoon:** Sojaedong Cafe Street heritage houses (3 min East Exit per walking-maps Daejeon Route) + Sungsimdang main branch (fried soboro) — lounge 08:30–21:30 per #64.
- **Evening:** Daejeon Skyroad LED canopy Tue–Sun 18:00–21:00 free per #79 + Hanbit Tower outlook if light.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — Toyoko Inn Government Complex (night 1/6).

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Daejeon · Gapcheon wheels

- **All day:** **Gapcheon Riverfront Park & Bike Path** — paved riverside trail past Expo Science Park and KAIST per #62; rent Daejeon Tashu ~₩1,000, cycle reeds and sports grounds, return by foot-bath.
- **Afternoon:** Yuseong Hot Springs Footbath Park free outdoor thermal (Line 1 Yuseong Spa Stn per #62) — warm feet after wheels.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 2/6.

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Daejeon · Arboretum roll

- **Morning:** **Hanbat Arboretum West & East Gardens** — Korea’s largest urban artificial arboretum, free, 06:00–21:00 per #77 — flat, bike-free boardwalks.
- **Afternoon:** KAIST Campus Ounji Duck Pond foliage trail per #76 — duck pond framed by maples, free.
- **Evening:** Sojaedong bamboo teahouse Pungryu (₩6k–8k per #65) sit.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 3/6.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Science & tower sit

- **Morning:** National Science Museum (free main hall per walking-maps) — weekday quiet.
- **Afternoon:** Expo Science Park + Hanbit Tower + Shinsegae Art Space 193 (₩15,000 38–39F per walking-maps) — sit-able observatories over Gapcheon.
- **Evening:** Gapcheon Arch Bridge lights 18:30–23:00 free per #61.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 4/6.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Gyeryongsan light

- **Half day:** Gyeryongsan National Park Donghaksa Valley (free, 24/7 per #78) — take the 2 km shaded stream trail only (not the summit) — bus 107.
- **Afternoon:** Cafe return, station locker check (Daejeon Station lockers ₩2,000–5,000/24h per `route-a-daejeon/README.md` logistics).
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 5/6.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Daejeon · Buffer

- **All day:** Buffer — bakery, bike redo, pack for Busan.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 6/6.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Daejeon → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Daejeon → Busan (~1 hr 20–1 hr 40).
- **Afternoon:** Haeundae Beach + Dongbaek Island coastal wooden boardwalk (APEC NuriMaru per Busan Route A) — flat, seated.
- **Evening:** Shinsegae Centum food hall; BEXCO preview.
- **Stay:** Busan — Toyoko Inn Haeundae 2 (night 1/7).

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Coast by rail-bike

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Spa Land + harbors

- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/7.
