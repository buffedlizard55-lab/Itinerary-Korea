## Who this suits

Travelers whose highlights list reads concerts, PC bangs, pop-up stores, music shows, and neon streets. Mornings start late, nights run long. Daejeon shrinks to 3 nights (just enough for its esports/science edges) so Seoul and Busan get maximum night range.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**; hotel arrival realistically **23:00–01:00+**. Late arrivals are this itinerary's natural habitat — but Night 1 still needs a guaranteed door.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun** — full-service desk, a short hop from Seoul Station by taxi or one metro stop; relaxed about past-midnight arrivals. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email your flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last services ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus toward Seoul Station/City Hall; (3) taxi ~60–75 min, ₩70,000–100,000. Save the Korean address offline.
- **Late supper:** you're in the nightlife belt — 24-hour spots around Namdaemun/City Hall or a convenience store on the walk.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX or taxi → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:45). If wired, walk Namdaemun's night edges; otherwise crash.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Courtyard Namdaemun (night 1/8).

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · K-pop reboot

- **Afternoon (late start):** **HiKR Ground** (KTO's K-pop experience center) → K-Star Road/Apgujeong drive-by or a label-area cafe (HYBE Insight neighborhood, YG area in Hapjeong — pick your fandom).
- **Evening:** Hongdae busker circuit; coin noraebang debut.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 2/8.

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Pop-up day

- **Afternoon:** **Seongsu-dong** pop-up row (Nov brand pop-ups cluster here) → Seoul Forest espresso break.
- **Evening:** DDP Dream in Light nightly show + Dongdaemun 24-hour shopping blocks.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/8.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Culture fix

- **Afternoon:** Gyeongbokgung + hanbok rental (yes, K-drama photo energy; palace entry free in hanbok) → Bukchon sunset.
- **Evening:** Myeongdong street food + skin-care sweep; Seoul Plaza free concert if scheduled (runs Nov 4–18).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/8.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Gaming & anime

- **Afternoon:** LoL Park / esports-store loop area + anime goods alley; PC bang hour (tournament-viewing snacks included).
- **Evening:** Hongdae club/live-bar night (weeknights are calmer; tonight is the warmup).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/8.
- **Note:** LoL Worlds 2026 Grand Final lands Nov 14 — the app flags watch-party options; this plan is already in Busan then (Day 15) — see that day.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Screens to stage

- **Afternoon:** Musical matinee option — *Elisabeth* (runs to Nov 15) or *Gwanghwamun Love Song* (to Nov 15); book ahead.
- **Evening:** Itaewon international food + vinyl bars, or Hannam-dong cafes by dusk.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/8.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Concert anchor night

- **Evening:** **My Chemical Romance live (Nov 7)** if tickets land; KGMA 2026 (Nov 7–8) is the backup list. Otherwise: Jujutsu Kaisen in Concert (Nov 7–8) or a Korean Series watch party.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul · Freebie Sunday
