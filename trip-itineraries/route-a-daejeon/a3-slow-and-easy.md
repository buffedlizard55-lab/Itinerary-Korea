## Who this suits

Anyone who wants to come home rested, not wrecked: later starts after the late arrival, generous cafe/museum pacing, a true buffer day in every leg, and three calm nights in Seoul before the flight out.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**; expect the hotel between **23:00 and 01:00**, later with delays.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Hotel Manu Seoul** — between Seoul Station and City Hall, walkable from the AREX end of the station, known for accommodating very late arrivals. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email your flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop to Seoul Station (last services ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi/Kakao T ~60–75 min, ₩70,000–100,000. This itinerary takes the taxi option seriously — with luggage and jet lag, a ~₩80,000 door-to-door ride is the "slow & easy" choice. Preload the hotel's Korean address.
- **Late supper:** convenience store or the hotel lounge; no restaurant plans tonight.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → consider the direct taxi to Hotel Manu → 24-hour check-in → straight to bed.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Hotel Manu (night 1/7).
- **Plan B:** AREX if alert and on schedule; night bus as budget fallback.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · No-alarm morning

- **Late morning:** Wake naturally; brunch near City Hall/Seoul Station.
- **Afternoon:** Deoksugung Palace + Stonewall Walkway (flat, central, gentle) → Jeongdong lookout cafe.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon Stream stroll; early dinner; bed on the early side.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 2/7.

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · One palace

- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung + Guard Changing Ceremony; sit down in the shade often.
- **Afternoon:** National Folk Museum (indoor, next door) or a hanok cafe in Seochon for a long restful lunch.
- **Evening:** Insadong early dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/7.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · River air

- **Morning:** Seoul Forest Park flat loop + cafe.
- **Afternoon:** Seongsu slow browsing — lifestyle shops and large-format cafes with real seats.
- **Evening:** Optional seated Han River cruise at sunset; otherwise hotel rest.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/7.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Museum day

- **Morning:** National Museum of Korea (free; plan 2–3 hours seated touring).
- **Afternoon:** Ichon-dong lunch; nap at hotel.
- **Evening:** Myeongdong light pass + early night. Seoul Plaza free concerts run Nov 4–18 if one fits.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/7.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Hanok lanes, short version

- **Morning:** Bukchon viewpoint loop (keep it to ~60–90 min).
- **Afternoon:** Ikseon-dong long cafe lunch.
- **Evening:** N Seoul Tower at dusk by cable car (minimal steps version: taxi to cable-car station).
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/7.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Full rest day

- **All day:** Read, laundry, favorite-cafe return, optional jjimjilbang; pack calmly for tomorrow.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 7/7.

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul → Daejeon (easy transfer)

- **Morning:** No alarm; midday KTX Seoul → Daejeon (~50–60 min) then short taxi to Yuseong.
- **Afternoon:** Check in; Yuseong free public foot baths.
- **Evening:** Quiet hotel-district dinner; sleep early.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — Hotel Interciti (night 1/5).

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Daejeon · Bakery & skyroad

- **Morning:** Sungsimdang flagship (weekday = shorter line).
- **Afternoon:** Sojaedong cafe street; hotel rest.
- **Evening:** Daejeon Skyroad LED canopy after dark.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 2/5.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Arboretum

- **Morning:** Hanbat Arboretum flat walking loop (free; benches everywhere).
- **Afternoon:** Daejeon Museum of Art (same complex area; rest between).
- **Evening:** Yuseong dinner; foot bath round two.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 3/5.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Gentle hill option

- **Morning:** Donghaksa temple valley at Gyeryongsan's base — you can enjoy the temple approach without summiting; or skip entirely for a spa morning.
- **Afternoon:** Jjimjilbang session or hotel rest.
- **Evening:** Old-downtown soup dinner.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 4/5.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Daejeon · Buffer day

- **All day:** Unscheduled on purpose. Coffee, books, pack for Busan.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 5/5.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Daejeon → Busan (easy transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Daejeon → Busan (~1 hr 20–1 hr 40); taxi to Haeundae.
- **Afternoon:** Check in **Grand Josun**; beachfront chairs and the promenade.
- **Evening:** Room-view sunset; simple seafood dinner nearby.
- **Stay:** Busan — Grand Josun (night 1/6).

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Beach day

- **Morning:** Haeundae beach + Dongbaek island flat loop.
- **Afternoon:** Hotel pool/spa or Spa Land Centum (pay entry, stay as long as you like).
- **Evening:** Gwangalli night view + M Drone show if Saturday schedule holds (verify).
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/6.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Temple & town

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa early; return before crowds peak.
- **Afternoon:** Shinsegae Centum easy browsing; department-store restaurant lunch.
- **Evening:** Quiet; maybe the hotel bar.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/6.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Port side, gentle

- **Morning:** Late breakfast; Gamcheon Culture Village with taxi up, wander down.
- **Afternoon:** Jagalchi market lunch (upstairs tables, no standing queue).
- **Evening:** Bupyeong Kkangtong market short pass; taxi home early.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/6.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Cable car & cliffs (seated versions)

- **Morning:** Songdo Marine Cable Car round trip.
- **Afternoon:** Taejongdae Danubi train loop — the train does the walking.
- **Evening:** Seomyeon or hotel-area dinner.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/6.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Flex/buffer day

- **All day:** Unscheduled. Catch up on anything skipped; pack tomorrow's move at leisure.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/6.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan → Seoul (easy transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50); check in Hotel Manu.
- **Afternoon:** Short Myeongdong pass; nap.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon evening walk; early night.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8 of 10 total.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Seoul · Optional culture

- **Morning:** Korea Kimjang Grand Festival (aT Center, Nov 20–22) — a relaxed half-day, or skip.
- **Afternoon:** Jongmyo shrine park (flat, peaceful) or hotel rest.
- **Evening:** Insadong tea farewell dinner.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 9 of 10.

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Pack & polish

- **Morning:** Souvenir loop (Namdaemun or Olive Young) — keep energy low, bags weighed.
- **Afternoon:** Long goodbye lunch near Seoul Station.
- **Evening:** Early to bed; two alarms; AREX timing confirmed.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 10 of 10.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:30; AREX Express ~09:00 → ICN ~09:45 (3h+ buffer).
- **Afternoon:** Fly 13:00.

## Booking checklist (in order)

1. Flights.
2. **Night 1 hotel (Hotel Manu) — confirm 24-hour desk + email flight number.**
3. Grand Josun Busan early (premium rooms sell first).
4. Daejeon + final Seoul hotels.
5. KTX ×3 segments when sales open (~1 month out).
6. Nothing else mandatory — that is the point of this itinerary. Optional: Spa Land hours check, cruise seats.

## If plans slip

- **Flight delay:** 24-hour desk absorbs it; nothing on Day 2 is timed.
- **Low-energy day:** every leg holds a full buffer day (7, 12, 18) — spend them freely.
- **Bad weather week:** this plan already leans indoor (museums, spas, cafes, department stores); move outdoor anchors to the brightest windows.
