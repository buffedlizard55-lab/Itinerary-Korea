## Who this suits

Travelers who want the trip to feel like a reset. You’ll sit more than you stride: tea ceremonies, hanok courtyards, gentle arboretums, and two real temple mornings without pretending to be a monastic. The busy Seoul transfer is cut to 6 nights to give Daejeon 6 — forest over friction.

## Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at **21:00**. Expect arrival at hotel **23:00–01:00+**.

- **Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun** — full-service desk, City Hall/Seoul Station edge, calm rooms after a long flight. **Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.**
- **Alternates (verify 24-hour desk):** Four Points Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul.
- **Scope of the rule:** **only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk.** From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
- **Late transit:** (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Korean address saved offline.
- **Late supper:** Convenience-store tea + hotel wind-down; no scheduled dinner tonight.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

- **Late:** Land 21:00 → AREX → 24-hour check-in (target ~23:30) → sleep.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Courtyard Namdaemun (night 1/6 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
- **Tomorrow:** check out ~10:00 and hop to **Ibis Styles Myeongdong** — bags stored until 15:00, then tea.
- **Plan B:** Missed AREX → N6001; heavy delay → taxi.

### Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Tea-house opener
- **First move:** check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to Ibis Styles Myeongdong.

- **Late morning:** Jogyesa Temple — incense, lantern lane (quiet circuit).
- **Afternoon:** Bukchon hanok tea house — long sit, no rush. Gyeongbokgung outer gardens only today.
- **Evening:** Insadong early dinner; lights out.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Ibis Styles Myeongdong (night 2/6).

### Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Palaces, seated

- **Morning:** Gyeongbokgung + National Folk Museum (both seated-friendly).
- **Afternoon:** Changdeokgung + Huwon Secret Garden pre-booked tour — the stillest walk in Seoul (moonlight tours ~Nov 8 finale).
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon dusk; herbal-tea bar.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 3/6.

### Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Mountain view without the climb

- **Morning:** N Seoul Tower plaza (skip observatory queue, walk the plaza forest) + Namsangol Hanok Village.
- **Afternoon:** Seoul Hanbang Jinheung Center (Jongno herbal center) — foot-bath + herbal sachets.
- **Evening:** Seochon quiet lanes.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 4/6.

### Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Forest breathing

- **Morning:** Seoul Forest — deer corral, ginkgo lane (early Nov color peak).
- **Afternoon:** Seongsu tea & hanok cafe bench; Han River Yeouido flat walk.
- **Evening:** Early night; pack for Daejeon.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 5/6.

### Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Museum quiet

- **Morning:** National Museum of Korea (free, generous seating).
- **Afternoon:** War Memorial of Korea (spare, vast — pick one hall) or Leeum if art calls.
- **Evening:** Hongdae soft walk or skip — rest is the plan.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 6/6.

### Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Seoul → Daejeon (~50–60 min). Check in Hotel Interciti (Yuseong side).
- **Afternoon:** Gyeryongsan foothold — Donghaksa temple approach only (short, temple bells, not the summit).
- **Evening:** Yuseong hot-spring district — free public foot baths; warm-towel sit.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — Hotel Interciti (night 1/6).

### Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Daejeon · Temple hill

- **Morning:** Gyeryongsan National Park proper — Donghaksa → Eunjusa gentle loop (pick taxi not full hike if tired).
- **Afternoon:** Hanbat Arboretum (free, flat) — glasshouse warmth if wind rises.
- **Evening:** Sojaedong tea house.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 2/6.

### Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Daejeon · Science, calmly

- **Full day:** Expo Science Park + Hanbit Tower + National Science Museum (weekday = quiet).
- **Evening:** Jungang Market light dinner (no-queue noodles).
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 3/6.

### Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Arboretum & art

- **Morning:** Hanbat Arboretum second pass for morning light + Mangrove greenhouse.
- **Afternoon:** Daejeon Museum of Art + Lee Ungno Museum (compact pair, seated).
- **Evening:** Yuseong oncheon soak (hotel or public bath).
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 4/6.

### Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Slow hill

- **Morning:** Bomunsan Park — low hill, pavilions, city view with minimal climb.
- **Afternoon:** Paper-plant cafe or Daecheong Lake drive quiet (taxi loop, lake wind).
- **Evening:** Early night.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 5/6.

### Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Daejeon · Buffer

- **All day:** Free — bakery redo, herbal market, pack for Busan.
- **Stay:** Daejeon — night 6/6.

### Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Daejeon → Busan (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Daejeon → Busan (~1 hr 20–1 hr 40).
- **Afternoon:** Haeundae beach flat + Dongbaek Island pine loop.
- **Evening:** Shinsegae Centum quiet dinner.
- **Stay:** Busan — ASTI Busan Station (night 1/7).

### Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Sea temple dawn

- **Morning:** Haedong Yonggungsa **at opening (~07:30)** — tide rocks before buses (10:00+).
- **Afternoon:** Songjeong beach tea balcony; rest.
- **Evening:** Gwangalli drone show (Sat verify) from the beach edge — seated viewing.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 2/7.

### Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Mountain temple

- **Morning:** Beomeosa Temple (Line 1 north) — mountain bowl, valley pines (bus close, minimal hike).
- **Afternoon:** Geumjeong quiet or F1963 eco-complex if wind is high.
- **Evening:** Seomyeon light dinner; early bed.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 3/7.

### Day 16 · Mon, Nov 16 — Busan · Port, gently

- **Morning:** UN Memorial Cemetery — spare, moving; headstone rows in pine.
- **Afternoon:** Huinnyeoul Culture Village cliff houses — sea-facing hanok cafes.
- **Evening:** BIFF Square small bite.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 4/7.

### Day 17 · Tue, Nov 17 — Busan · Coast by train

- **Morning:** Blueline Sky Capsule Mipo → Cheongsapo (book) + Daritdol skywalk.
- **Afternoon:** Cheongsapo long tea; beach train back.
- **Evening:** Jeonpo Cafe Street gentle loop.
- **Stay:** Busan — night 5/7.

### Day 18 · Wed, Nov 18 — Busan · Buffer / temple redo

- **All day:** Choice: Oryukdo Skywalk + Igidae short trail *or* Spa Land Centum seated soak (indoor backup).
- **Stay:** Busan — night 6/7.
- **Note:** Busan Fireworks/G-STAR mid-Nov windows — swap if official 2026 dates land here.

### Day 19 · Thu, Nov 19 — Busan · Full rest

- **All day:** CSAT/Suneung day — deliberately off. Hotel tea, beach sit, souvenir fish-cake (Samjin).
- **Stay:** Busan — night 7/7.

### Day 20 · Fri, Nov 20 — Busan → Seoul (transfer)

- **Morning:** KTX Busan → Seoul (~2 hr 30–2 hr 50). Check in Ibis Styles Myeongdong — daytime arrival, 15:00 check-in.
- **Afternoon:** Namdaemun Market easy sweep.
- **Evening:** Cheonggyecheon + Kimjang Festival at aT Center (Nov 20–22) hands-on if you like.
- **Stay:** Seoul — Ibis Styles Myeongdong (night 7 of 8 total).

### Day 21 · Sat, Nov 21 — Seoul · Last tea

- **Morning:** Jogyesa repeat or Bukchon quiet tea house; weigh bags.
- **Afternoon:** Final shopping — herbal teas, ceramics.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner near Seoul Station; two alarms.
- **Stay:** Seoul — night 8 of 8.

### Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)

- **Morning:** Check out ~08:15; taxi to Seoul Station → **AREX Express → ICN** (~43 min, depart ~09:00, arrive ~09:45) — 3h+ before 13:00 flight.
- **Afternoon:** Fly home 13:00.
