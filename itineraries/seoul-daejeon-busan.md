> **Route:** Seoul → Daejeon → Busan → Seoul

## Route decision

This is the stronger choice when the middle stop should feel like a real city stay rather than only a rail-efficient pause. It adds a little more rail distance than Cheonan but gives five nights enough texture to justify the move.

**Recommendation:** Choose this route if experience quality and middle-city variety are the deciding factors. Daejeon earns five nights through science/design, food, parks, and a hot-spring/nature contrast.

**Best for:** Travelers who want the broader third-city experience: science and design spaces, bakery culture, parks, hot springs, and more restaurant variety without giving up a clean KTX route.

**Tradeoff to accept:** Daejeon is farther south than Cheonan, so city-change days need slightly more rail time. Keep the hotel near Daejeon Station or commit to a short taxi on KTX days rather than creating a luggage-heavy local transfer.

**Recommended flow:** Leave the hotel early enough to arrive at ICN around three hours before departure, following airline advice. AREX Express is a good fit near Seoul Station; an airport limousine or taxi can be better for a hotel away from the rail hub or with heavy bags.
