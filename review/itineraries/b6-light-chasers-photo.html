Who this suits

Photographers who want the Seoul–Busan classic spine with a quieter, cheaper middle. You trade Daejeon’s Expo geometry for the Independence Hall’s massive brutalist campus and the Gakwonsa bronze Buddha hill — both excellent at 08:00. Busan then gets 8 nights, so you can wait for the right drone-show sky.

Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at 21:00. Hotel arrival 23:00–01:00+.

  • Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Hotel Manu Seoul — Seoul Station/City Hall edge, steady 24-hour desk, easy AREX connection. Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.

  • Late transit: (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
  • Late supper: CU/GS25 by the hotel — bed, not bowls, tonight.
