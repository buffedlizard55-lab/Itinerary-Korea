Review notes: (leave decisions/comments here)

Who this suits

Anyone treating this trip as actual recovery. Cheonan is the best hot-spring gateway of the two middle cities (the historic Onyang oncheon district and Asan's modern spa resorts are a short Line 1 ride away), so this plan soaks there, adds Busan's Spa Land Centum, and keeps sightseeing to one calming anchor a day.

Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at 21:00; hotel arrival realistically 23:00–01:00+.

  • Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun — full-service desk, 2-minute taxi from the AREX terminus; late arrivals are routine here. Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email your flight number.
  • Alternates (verify 24-hour desk): Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul.
  • Scope of the rule: only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk. From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 3 PM check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
  • Late transit: (1) AREX all-stop (last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70,000–100,000 — this itinerary's default is the taxi: spa trip energy starts tonight.
  • Late supper: hotel lounge or convenience store. Bed is the program.

Booking checklist (in order)

  1. Flights.
  2. Night 1 hotel (Courtyard Namdaemun) — 24-hour desk confirmed + flight number emailed.
  3. Grand Josun Busan early (premium inventory thins fast for November weekends).
  4. Sono Belle Cheonan (resort weekends book up) + final Seoul nights.

  6. Spa reservations: Dragon Hill status check, Spavis ticket window, Spa Land hours/age rules.

If plans slip

  • Flight delay: taxi + 24-hour desk; Day 2 starts at noon by design.
  • Rain all week: honestly fine — this itinerary is 60% indoor by default (spas, museums, cafes, department stores).
  • Too-tired days: buffer days (7, 13, 19) absorb anything; never cut the spa anchors, cut the sights around them.
