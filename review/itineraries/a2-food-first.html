Review notes: (leave decisions/comments here)

Who this suits

Travelers who plan days around meals. Extra Seoul night for market depth, a compressed Daejeon leg focused on the Sungsimdang bakery pilgrimage and Jung-market noodle alleys, then a full Busan seafood week.

Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at 21:00; hotel arrival realistically between 23:00 and 01:00, later if delayed.

  • Night 1 hotel (Seoul): GLAD Mapo — big full-service property by Gongdeok/Mapo on the AREX line, relaxed about very late arrivals. Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email the hotel your flight number.
  • Alternates (verify 24-hour desk): Hotel Manu Seoul · Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
  • Late transit, in order of preference: (1) AREX all-stop ICN → Gongdeok/Seoul Station (last services ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) night bus N6001; (3) official/Kakao T taxi ~60–75 min, ₩70,000–100,000. Save the hotel's Korean address offline.
  • Late supper (food-first angle): CU/GS25 triangle kimbap and hot bar, or Mapo-gu's 24-hour gamjatang/gukbap joints near Gongdeok — a legitimately good first meal at midnight.

Day-by-day

Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

  • Late: Land 21:00 → 24-hour check-in at GLAD Mapo (target ~23:45) → midnight convenience-store or 24-hour gukbap supper.

Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Soft landing, market lunch

  • Morning: Sleep in; Mapo neighborhood coffee.
  • Afternoon: Gwangjang Market long lunch — bindaetteok, yukhoe (beef tartare), mayak gimbap.
  • Evening: Cheonggyecheon digestif walk; early night.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 2/8.

Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Royal table day

  • Morning: Gyeongbokgung + Royal Guard Changing Ceremony.
  • Afternoon: Tongin Market dosirak cafe (buy old-style brass coins, build a lunchbox) → Samcheong-dong tea.
  • Evening: Insadong traditional-set-menu dinner (hanjeongsik) or temple-cuisine restaurant.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 3/8.

Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Old neighborhoods feed

  • Morning: Ikseon-dong alley cafes (soufflé pancakes, salt bread).
  • Afternoon: Euljiro "Hip-jiro" walk → old-school kalguksu/bindaetteok shops tucked in print-shop alleys.
  • Evening: Myeongdong street-food crawl (lobster tails, egg bread, hotteok) + fried chicken.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 4/8.

Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Mangwon & Hongdae

  • Morning: Mangwon Market — locals' market; snack your way down the main aisle.
  • Afternoon: Hapjeong/Hongdae dessert cafes; record shops.
  • Evening: Hongdae Korean BBQ with all the banchan; noraebang (coin karaoke) after.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 5/8.

Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Kimchi day

  • Morning: Museum Kimchikan kimchi class (book English session ahead).
  • Afternoon: Bukchon viewpoint walk (work off lunch) or National Museum if tired.
  • Evening: N Seoul Tower dusk; Namsan-area chef's-table or Korean-fine-casual dinner (reserve).
  • Stay: Seoul — night 6/8.

Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · Noryangjin & river

  • Afternoon: The Hyundai Seoul mall food-hall reconnaissance + Han River parks.
  • Evening: Eland Han River cruise with a light on-board dinner, or riverside chimaek (chicken + beer).
  • Stay: Seoul — night 7/8.

Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul · Free-feed day
