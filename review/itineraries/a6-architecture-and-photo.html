Review notes: (leave decisions/comments here)

Who this suits

Photographers, architecture lovers, and anyone who plans a day around golden hour. You’ll shoot palace wood and DDP curves in the same afternoon, then trade city geometry for Daejeon’s Expo relics and Busan’s painted hills and sea cliffs. Mornings start early for empty streets; midday is for cafes, edits, and indoor backup.

Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at 21:00. Expect immigration + bags to take 45–90 minutes, so hotel arrival lands between 23:00 and 01:00 — later if delayed.

  • Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station — at the AREX terminus, so a late arrival is a flat walk to the elevator. Confirm the 24-hour front desk and late-check-in policy at booking; email the hotel your flight number.
  • Alternates (also verify 24-hour desk): GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
  • Scope of the rule: only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk. From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
  • Late transit, in order of preference: (1) AREX all-stop ICN → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi/Kakao T ~60–75 min, ₩70,000–100,000. Save the hotel’s Korean address offline.
  • Late supper: CU/GS25 or a 24-hour gukbap near Seoul Station — don’t plan a timed dinner tonight.

Day-by-day

Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

  • Evening/Late: Land 21:00 → immigration, bags, eSIM → AREX all-stop → check in at the 24-hour desk (target ~23:30).
  • Stay: Seoul — Four Points Seoul Station (night 1/7 — arrival night; the only 24-hour-desk night).
  • Tomorrow: check out ~10:00 and hop (one metro stop / 5-min taxi) to L7 Myeongdong — bags stored until 15:00, then shooting starts.
  • Plan B: Missed last AREX → N6001 night bus; heavy delay → taxi. The 24-hour desk absorbs it.

Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Empty palaces at blue hour

  • First move: check out of arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to L7 Myeongdong — bags stored, check in from 15:00.
  • Morning: Bukchon Hanok Village before 09:00 for empty lanes (quiet-please zone) → Gyeongbokgung outer walls.
  • Afternoon: Gyeongbokgung + Huwon Secret Garden (book timed Huwon entry; with tripod off, shoot details). Backup: National Palace Museum indoors.
  • Evening: Cheonggyecheon at dusk → Euljiro neon — tripod reflections after 18:00.
  • Stay: Seoul — L7 Myeongdong (night 2/7).

Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Concrete and curve
