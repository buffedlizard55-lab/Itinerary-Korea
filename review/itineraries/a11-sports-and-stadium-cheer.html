Who this suits

First-timers who want the loud side of Korea: drum lines, cheerleaders, chant sheets, and stadium street food — without needing Korean. All venues are metro-reachable and tickets are typically ₩10,000–25,000 per the research calendar; fixtures for November release late Sep / early Oct 2026.

Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at 21:00. Expect immigration + bags to take 45–90 minutes, so hotel arrival lands 23:00–01:00+.

  • Night 1 hotel (Seoul): L7 MYEONGDONG by LOTTE HOTELS — central Myeongdong, 2-min walk to Myeongdong Station. Dataset price range $150–210 (standard queen/king). Confirm the 24-hour front desk and late-check-in policy at booking; email flight number. Standard check-in 15:00.
  • Alternates (also verify 24-hour desk): Nine Tree by Parnas Seoul Myeongdong 1 ($100–145) · Ibis Styles Ambassador Seoul Myeongdong ($90–130) · Hotel Skypark Myeongdong 3 ($95–135).
  • Scope of the rule: only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk. From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
  • Late transit, in order of preference: (1) AREX all-stop ICN → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi/Kakao T ~60–75 min, ₩70,000–100,000. Save the hotel’s Korean address offline.
  • Late supper: CU/GS25 or 24-hour soup near Myeongdong — no timed dinner tonight.
