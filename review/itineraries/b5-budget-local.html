Who this suits

Travelers who'd rather spend on food and experiences than square footage. Uses the repo's savings research: tourist discount programs, free museum days, market eating, and public transit for everything except the arrival night.

Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

Budget or not, Night 1 obeys the same rule — a guaranteed 24-hour check-in after the 21:00 landing.

  • Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Hotel Manu Seoul — the best value of the 24-hour-desk candidates, near Seoul Station/City Hall. Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email your flight number.
  • Alternates (verify 24-hour desk): Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun. If a confirmed-24-hour bed is sold out everywhere, book the cheapest remaining option with a stated 24-hour desk, not just the cheapest room.
  • Scope of the rule: only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk. From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 3 PM check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
  • Late transit: (1) AREX all-stop train (much cheaper than the Express; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus (cheapest of all); (3) taxi is the splurge fallback, ₩70,000–100,000. This plan takes whichever train/bus is running — taxi only if both are gone.
  • Late supper: convenience-store budget dinner (~₩8,000 covers a feast).

Day-by-day

Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

  • Late: Land 21:00 → AREX all-stop (~₩5,000 range, verify) → 24-hour check-in (~23:30–00:30) → CU/GS25 supper.
  • Stay: Seoul — Hotel Manu (night 1/6 — arrival night; the only 24-hour-desk night).
  • Tomorrow: check out ~10:00 and hop (5-minute taxi / one subway stop) to Ibis Styles Ambassador Seoul Myeongdong in Myeongdong — drop the bags, sightseeing runs as written, check in from 15:00.
  • Plan B: Missed train → N6001 night bus; delay → the desk is staffed, that's the point.

Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Free-day opener

  • First move: check out of the arrival hotel (~10:00); 5-minute taxi or one subway stop to Ibis Styles Ambassador Seoul Myeongdong — bags stored at the desk until 15:00 check-in, then the day below runs as planned.
  • Late morning: Cheonggyecheon + Gwanghwamun (free).
  • Afternoon: Gyeongbokgung — rent hanbok (~₩15,000–25,000) and palace entry is free; Guard Changing Ceremony is free.
  • Evening: Gwangjang Market cheap dinner (share plates); Seoul Plaza? concerts start Nov 4.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 2/6.

Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Free museums day

  • Morning: National Museum of Korea (free).
  • Afternoon: War Memorial of Korea (free).
  • Evening: Hongdae free busker shows; budget fried chicken.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 3/6.

Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Wall walk & market

  • Morning: Seoul City Wall Naksan section (free; Ihwa mural lanes).
  • Afternoon: Tongin Market coin lunchbox (₩5,000-ish of coins = lunch + fun).
  • Evening: Cheonggyecheon night walk; free Seoul Plaza concert if on (Nov 4–18).
  • Stay: Seoul — night 4/6.

Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · Hanok & hills

  • Morning: Bukchon viewpoints (free) → Samcheong free galleries.
  • Afternoon: Namsan climb on foot (skip the cable car; the view is the reward) → N Seoul Tower plaza (observatory paid, plaza free).
  • Evening: Myeongdong window shopping; street-food budget dinner.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 5/6.

Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · River & Seongsu (free favorites)

  • Morning: Seoul Forest (free autumn color).
  • Afternoon: Seongsu pop-up browsing (pop-ups are free to enter 90% of the time) → Han River park rest.
  • Evening: Pack; coin-laundry run; last Seoul-1 night.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 6/6.

Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul → Cheonan (cheap transfer)

  • Morning: Subway Line 1 Seoul Station → Cheonan (~1 hr 20) — costs a few thousand won on T-money, no booking; or Mugunghwa ~₩5,000-ish. Check in ON City Hotel.
  • Afternoon: Cheonan Jungang Market free wandering + market lunch.
  • Evening: Sundae-guk dinner — the budget-luxe dish of the region (~₩10,000).
  • Stay: Cheonan — ON City Hotel (night 1/5).

Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Cheonan · Free national monument

  • Full day: Independence Hall of Korea — free admission. Pack convenience-store kimbap for a campus picnic; this is the best free day of the trip.
  • Evening: Hodu-gwaja box (₩ few thousand) as both dessert and tomorrow's snack; home-style dinner near the market.
  • Stay: Cheonan — night 2/5.

Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Cheonan · Buddha hill (free)

  • Afternoon: Taejosan hillside park walk (free, local's choice); Arario Gallery if a show excites (small ticket).
  • Evening: Pocha street dinner; early night.

Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Onyang cheap-soak day

  • Evening: Back to Cheonan; street snacks.
  • Stay: Cheonan — night 4/5.

Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Cheonan · Local rhythm

  • Morning: Market breakfast; university-district walk (cheap eats belt).

  • Stay: Cheonan — night 5/5.

Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Cheonan → Busan (transfer)

Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Busan · Free coast combo

Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Gwangalli Saturday

Day 15 · Sun, Nov 15 — Busan · Temple cliffs for free

Booking checklist (in order)

  1. Flights.
  2. Night 1 hotel (Hotel Manu) — 24-hour desk confirmed + flight number emailed.
  3. All hotels (budget inventory sells fastest in November).
  4. KTX at sales open for the two long legs — cheapest fares go first (~1 month out, 07:00 KST).
  5. Zero mandatory reservations otherwise; optional one-splurge-per-leg pick.
  6. Cards: load T-money; consider Seoul Climate Card (5-day tourist pass) math vs. pay-per-ride for the Seoul legs; WOWPASS if the forex rate beats your card (see the transport research desk).

If plans slip

  • Flight delay: 24-hour desk + night bus = cheapest possible recovery (~₩15,000-ish vs ₩80,000 taxi).
  • Over-budget week: Days 3, 8, 13, 14, 16 are ~₩0-attraction days — lean on them.
  • Rain: free museums in every city; Toyoko free breakfast covers one meal a day all Busan leg.
