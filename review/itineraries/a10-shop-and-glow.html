Who this suits

Shoppers who want a plan, not a haul panic. You’ll do Myeongdong and Olive Young with a list, hit Seongsu pop-ups while they’re fresh, use Daejeon as a low-pressure market rehearsal, then size the big Busan buys before a generous 3-night Seoul finish with time for exchanges and tax-refund processing.

Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at 21:00. Arrival at hotel 23:00–01:00+.

  • Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun — full-service desk, City Hall/Seoul Station edge, easy next-day hop to Myeongdong. Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.
  • Alternates (verify 24-hour desk): Four Points Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Hotel Manu Seoul.
  • Scope of the rule: only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk. From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
  • Late transit: (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
  • Late supper: Convenience-store tonight — save the shopping budget for tomorrow.
