Who this suits

Cafe lovers, slow travelers who collect neighborhoods not landmarks, and design-curious visitors who’d rather sit in a well-made room than queue. Minimal palace protocol; maximal window seats and bakery science.

Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at 21:00. Hotel arrival 23:00–01:00+.

  • Night 1 hotel (Seoul): Hotel Manu Seoul — Seoul Station/City Hall edge, reliable 24-hour desk, easy taxi from AREX. Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number.
  • Alternates (verify 24-hour desk): Four Points Seoul Station · GLAD Mapo · Courtyard Namdaemun.

  • Late transit: (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) taxi ₩70k–100k. Korean address saved offline.
  • Late supper: Save the cafe tour — tonight is CU/GS25 or a 24-hour bakery near City Hall.
