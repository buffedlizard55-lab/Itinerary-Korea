A7 · Family Playground — Seoul → Daejeon → Busan → Seoul

Who this suits

Families with kids ~4–12 (and the adults who carry the snacks). Walking is capped, naps are real, and every leg has a paid indoor backup for rain. No late nights unless you want them — Busan’s drone show is the only night anchor.

Arrival-night rule (non-negotiable)

We land at 21:00. With kids, immigration + bags will feel like 60–90 minutes, so hotel arrival lands 23:00–01:00+.

  • Night 1 hotel (Seoul): GLAD Mapo — AREX-adjacent (Gongdeok/Hongik line), family-friendly rooms, relaxed about very late arrivals. Confirm the 24-hour front desk at booking; email flight number and kid count.
  • Alternates (verify 24-hour desk): Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station · Hotel Manu Seoul · Courtyard by Marriott Seoul Namdaemun.
  • Scope of the rule: only Night 1 needs the 24-hour desk. From Day 2 onward we move to a regular Seoul hotel with standard 15:00 check-in, and the final Seoul leg arrives mid-afternoon — so no other night needs any special late-arrival policy.
  • Late transit, in order of preference: (1) AREX all-stop → Seoul Station/Gongdeok (~60 min; last ~23:30–23:50 — check night-of); (2) N6001 night bus; (3) official taxi/Kakao T with car seat request ~60–75 min, ₩70k–100k. Save Korean address offline.
  • Late supper: CU/GS25 triangle kimbap + banana milk by the hotel — nothing timed tonight.

Day-by-day

Day 1 · Sun, Nov 1 — ICN → Seoul (arrival night)

  • Evening/Late: Land 21:00 → bags → AREX all-stop → check in at 24-hour desk (target ~23:45). No sightseeing; juice box + bed.
  • Stay: Seoul — GLAD Mapo (night 1/7 — arrival night; only 24-hour-desk night).
  • Tomorrow: check out ~10:00 and hop (one stop / 5-min taxi) to Nine Tree Myeongdong — bags stored until 15:00, then gentle start.
  • Plan B: Missed last AREX → N6001 night bus; heavy delay → taxi. Desk is staffed either way.

Day 2 · Mon, Nov 2 — Seoul · Palace with hanbok magic

  • First move: check out arrival hotel (~10:00); taxi to Nine Tree Myeongdong.
  • Late morning: Late wake. Cheonggyecheon Stream flat stroll.
  • Afternoon: Gyeongbokgung Palace — rent hanbok (free palace entry in hanbok) — Guard Changing Ceremony (10:00/14:00 verify) is the show.
  • Evening: Insadong early dinner; convenience-store ice cream walk.
  • Stay: Seoul — Nine Tree Myeongdong (night 2/7).
  • Plan B: Rain → National Palace Museum (on palace grounds, indoor).

Day 3 · Tue, Nov 3 — Seoul · Lotte World day

  • Full day: Lotte World Adventure (indoor park) + Folk Museum inside Lotte. Lockers for coats; weekday = shorter ride lines.
  • Evening: Early night — jet lag still real.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 3/7.

Day 4 · Wed, Nov 4 — Seoul · Underwater & sky

  • Morning: COEX Aquarium (1123 Gangnam; strollers ok) + Starfield Library photo stairs.
  • Afternoon: Seoul Forest — deer corral + playground + leaf piles (early Nov color).
  • Evening: Myeongdong night-market eye-spy — street-food + Olive Young sticker sweep.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 4/7.

Day 5 · Thu, Nov 5 — Seoul · City Wall treasure walk

  • Morning: Namsangol Hanok Village (free, compact) + Seoul City Wall Naksan easy segment (mural lanes = treasure hunt).
  • Afternoon: DDP design plaza run-around (the curve lawn is a kid magnet).
  • Evening: Hongdae walk — buskers with space to dance; early subway home.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 5/7.

Day 6 · Fri, Nov 6 — Seoul · Aquariums vs science

  • Morning: National Museum of Korea (free, huge, benches everywhere) — pick one floor only.
  • Afternoon: Seongsu-dong cafe street — bakery + hot chocolate; Han River Yeouido kites if energy remains.
  • Evening: Pack-light night; laundry slot.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 6/7.

Day 7 · Sat, Nov 7 — Seoul · River and big-event option

  • Morning: Tongin Market dosirak — kids fill the brass coin lunchbox themselves.
  • Afternoon: DMZ kids bus option (book far ahead) — if that’s too much, Han River cruise (40–70 min seated) is the calm swap.
  • Evening: If tickets land: My Chemical Romance (Nov 7) is adult-only; for families use Seoul Plaza free concert (Nov 4–18) or just night-market repeat.
  • Stay: Seoul — night 7/7.

Day 8 · Sun, Nov 8 — Seoul → Daejeon (transfer)

  • Late morning: KTX Seoul → Daejeon (~50–60 min). Check in near Daejeon Station.
  • Afternoon: Sungsimdang bakery HQ — the famous fried soboro & chive bread line is a kid adventure in itself (take numbers, share).
  • Evening: Daejeon Skyroad LED canopy — free light show.
  • Stay: Daejeon — LOTTE City Hotel Daejeon (night 1/5).

Day 9 · Mon, Nov 9 — Daejeon · Science city

  • Full day: National Science Museum (weekday = quiet) + nearby Expo Science Park + Hanbit Tower.
  • Evening: Jungang Market candy/dried-snack hunt; kalguksu noodles for dinner.
  • Stay: Daejeon — night 2/5.

Day 10 · Tue, Nov 10 — Daejeon · Parks & animals

  • Morning: Hanbat Arboretum (free, flat, duck ponds) + Daejeon Citizen Observatory if a show is on.
  • Afternoon: Daejeon O-World — flower park + small animal zone + rides (Chrysanthemum Festival window).
  • Evening: Yuseong hot-spring foot baths — free warm soak, kid-friendly.
  • Stay: Daejeon — night 3/5.

Day 11 · Wed, Nov 11 — Daejeon · Gyeryongsan lite

  • Morning: Short Gyeryongsan approach — Donghaksa temple steps only (not the summit) or Bomunsan hill in town if legs are tired.
  • Afternoon: Art Space 193 or a bakery-cafe redo — let kids pick.
  • Evening: Fried-chicken + beer/adult + kids’ cheese balls night.
  • Stay: Daejeon — night 4/5.

Day 12 · Thu, Nov 12 — Daejeon · Buffer day

  • All day: Sleep in; revisit favorite bakery; pack for Busan. Laundry if needed.
  • Stay: Daejeon — night 5/5.

Day 13 · Fri, Nov 13 — Daejeon → Busan (transfer)

Day 14 · Sat, Nov 14 — Busan · Sky capsule day

Day 22 · Sun, Nov 22 — Seoul → ICN (departure)
