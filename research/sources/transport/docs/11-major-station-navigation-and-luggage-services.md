# Major Station Navigation & Verified Luggage Services

Navigating train stations with heavy luggage is easy in South Korea once you know where the elevators, luggage lockers, and luggage forwarding desks are located.

---

## 1. Verified Luggage Storage & Delivery Services

Instead of hauling 27-inch+ suitcases across subway stairs or onto day trips, use these official verified services:

## 2. Seoul Station Guide (KTX / AREX / Subways 1 & 4)

* **Where AREX Express departs:** Underground floors B2 to B7. Look for orange **"Airport Railroad Express"** signs.
* **Where KTX departs:** Ground Level (2F Main Concourse).
* **Where WOWPASS Kiosks are located:** Near AREX ticket gates (B2) and near Subway Line 1 interchange.
* **Pro-Tip for Day 23 Departure:**
  * If flying **Korean Air** or **Asiana Airlines**, visit the **Seoul Station City Airport Terminal (B2)**.
  * You can check your suitcases and clear Korean outbound immigration right at Seoul Station up to 3 hours before your flight!
  * Then take the AREX Express train empty-handed to Incheon Airport and use the dedicated diplomat/fast-track security lane at T1/T2!

## 3. Busan Station & Singyeongju Station Guide

* **Busan Station (KTX):**
  * Exit 1 on the 2nd floor connects to a pedestrian bridge leading directly to **Busan Metro Line 1 (Orange Line)**.
  * Drop your bags at the **Zimcarry desk** on the 2nd floor as soon as you step off the train!
* **Singyeongju Station (Gyeongju KTX/SRT):**
  * Exit 1 leads directly to the bus platform for **Bus #700** and the **Kakao T official taxi stand**.
  * Use the station coin lockers on the right side of the lobby if you want to visit Bulguksa Temple before checking into your hotel.
