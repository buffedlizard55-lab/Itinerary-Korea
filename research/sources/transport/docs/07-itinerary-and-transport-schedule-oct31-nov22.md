## Trip Summary & Budget Overview for 2 Travelers

* **Dates:** Oct 31, 2026 – Nov 22, 2026 (23 Days / 22 Nights)
* **Recommended Cards:** 2 WOWPASS Cards + 2 Seoul Climate Cards (5-Day Tourist Edition) + KORAIL Saver Pass (or individual KTX tickets).
* **Estimated Total Transit Budget for 2 Travelers (All 23 Days):** ~450,000 KRW (~$320 USD total, or ~$160 USD per person including airport express, KTX intercity trains, subways, buses, and occasional Kakao T taxis!).
