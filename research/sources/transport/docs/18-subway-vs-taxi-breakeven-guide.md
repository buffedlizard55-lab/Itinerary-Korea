# Subway vs. Taxi Break-Even Guide for 2 Travelers

In San Francisco and Japan, taxis are 5x–10x more expensive than public transit. In South Korea, taxi base fares are so low (**4,800 KRW = ~$3.40 USD**) that for **2 travelers splitting the cost**, short taxi trips are often the same price as subway tickets!

---

## 1. Break-Even Rule of Thumb for 2 Travelers

* **Trips Under 3.5 km (e.g. Myeongdong to Bukchon, or Busan Station to Gamcheon):**
  * **2 Subway Tickets Cost:** 3,000 KRW (1,500 KRW × 2).
  * **Kakao T Taxi Cost:** ~5,500 to 6,500 KRW total.
  * **The Verdict:** You pay only **~$2 USD extra total** for both travelers to ride in a climate-controlled taxi, saving 20–30 minutes of stairs and walking!
* **Trips Over 4 km:**
  * Subway remains significantly cheaper (e.g. Gangnam to Hongdae taxi is ~18,000 KRW vs. 3,000 KRW subway).
  * Use **Subway Line 2** or **KTX** for cross-city distances.

---

## 2. How to Call & Pay Without Language Friction

1. Open the **Kakao T app**.
2. Enter your destination pin in English.
3. Select **"General Taxi" (`일반호출`)** and choose **"Pay to Driver" (`직접결제`)**.
4. Pay the driver at the end of the ride using your **WOWPASS**, **T-money card**, or foreign credit card!
