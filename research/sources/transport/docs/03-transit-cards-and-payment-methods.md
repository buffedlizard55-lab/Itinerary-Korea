# Transit Cards & Payment Methods: T-money, WOWPASS, and Climate Card

South Korea is one of the most cashless societies in the world, but **Apple Pay is NOT accepted at subway turnstiles or bus fare boxes.** To travel seamlessly, every traveler needs the right combination of physical transit and payment cards.

---

## 1. T-money Card (The Baseline Essential)

* **What it is:** South Korea's universal rechargeable contactless transit card.
* **Cost:** 3,000 KRW (non-refundable card fee) + whatever balance you load.
* **Where to Buy:** Every convenience store (CU, GS25, 7-Eleven, Emart24) and subway station ticket machines.
* **Where to Recharge:**
  * Convenience stores (hand cash to cashier and say *"T-money charge, please"* or *"충전해 주세요 - Chungjeonhae-juseyo"*).
  * Subway station ticket machines.
  * **CRITICAL NOTE:** Recharging standard T-money cards at convenience stores and subway kiosks is **CASH ONLY (KRW)**! Always keep a small reserve of KRW cash (~20,000–30,000 KRW) for top-ups.
* **Coverage:** Works on all subways, buses, taxis, and convenience stores in Seoul, Busan, Gyeongju, Jeju, Jeonju, and across the entire country.
* **Official Website:** [https://www.t-money.co.kr](https://www.t-money.co.kr)

---

## 2. WOWPASS (The Best All-in-One for Tourists)

* **What it is:** A physical prepaid debit card combined with a built-in T-money IC transit chip.
* **Cost:** 5,000 KRW card issuance fee.
* **How it works:**
  1. Insert USD, EUR, JPY, or GBP cash into a WOWPASS Kiosk (located at Incheon Airport, Seoul Station, major subway stations, and hotels).
  2. The kiosk converts your foreign cash into Korean Won (KRW) debit balance at competitive exchange rates without bank foreign transaction fees.
  3. Use the debit balance at 99% of restaurants, cafes, and shops in Korea.
  4. **Important Dual-Balance Rule:** The WOWPASS card has **two separate balances**:
     * **Debit Balance:** Used for shopping and dining (loaded at kiosks or via app).
     * **T-money Transit Balance:** Used for subway and bus turnstiles (must be topped up with KRW cash at subway machines or convenience stores).
* **Why we recommend it:** You don't have to carry bundles of KRW notes around Korea, and you can track every purchase on the WOWPASS iPhone app!
* **Official Website:** [https://www.wowpass.io](https://www.wowpass.io)

## 3. Seoul Climate Card (Tourist Edition - 2026)

If you are spending several intensive sightseeing days in Seoul, the **Seoul Climate Card (`기후동행카드`)** for tourists is one of the greatest travel bargains in the world.

* **What it is:** A short-term unlimited transit pass for Seoul subways and city buses.
* **2026 Tourist Pricing & Break-Even Points:**
  * **1-Day Pass:** 5,000 KRW (~3.50 USD) — *Break-even: 4 rides*
  * **2-Day Pass:** 8,000 KRW (~5.70 USD) — *Break-even: 6 rides*
  * **3-Day Pass:** 10,000 KRW (~7.10 USD) — *Break-even: 7 rides*
  * **5-Day Pass:** 15,000 KRW (~10.70 USD) — *Break-even: 10 rides (Only 3,000 KRW/day!)*
  * **7-Day Pass:** 20,000 KRW (~14.30 USD) — *Break-even: 13 rides*
* **What is Covered:**
  * Unlimited rides on all Seoul Subway lines (Lines 1–9, Ui-Sinseol Line, Silim Line, Suin-Bundang Line within Seoul).
  * Unlimited rides on Seoul city buses (Blue, Green, Yellow, and Town buses).
* **What is NOT Covered:**
  * AREX Express non-stop train, Shinbundang Line, red intercity buses, or rides outside Seoul city limits (e.g., traveling to Incheon Airport or Suwon requires separate payment or tapping off with extra fee).
* **Where to Buy:** Seoul Subway station Customer Safety Centers (Lines 1–8) and convenience stores near tourist centers (Myeongdong, Hongdae, Seoul Station).
* **Official Website:** [https://english.seoul.go.kr](https://english.seoul.go.kr)

## 4. NAMANE Card & Foreign Credit Cards

* **NAMANE Card:** A customizable photo card that lets you transfer funds between your transit balance and shopping debit balance via smartphone app. Good alternative if you want a custom photo keepsake! ([Official Website](https://www.namanecard.com)).
* **Foreign Visa / Mastercard / Amex:**
  * Works smoothly at hotels, KTX train stations, department stores, cafes, and major restaurants.
  * Does **not** work at subway turnstiles, local buses, or small market stalls (Gwangjing Market food stalls prefer WOWPASS or KRW cash).
