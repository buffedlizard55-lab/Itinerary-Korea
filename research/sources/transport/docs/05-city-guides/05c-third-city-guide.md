# The 3rd City Guide: Gyeongju vs. Jeju Island vs. Jeonju

For your Oct 31 – Nov 22 trip, adding a third destination between Seoul and Busan elevates your itinerary. Here is a detailed transit and cost comparison of the three best options, with **Gyeongju** as our top beginner-friendly recommendation.

## Option A: Gyeongju (The "Museum Without Walls") - Top Choice

### 1. Why Gyeongju is Ideal for Your Dates
In early-to-mid November, Gyeongju's **Bulguksa Temple** and **Daereungwon Tumuli Park** become a sea of crimson maple leaves and golden ginkgos.

### 2. How to Get There & Cost
* **From Busan Station to Singyeongju Station:**
  * **Time:** 35 minutes via KTX or SRT!
  * **Cost:** 11,000 KRW ($7.86 USD) per person | **22,000 KRW ($15.71 USD) for 2 travelers.**
* **From Singyeongju Station to Central Gyeongju:**
  * Take **Bus #700** (~30 mins, 1,500 KRW with T-money) or a **Kakao T Taxi** (~15 minutes, ~15,000 KRW / $10.70 USD) into the historic center.

### 3. Getting Around Gyeongju
* **Buses #10 and #11:** Circular routes that connect downtown Gyeongju to **Bulguksa Temple** and **Seokguram Grotto**. Announcements are in English!
* **Walking & Biking:** The central UNESCO zone (Daereungwon, Cheomseongdae, Hwangridan-gil, Wolji Pond) is flat and walkable—similar to Kyoto.

## Option B: Jeju Island (South Korea's Volcanic Island)

### 1. How to Get There & Cost
* **From Seoul (GMP) or Busan (PUS) to Jeju International Airport (CJU):**
  * **Time:** 1 hour to 1 hour 10 mins.
  * **Cost:** ~50,000 to 70,000 KRW ($35–$50 USD) per person one-way on Jeju Air, T'way, or Korean Air.
* **Booking Site:** [https://www.airport.co.kr/jejueng/index.do](https://www.airport.co.kr/jejueng/index.do)

### 2. Getting Around Jeju Without a Car
* **Express Buses (Red #101 & #102):** Loop the entire island along the coast. Pay with T-money or WOWPASS.
* **Charter Tourist Taxi / Kakao T:** For two travelers without an International Driving Permit (IDP), hiring a licensed tourist taxi for a full day (~180,000 to 200,000 KRW / ~$130 USD for 8 hours) lets you see 5–6 waterfalls and craters effortlessly.

## Option C: Jeonju (Hanok Heritage & Food Capital)

### 1. How to Get There & Cost
* **From Seoul (Yongsan Station) to Jeonju Station:**
  * **Time:** 1 hour 40 minutes via KTX.
  * **Cost:** 34,400 KRW ($24.57 USD) per person | **68,800 KRW ($49.14 USD) for 2 travelers.**

### 2. Getting Around Jeonju
* From Jeonju KTX Station, take a 15-minute Kakao T taxi (~7,000 KRW / $5 USD) directly to the **Jeonju Hanok Village**. The entire heritage village is pedestrian-friendly.
