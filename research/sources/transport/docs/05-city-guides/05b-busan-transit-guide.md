# Busan City Transit Guide

Busan is South Korea's vibrant second city and coastal capital. While its subway system is simpler than Seoul's, its hilly terrain and coastal geography make buses and taxis especially useful.

---

## 1. Arriving in Busan: KTX Station vs. Gimhae Airport

### Arriving by KTX at Busan Station
* You exit directly into central Busan.
* **To Seomyeon (Downtown Hub):** Take **Busan Metro Line 1 (Orange)** north for 6 stops (~10 mins).
* **To Haeundae Beach:** Take Metro Line 1 to Seomyeon and transfer to **Line 2 (Green)** east (~45 mins total), or take Express Bus #1003 directly from outside Busan Station (~40 mins).
* **To Gwangalli Beach:** Take Metro Line 1 to Seomyeon, transfer to Line 2 to Gwangan Station (~35 mins).

### Arriving by Flight at Gimhae International Airport (PUS)
* **By Metro:** Take the **Busan-Gimhae Light Rail Transit (BGLRT)** from the airport to **Sasang Station**, then transfer to **Busan Metro Line 2** toward Haeundae/Seomyeon (~45–60 mins).
* **By Taxi / Kakao T:** ~25,000 to 30,000 KRW to central hotels (approx 30–45 mins).
* **Official Website:** [https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/index.do](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/index.do)

## 2. Busan Metro Lines & Transit Cards

* **Line 1 (Orange):** North-south backbone connecting Busan Station, Nampo-dong (Jagalchi Market / BIFF Square), and Seomyeon.
* **Line 2 (Green):** East-west coastal artery connecting Seomyeon, Gwangalli Beach, Centum City (Shinsegae Department Store / Spa Land), and Haeundae Beach.
* **Transit Card Acceptance:** Your **T-money**, **WOWPASS**, or **Visit Busan Pass** works seamlessly on all Busan subways and buses without any extra registration!

---

## 3. Haeundae Blue Line Park (Beach Train & Sky Capsule)

One of Busan's most scenic transit experiences is a repurposed coastal railway line running from Haeundae (Mipo) to Songjeong Beach:
* **Haeundae Beach Train:** A retro coastal train running at ground level.
  * **Free with Visit Busan Pass!** Otherwise ~7,000 KRW one-way / ~12,000 KRW all-stops pass.
* **Sky Capsule:** A private elevated 4-seater colorful cabin traveling between Mipo and Cheongsapo (~35,000 KRW for 2 people).
* **Booking Tip:** Book Sky Capsule tickets 2–3 weeks early on the official site for sunset slots!

---

## 4. Busan Bus Tips for Steep Hills

* Busan is famous for colorful cliffside neighborhoods like **Gamcheon Culture Village** and **Huinnyeoul Culture Village**.
* Standard subway lines do not reach top cliff elevations—you will ride **Maeul Buses (`마을버스` - Town Buses)** such as Saha 1-1 or 2-2.
* **Safety Warning:** Busan bus drivers navigate sharp switchbacks on steep hills! **Always hold onto grab handles firmly** before the bus starts moving.
* **Official Tourist Portal:** [https://www.visitbusanpass.com](https://www.visitbusanpass.com)
