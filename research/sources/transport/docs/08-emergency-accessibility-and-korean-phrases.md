# Emergency, Accessibility & Essential Korean Transit Phrases

Even with English-friendly apps, knowing a few key phrases and emergency numbers will give you complete peace of mind in South Korea.

---

## 1. 24/7 Tourist Helplines & Emergency Numbers

* **1330 Korea Travel Helpline:**
  * **Phone:** Dial `1330` anywhere in South Korea (toll-free).
  * **Service:** Free 24/7 English, Japanese, and Chinese assistance from official tourism officers. They can translate for your taxi driver in real-time or look up bus timetables!
  * **Website:** [VisitKorea 1330 Info](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140632)
* **Police:** Dial `112`
* **Ambulance / Fire:** Dial `119` (operators have English translation support).

## 2. Lost & Found Procedures on Transit

1. **Lost item on Seoul Subway:**
   * Visit the station's **Customer Safety Center (`고객안전실`)** or call the Seoul Metro Lost & Found (`120` Dasan Call Center).
   * Note the **subway car number** (4-6 digits inside the train car) and the **exact time/station** where you exited.
2. **Lost item in a Kakao T Taxi:**
   * Open the **Kakao T app**, go to **Ride History (`이용기록`)**, select the ride, and tap **"Call Driver" (`기사님과 통화`)**. Because Kakao T tracks every vehicle, recovery rates are exceptionally high!
3. **National Police Lost & Found Portal:**
   * **Lost112 (`로스트112`):** Official nationwide lost property database. [https://www.lost112.go.kr](https://www.lost112.go.kr)

## 3. Avoiding Taxi Scams & Surcharges

* **Always use Official Taxis or Kakao T / Uber:** Official taxis have a glowing sign on top (`택시` / TAXI) and a red LED meter on the dashboard.
* **Meter Rule:** Ensure the driver turns on the meter (`미터기 - Miteogi`). In Seoul, the base fare is **4,800 KRW** for the first 1.6 km.
* **Late-Night Surcharge (`할증` - Haljeung):**
  * Between **22:00 and 23:00 / 02:00 and 04:00:** +20% surcharge.
  * Between **23:00 and 02:00:** +40% surcharge.
* **Highway Tolls:** When traveling from Incheon Airport to central Seoul, highway tolls (~6,600 KRW) are legally added to your final metered total.

## 4. Essential Korean Phrases for Transit

| English Meaning | Korean Hangul | Pronunciation | When to Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Please take me here.** *(showing map/phone)* | 여기로 가주세요. | *Yeogi-ro gaju-seyo.* | Getting into a taxi without Kakao T. |
| **Please stop here.** | 여기서 내려주세요. | *Yeogi-seo naeryeo-juseyo.* | Asking a taxi driver to pull over. |
| **Where is the subway station?** | 지하철역이 어디예요? | *Jihacheol-yeok-i eodi-yeyo?* | Asking for walking directions. |
| **Please recharge T-money.** | 티머니 충전해 주세요. | *T-money chungjeon-hae juseyo.* | Handing KRW cash at convenience stores. |
| **10,000 Won, please.** | 만원 충전해 주세요. | *Man-won chungjeon-hae juseyo.* | Recharging 10,000 KRW onto transit card. |
| **Can I pay by card?** | 카드 결제 되나요? | *Kadeu gyeolje doenayo?* | Asking if international credit card works. |
| **Thank you!** | 감사합니다! | *Gamsahamnida!* | Exiting a bus or taxi. |
