# Public Bikeshare & Pedestrian Micro-Transit Guide

For short 1–2 km scenic journeys in flat areas, renting a city bikeshare is faster and more immersive than waiting for a bus.

---

## 1. Seoul Bikeshare: Ttareungyi (`따릉이`) via T-money GO

* **How to Rent:** Foreign tourists can rent **Ttareungyi** green bicycles directly inside the **T-money GO iPhone app** using a foreign credit card or WOWPASS—no Korean resident ID required!
* **Cost:** **1,000 KRW (~$0.70 USD)** for 1 hour of cycling.
* **Best Scenic Route:** Ride along the flat **Yeouido Han River Park** paths or from **Hongdae** to **Yeonnam-dong Forest Park**.

---

## 2. Gyeongju Heritage Bikeshare (`TaSoJa`)

* **Why Gyeongju is perfect for bikes:** Gyeongju is completely flat, similar to Kyoto.
* **Cost:** **~5,000 KRW** for a half-day rental outside Singyeongju Station or along Hwangridan-gil.
* **Best Route:** Cycle between **Daereungwon Tumuli Park**, **Cheomseongdae Observatory**, and **Wolji Pond**.
