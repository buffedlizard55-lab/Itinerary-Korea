# Deals, Promotions & Tourist Passes: Maximizing Savings for 2 Travelers

Traveling as a pair opens up exclusive small-group discounts in South Korea. Here is our mathematical breakdown of the best verified tourist passes and promotions for your Oct 31 – Nov 22 trip.

---

## 1. KORAIL Saver Pass (For Groups of 2 to 5 Travelers)

The standard KORAIL Pass allows unlimited KTX and regional train rides across Korea, but **the "Saver Pass" option gives an immediate ~10% discount when 2 to 5 people travel together!**

### A. 2026 KORAIL Saver Pass Pricing (per person in KRW / USD)
* **2-Day Flexible Pass:** 121,000 KRW ($86.43 USD) *vs. Standard 131,000 KRW*
* **3-Day Consecutive Pass:** 155,000 KRW ($110.71 USD) *vs. Standard 165,000 KRW*
* **4-Day Flexible Pass:** 224,000 KRW ($160.00 USD) *vs. Standard 234,000 KRW*
* **5-Day Consecutive Pass:** 234,000 KRW ($167.14 USD) *vs. Standard 244,000 KRW*

### B. When Does the Saver Pass Save Money for 2 Travelers?
* **Example Multi-City Loop:** Seoul -> Busan -> Gyeongju -> Seoul
  * *Individual Tickets Cost:*
    * Seoul -> Busan KTX: 59,800 KRW
    * Busan -> Gyeongju KTX: 11,000 KRW
    * Gyeongju -> Seoul KTX: 49,300 KRW
    * **Total Individual Cost:** **120,100 KRW per person (240,200 KRW for 2 people)**
  * *Saver Pass 2-Day Flexible:* **121,000 KRW per person**
  * **Verdict:** You break even immediately, AND you get **up to 2 free reserved seat bookings per day**, plus flexibility to hop on any KTX or ITX train without paying extra!
* **Official Booking Site:** [https://www.letskorail.com](https://www.letskorail.com)

## 2. Discover Seoul Pass (DSP)

* **What it is:** A tourist pass providing free admission to 70+ attractions (Lotte World, N Seoul Tower, Gyeongbokgung Palace, river cruises) + **1 free AREX Express Train ticket** + built-in T-money IC transit card.
* **Pricing (2026):**
  * **24-Hour Pass:** 50,000 KRW ($35.71 USD)
  * **48-Hour Pass:** 70,000 KRW ($50.00 USD)
  * **72-Hour Pass:** 90,000 KRW ($64.29 USD)
* **Break-Even Analysis:** A ticket to Lotte World (~59,000 KRW) + N Seoul Tower (~21,000 KRW) + AREX Express (~11,000 KRW) = **91,000 KRW**. A 48-Hour pass for 70,000 KRW saves 21,000+ KRW per person!
* **Official Website:** [https://www.discoverseoulpass.com](https://www.discoverseoulpass.com)

## 3. Visit Busan Pass

* **What it is:** Provides free entry to top Busan attractions + built-in transit function.
* **Pricing (2026):**
  * **24-Hour Pass:** 55,000 KRW ($39.29 USD)
  * **48-Hour Pass:** 85,000 KRW ($60.71 USD)
  * **Big3 / Big5 Flex Pass:** Select 3 or 5 attractions valid for 180 days.
* **Break-Even Analysis:** Riding the **Haeundae Blue Line Beach Train** (~12,000 KRW) + visiting **Busan X the Sky** observation deck (~27,000 KRW) + **Spa Land Centum City** (~23,000 KRW) = **62,000 KRW**. The 24-hour pass for 55,000 KRW pays for itself on day one.
* **Official Website:** [https://www.visitbusanpass.com](https://www.visitbusanpass.com)

## 4. WOWPASS Cashback & Olive Young Discounts

* When setting up your **WOWPASS** at an airport kiosk, use a verified **Invitation Code** (available on WOWPASS promotion banners) to get **0.5% cashback on foreign currency recharge**.
* **Olive Young, Starbucks, and CU/GS25 Promotions:** The WOWPASS app lists rotating monthly cashback promos (e.g., 1,000 KRW instant cashback at Olive Young or Starbucks when paying with WOWPASS debit).
* **Official Website:** [https://www.wowpass.io](https://www.wowpass.io)

## 5. Immediate Tax Refund (Duty-Free & Tax-Free Shopping)

South Korea offers an **Immediate Tax Refund (`즉시 환급`)** for foreign tourists at participating shops (Olive Young, Lotte Mart, Uniqlo, department stores):
* **How it works:** When spending over **15,000 KRW** in a single purchase, present your physical passport (or electronic passport scan) at the cash register.
* **The Benefit:** The **7–8% VAT tax is instantly deducted** from your total bill at checkout—no need to wait in line at Incheon Airport refund kiosks!
