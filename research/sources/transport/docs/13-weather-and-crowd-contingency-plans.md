# Weather & Peak Autumn Crowds Contingency Guide (Plan B)

November in South Korea offers stunning crimson maples and golden ginkgos, but sudden chilly rain or weekend crowd surges can happen. Here is your verified Plan B transit and activity guide.

---

## 1. Rain & All-Weather Transit Contingencies

| City | Weather Scenario | Original Plan A | Verified Indoor Plan B | Why It Works for Transit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seoul** | Cold Rain | Outdoor Gyeongbokgung Palace & Bukchon | **The Hyundai Seoul (Yeouido)** & **National Museum of Korea** | Both are connected to subway exits via climate-controlled underground tunnels (**Yeouinaru Station** Line 5 / **Ichon Station** Line 4). |
| **Busan** | Rain / Coastal Winds | Taejongdae Coastal Cliff Walk | **Spa Land Centum City** (Luxury Sauna) & Shinsegae Mall | Direct underground access from **Centum City Station (Metro Line 2)**. Spend 4 hours in 18-temperature therapeutic rooms. |
| **Gyeongju** | Rain | Outdoor Cycling & Daereungwon Mounds | **Gyeongju National Museum** & Hwangnyongwon | Indoor Silla dynasty golden crown galleries. Take a short Kakao T taxi (~6,000 KRW). |

---

## 2. Weekend Peak Foliage Crowd Surges

* **Nami Island on November Weekends:** Ferry queues on Saturday/Sunday mornings can exceed 45 minutes.
  * **Plan B Alternative:** Visit **Suwon Hwaseong Fortress** via a 30-minute KORAIL ITX-Saemaeul train from Seoul Station. Its 5.7 km fortress wall never feels crowded!
