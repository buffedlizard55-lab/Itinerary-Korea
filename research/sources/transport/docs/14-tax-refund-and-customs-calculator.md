# Korean VAT Tax Refund & Customs Guide (`즉시 환급`)

South Korea levies a 10% VAT on goods, but foreign tourists are entitled to a **7–8% refund** on purchases over 15,000 KRW (~$11 USD).

---

## 1. Immediate Tax Refund (`즉시 환급`) — The Best Option!

* **Where:** Participating shops including Olive Young, Lotte Mart, Uniqlo, and major department stores.
* **Eligible Spend:** Between **15,000 KRW** and **1,000,000 KRW** per transaction.
* **How It Works:**
  1. Present your physical passport (or electronic passport scan) at the cash register.
  2. The cashier instantly deducts the 7–8% VAT tax from your total bill!
  3. No paperwork or airport kiosk queues required.

---

## 2. Airport Kiosk Tax Refund (Incheon Airport T1 / T2)

If you shop at non-immediate tax-free boutiques or exceed 1,000,000 KRW:
1. Receive a **Tax Refund Receipt** at the store.
2. At Incheon Airport T1/T2 **before checking in your luggage**, scan your passport and receipts at the automated VAT kiosk (3F Departure Hall).
3. Clear security and collect your refund in KRW cash or credit card refund at the airside refund desk.
