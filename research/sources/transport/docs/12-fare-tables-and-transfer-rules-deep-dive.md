## 2. Official Taxi Meter Rates & Surcharges (Seoul & Busan)

* **Base Meter Fare (First 1.6 km):**
  * **Seoul:** 4,800 KRW (~$3.43 USD)
  * **Busan:** 4,800 KRW (~$3.43 USD)
* **Distance & Time Increment:** +100 KRW per 131 meters or 30 seconds.
* **Late-Night Surcharges (`할증`):**
  * **22:00 – 23:00 & 02:00 – 04:00:** +20% surcharge (Base fare becomes 5,800 KRW).
  * **23:00 – 02:00 (Peak Midnight):** +40% surcharge (Base fare becomes 6,700 KRW).
* **Highway Tolls:** Tolls (e.g. Incheon Airport highway toll ~6,600 KRW) are legally added to the metered total at the end of the ride.
* **How to Pay:** Pay via **Kakao T app**, **WOWPASS debit**, physical foreign Visa/Mastercard, or **T-money card**.

## 4. Summary of the Golden Transfer Rules

1. **Tap ON & OFF:** Always tap your physical IC card at the fare box when entering AND exiting buses and subways.
2. **30-Minute Transfer Window:** You get up to **4 free transfers** between subways and buses within 30 minutes of alighting (extended to 60 minutes between 21:00 and 07:00).
3. **Same Route Exception:** You cannot get a free transfer if you get off a bus and board the exact same bus number in the same direction.
