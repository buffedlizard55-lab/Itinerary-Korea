# KTX Station Dosirak (`도시락`) & Transit Food Guide

Like Japan's Ekiben (train station bento), KTX train stations and highway express bus rest areas in South Korea offer gourmet takeaway food culture.

---

## 1. Best Transit Food Spots for 2 Travelers

| Transit Hub | Recommended Spot | Signature Item & Price | Why It's Great |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seoul Station KTX Concourse (2F)** | **Korail Dosirak (`도시락`)** | Bulgogi & Kimchi Hanwoo Bento Box (**11,000 KRW** / ~$7.80 USD) | Buy 15 minutes before your KTX departs and enjoy a hot gourmet bento at your seat! |
| **Busan Station KTX Concourse (2F)** | **Samjin Amook (`삼진어묵`)** | Cheese Amook Croquette & Fish Cake Skewers (**3,500 KRW**) | Busan's most iconic fish cake bakery is right inside the waiting hall. |
| **Highway Express Bus Rest Area (`휴게소`)** | **Rest Area Food Court** | Sotteok Sotteok (`소떡소떡` - Sausage & Rice Cake Skewer) + Walnut Pastry (`호두과자`) | On your Premium Gold bus ride, the driver will pause for 15 minutes at a highway rest area. |
