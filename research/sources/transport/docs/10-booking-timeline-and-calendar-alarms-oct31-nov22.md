## 1. Why Time Zone Math Matters for KORAIL Tickets

* **KORAIL Release Rule:** KTX high-speed train tickets release **exactly 30 days prior to your travel date at 07:00 Korean Standard Time (KST)**.
* **The San Francisco Conversion:** South Korea is 16 hours ahead of San Francisco (Pacific Daylight Time, PDT) in October. Therefore, **07:00 KST on October 8 is 3:00 PM PDT on October 7 in San Francisco!**
* **Why book immediately:** November is peak autumn foliage season in Korea. Friday afternoon and weekend KTX trains between Seoul, Busan, and Gyeongju sell out within hours of release.
