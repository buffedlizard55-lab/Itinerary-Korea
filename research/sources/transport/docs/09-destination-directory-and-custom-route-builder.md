# Destination Directory & Custom Route Builder Guide

To make adding dates, custom activities, and regional day trips as fast and effortless as possible, we have built a **Verified Destination Library** (`data/destinations.json`) and an **Interactive Trip Builder** (`index.html` -> *"🛠️ Custom Trip Builder"* tab).

Every destination below includes 100% verified official website links, nearest subway/KTX stations, transit modes, estimated 2-person costs, and November autumn foliage highlights.

---

## 1. How to Use the Custom Itinerary & Route Builder

When you are ready to finalize your exact dates and activities for your Oct 31 – Nov 22 trip:
1. Open **`index.html`** in your browser and click **`🛠️ Custom Trip Builder`**.
2. Select the **Trip Day** (Day 1: Oct 31 to Day 23: Nov 22).
3. Choose any verified destination from the dropdown. The builder will automatically calculate:
   * Recommended transit mode and card to use.
   * Total cost for 2 travelers in KRW and USD.
   * Verified official website button.
4. Click **`➕ Add to My Trip Schedule`** to dynamically build your customized table.
5. Click **`📋 Copy Itinerary Table (Markdown)`** or **`💾 Export Itinerary (JSON)`** to instantly save or share your customized plan!
