# Intercity Travel Guide: KTX, SRT, Express Buses & Domestic Flights

Getting between Seoul, Busan, and your 3rd City is fast, punctual, and cost-efficient. South Korea's intercity transport network is among the world's best.

---

## 1. High-Speed Rail: KTX vs. SRT

### KTX (Korea Train Express - KORAIL)
* **What it is:** The national high-speed train network operating at up to 305 km/h (190 mph).
* **Primary Hubs:**
  * **Seoul Station** (Central Seoul) ↔ **Busan Station** (2h 30m, 59,800 KRW/person).
  * **Seoul / Yongsan Station** ↔ **Singyeongju Station** (Gyeongju, 2h 05m, 49,300 KRW/person).
  * **Yongsan Station** ↔ **Jeonju Station** (1h 40m, 34,400 KRW/person).
* **When to Book:** Tickets open exactly **1 month in advance at 07:00 KST**. For weekend travel in November (peak autumn foliage), book 2 to 4 weeks early!
* **Luggage Storage:** Large luggage racks are located in the vestibules between train cars. Overhead racks above seats easily fit carry-on bags and backpacks.
* **Official Booking Site:** [https://www.letskorail.com](https://www.letskorail.com) | **iPhone App:** KorailTalk

### SRT (Super Rapid Train - SR)
* **What it is:** An alternative high-speed rail network departing from **Suseo Station** in Gangnam (southern Seoul).
* **Why choose SRT:** ~10% cheaper than KTX (Seoul to Busan is 52,600 KRW/person) and faster if your hotel is in Gangnam or southern Seoul.
* **Official Booking Site:** [https://etk.srail.kr](https://etk.srail.kr)

## 2. Express & Intercity Buses: Premium Gold Reclining Comfort

If trains are sold out or you want a budget-friendly luxury experience, South Korea's highway buses are incredible.

* **Express Buses (`고속버스` - KOBUS):** Connect major city terminals non-stop over highways.
  * **Seoul Hubs:** Seoul Express Bus Terminal (Gangnam / Central City) & Dong Seoul Terminal.
  * **Bus Classes:**
    * **Premium Gold (`프리미엄`):** 160-degree flat-reclining individual pods, privacy curtains, wireless charging, and personal screens. Only 39,800 KRW (~$28 USD) from Seoul to Busan!
    * **Excellent / Udeung (`우등`):** Spacious 2x1 seating with footrests.
    * **Economy / Ilban (`일반`):** Standard 2x2 seating.
* **Official Booking Sites:**
  * KOBUS (Express Bus): [https://www.kobus.co.kr](https://www.kobus.co.kr)
  * TxBus (Intercity Bus): [https://txbus.t-money.co.kr](https://txbus.t-money.co.kr)
  * BUSTAGO: [https://www.bustago.or.kr](https://www.bustago.or.kr)
  * **iPhone App:** T-money GO

## 3. Domestic Flights (Gimpo ↔ Gimhae ↔ Jeju)

* **Seoul Gimpo Airport (GMP):** Located in western Seoul (easily reachable by AREX All-Stop train or Subway Line 9 Express).
* **When Flying Makes Sense:**
  * **Traveling to Jeju Island (CJU):** Flights from Gimpo or Busan Gimhae are mandatory (world's busiest air corridor, flights every 5–10 minutes).
  * **Seoul to Busan:** Flight time is only 60 minutes (~45,000 KRW on sale), but when you add airport check-in and transit times, KTX is faster door-to-door.
* **Official Airport Websites:**
  * Incheon International Airport: [https://www.airport.kr/ap/en/index.do](https://www.airport.kr/ap/en/index.do)
  * Gimhae International Airport (Busan): [https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/index.do](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/index.do)
