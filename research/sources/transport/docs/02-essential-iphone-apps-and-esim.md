# Essential iPhone Apps & eSIM Guide for South Korea

To travel fast, cost-efficiently, and without language stress in South Korea, your iPhone is your command center. Before leaving for your Oct 31 – Nov 22 trip, download and set up these essential applications.

---

## 1. Must-Have iPhone Apps Directory

### A. Navigation & Transit
* **[Naver Map](https://apps.apple.com/app/naver-map-navigation/id311867728)** (`id311867728` | [Official Website](https://map.naver.com))
  * *Why:* #1 navigation app in Korea. Provides turn-by-turn walking paths, exact subway exit numbers, live bus countdowns, and car/door boarding tips.
  * *Setup Tip:* Go to **Settings (`설정`) → Language (`언어`) → English**.
* **[KakaoMap](https://apps.apple.com/app/kakaomap-korea-no-1-map/id304608425)** (`id304608425` | [Official Website](https://map.kakao.com))
  * *Why:* Great alternative map with excellent bus arrival precision and "Road View" street views.
* **[Subway Korea (Malang Studio)](https://apps.apple.com/app/subway-korea/id325944444)** (`id325944444` | [Official Website](https://subway.co.kr))
  * *Why:* The fastest offline-capable subway map and timetable app for Seoul, Busan, Daegu, Gwangju, and Daejeon.

### B. Ride-Hailing & Taxis
* **[Kakao T (Kakao Taxi)](https://apps.apple.com/app/kakao-t/id981110422)** (`id981110422` | [Official Website](https://www.kakaocorp.com/page/service/service/KakaoT))
  * *Why:* The undisputed taxi dispatch app in Korea. Eliminates language barriers with drivers.
  * *Foreigner Setup Secret:* You do **not** need a Korean phone number or Korean bank account to use Kakao T! When requesting a ride, select **"General Taxi" (`일반호출`)** and choose **"Pay to Driver" (`직접결제`)**. Pay at the end of your trip using your physical foreign Visa/Mastercard, WOWPASS, or T-money card.
* **[Uber / UT](https://apps.apple.com/app/uber-request-a-ride/id368677368)** (`id368677368` | [Official Website](https://www.uber.com/kr/en/))
  * *Why:* Your standard SF Uber app works automatically in South Korea! It calls official licensed UT taxis and charges your saved US credit card in KRW.

### C. High-Speed & Intercity Trains/Buses
* **[KorailTalk](https://apps.apple.com/app/korailtalk/id927945147)** (`id927945147` | [Official Website](https://www.letskorail.com))
  * *Why:* Official KORAIL app for booking KTX and ITX trains between Seoul, Busan, Gyeongju, and Jeonju.
* **[T-money GO](https://apps.apple.com/app/t-money-go/id1483433333)** (`id1483433333` | [Official Website](https://txbus.t-money.co.kr))
  * *Why:* Integrates express bus bookings and Seoul bikeshare (Ttareungyi).
* **[BUSTAGO](https://apps.apple.com/app/bustago/id1056586036)** (`id1056586036` | [Official Website](https://www.bustago.or.kr))
  * *Why:* Nationwide intercity bus reservation platform.

### D. AI Translation & Communication
* **[Naver Papago - AI Translator](https://apps.apple.com/app/naver-papago-ai-translator/id1147874819)** (`id1147874819` | [Official Website](https://papago.naver.com))
  * *Why:* Far more accurate than Google Translate for Korean honorifics, menus, and conversational nuances.
  * *Top Feature:* Use **"Image Translation"** to snap photos of Korean restaurant menus, bus signs, or museum labels for instant English overlays.

## 2. Connectivity & eSIM Guide: Why Data is Mandatory

In Korea, uninterrupted high-speed cellular data is essential for checking bus arrivals, hailing taxis via Kakao T, and translating menus.

### Recommended Connectivity Options for 2 Travelers:
1. **eSIM (Best & Easiest for iPhone XS and newer):**
   * *Verified Official Providers:*
     * **[KT (Korea Telecom) Roaming eSIM](https://roaming.kt.com/ib/eng/main.do)** - Unlimited 4G/5G LTE data on Korea's fastest network.
     * **[SK Telecom T world Roaming](https://www.tworld.co.kr)** - Excellent nationwide coverage.
     * **[LG U+ Prepaid eSIM](https://www.lguplus.com)** - Often includes optional Korean phone number for restaurant queuing (CatchTable / Tableling).
   * *Pro-Tip:* Install your eSIM QR code while at home in SF or on airport Wi-Fi at Incheon Airport. Turn on "Data Roaming" for the eSIM once you land.
2. **Pocket Wi-Fi (Alternative for sharing 1 connection):**
   * Can be rented at Incheon Airport T1/T2 arrival desks (~3,000–4,000 KRW/day). However, having individual eSIMs on both iPhones is much safer so you don't get separated.

## 3. Emergency Apps & Helplines

* **1330 Korea Travel Helpline:** Call `1330` toll-free 24/7 in Korea for free English assistance, taxi translation, or emergency bus info. [VisitKorea Helpline Website](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140632).
* **Emergency Ready App:** Official app by the Korean Ministry of Interior and Safety for real-time alerts and nearby shelter/hospital maps. ([App Store Link](https://apps.apple.com/app/emergency-ready-app/id820967368)).
