# 2. Entry Requirements & Documents

Sources: [S1][S3][S7][S8] — see `sources.md`. **Re-verify 1–2 weeks before
departure**, as policies change with little notice.

## Do I need a visa?
**No.** U.S. citizens can visit for **tourism or business up to 90 days**
without a visa. Your ~22-day trip is well within that. [S1]

## K-ETA — NOT required for this trip
- The **Korean Electronic Travel Authorization (K-ETA) exemption for U.S.
  passport holders has been extended through 31 December 2026**. Your trip is
  in 2026, so you travel on the exemption and do **not** need to apply. [S1][S3]
- The requirement resumes **1 Jan 2027**. (If your plans ever slide past
  New Year, you'd need K-ETA.)
- **Do not** use unofficial "K-ETA" sites that charge inflated fees. The only
  official portal is **https://www.k-eta.go.kr**.

## e-Arrival Card — REQUIRED for this trip ⚠️
Because you're traveling on the K-ETA exemption (no valid K-ETA), you must
submit the **e-Arrival Card**, mandatory for all foreign arrivals since
**1 Jan 2026**. [S7][S8]

- **What:** A digital declaration replacing the old paper arrival card.
- **Where:** Official portal **https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr**.
- **When:** Submit **within 72 hours before arrival** (valid for 72 h from
  submission). Do it after check-in/on your outbound flight day.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Who:** All foreign nationals who don't hold a valid K-ETA — including you.
- You'll need: passport details, flight number, accommodation address, basic
  health/contact info.

## Other required / strongly recommended documents
- **Passport** — valid for your entire stay (U.S. passport; no specific
  minimum-validity rule for Korea, but ≥6 months is best practice). [S1]
- **Return / onward ticket** — proof you'll leave within 90 days.
- **Accommodation details** — address of every hotel/Airbnb you'll use.
- **Sufficient funds** — ability to support yourself during the stay.
- **Travel insurance** — strongly recommended; see `04-health-medications.md`.

## Make 3 copies of key documents
Put one set in your carry-on, one in your main bag, and save one in secure
cloud storage (e.g., a private Google Drive/OneDrive folder) accessible
offline:
1. Passport bio page
2. Driver's license / government ID
3. Flight itinerary (outbound + return)
4. e-Arrival Card confirmation
5. Hotel/hostel addresses + phone numbers
6. Travel insurance policy + emergency phone number
7. Prescription medication documentation (see `04-health-medications.md`)

> Keep the cloud copy **encrypted** and shared with a trusted person back home.
