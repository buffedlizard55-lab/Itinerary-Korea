# 11. Emergency Scenario Guide — What to Do When Unplanned Things Happen

> **For:** Two U.S. citizens (1 male / 1 female), California → Seoul 31 Oct – 22 Nov 2026
> **Sources:** Verified official sources S1–S15; scenario protocols derived from U.S. State Dept / Embassy guidance; Korean emergency-service procedures; travel-preparedness best practice
> **Use with:** `docs/10-itinerary-day-plan.md`, `checklists/offline-emergency-card.md`, `checklists/medical-alert-card.md`, `checklists/translation-card.md`, `print/emergency-card.pdf`

## Scenario A — Earthquake / Natural Disaster / Winter Storm (Rare in Nov, but possible)

**Korea risk:** South Korea is on the Pacific Ring of Fire; earthquakes are infrequent but occur (most recently near Gyeongju / Pohang / Ulsan). November is generally stable, but **early winter storms** can delay KTX / flights / roads, especially in mountainous Daejeon/Cheonan area or Busan coast.

**Before travel:** Confirm travel insurance covers **trip interruption / natural disaster / weather delay**.

**If an earthquake occurs:**
1. **Drop, Cover, Hold.** Get under a sturdy table / against an interior wall; protect head/neck; do not run outside during shaking.
2. After shaking stops: check for injuries; call **119** for ambulance if serious; check gas / water / electricity — if gas smell, turn off at valve if safe; do not use elevators.
3. Gather at pre-agreed meeting point (hotel lobby / outside away from buildings / train station open area). Confirm both travelers are safe.
4. Use **1330** (English 24/7) or **U.S. Embassy Seoul (+82-2-397-4114)** for guidance if communications fail / buildings are Unsafe.
5. Monitor **KMA** (`https://www.kma.go.kr/eng/`, S12) for weather / storm updates; check **ICN** (`https://www.airport.kr`, S14) for flight delays; check **AREX** (`https://www.airportrailroad.com`, S15) for train disruptions.

**If an early winter storm delays your 8 Nov / 13 Nov / 20 Nov KTX:**
- Build **3-hour buffer** for any critical travel day (arrival, inter-city moves, departure). Do not book non-refundable events on travel days.
- If KTX delayed: use **KORAIL customer service / station staff** (English generally available at Seoul Station / Busan Station / Daejeon Station) for next available train; ask 1330 for alternatives.
- If flight from Busan / Seoul to U.S. is delayed (before 22 Nov): contact airline for rebooking; use travel insurance claim line; notify CA emergency contact.
- **CSAT 19 Nov + storm:** A storm + CSAT would compound morning traffic; do not attempt to drive near Busan Station in the morning — use metro / walk / hotel concierge.

## Scenario B — Theft / Robbery / Lost Wallet / Lost Phone

**Risk areas:** Crowded markets (Myeongdong, Dongdaemun, Namdaemun, Busan Jagalchi / Seomyeon), Itaewon / Hongdae weekend nights, crowded subway stations.

**If you are robbed / pickpocketed:**
1. **Do not resist.** Safety first — your life is more valuable than cash/cards/passport.
2. Call **112** (police) immediately — report location, description, time. Ask for a copy of the police report (needed for travel insurance claim / embassy if passport taken).
3. If passport stolen: call **U.S. Embassy Seoul +82-2-397-4114 (24/7)** immediately; file police report; get emergency travel document. Do NOT attempt to travel with only a photocopy — you need the emergency doc or a replacement.
4. If cards stolen: call bank numbers (write these down before travel — use the numbers on back of your cards; if you don't have them, ask your CA contact to look up and call for you, or use your bank's international collect number from any phone).
5. If wallet/cash stolen but cards/passports safe: use backup card (carry one card in separate pocket / bag from the other); use Kakao T / metro / hotel concierge; ask 1330 for help.
6. **Notify your CA emergency contact** — they can help with insurance claims, wire money, or contact banks.

**If your PHONE is lost / stolen:**
- Use the **second traveler's phone** immediately to call banks / embassy / 112.
- Use **paper copies** of emergency cards / passport scans / insurance cards / e-Arrival confirmation (kept separate from phones).
- If both phones lost: ask hotel concierge / police station / 1330 for help; use a public phone (available at subway stations / convenience stores) to call 112 or 1330; ask the concierge to call U.S. Embassy on your behalf.
- **Preventive:** Enable “Find My Device” / “Find My iPhone” / Google Find My Device before leaving; share location with CA contact; write down both travelers' IMEI / serial numbers.

## Scenario C — Lost / Dead Phone / No Data / No WiFi

**What it means:** You can't call 1330, use Kakao T, or access maps / translations.

**Protocol:**
1. **Use paper:** Keep `checklists/translation-card.md`, `checklists/medical-alert-card.md`, `checklists/offline-emergency-card.md`, and `docs/10-itinerary-day-plan.md` printed and in each bag.
2. **Use the other traveler's phone.** Designate one phone as “primary” for both travelers; the other is backup. Carry both.
3. **Use hotel / concierge / staff:** Major hotels (especially near Seoul Station / Myeongdong / Busan Haeundae) have English-speaking front desk staff. They can call taxis, translate for 119/1330, and help with directions.
4. **Use public/phone booths:** Korean phone booths (at subway stations, major intersections) work with coins / cards — dial 112, 119, 1330, or +82-2-397-4114 directly.
5. **Use WiFi at any cafe / convenience store (CU / GS25 / 7-Eleven / Starbucks):** Most offer free WiFi; you can use Naver Papago online (or download offline packs before travel) to translate; you can message CA contact via WhatsApp / iMessage if on WiFi.
6. **Pre-download offline maps:** Download Seoul / Busan / Daejeon offline maps in **Naver Maps** or **KakaoMap** before departure; download **Naver Papago** offline translation packs.
7. **Power bank / charger:** Carry at least one 20,000 mAh power bank per traveler; carry a universal adapter (Korea uses 220V / 60Hz, Type C / Type F plugs — same as much of Europe).

## Scenario D — Food Poisoning / Severe Gastroenteritis / Dehydration

**Risk:** Common for travelers — unfamiliar bacteria in food / water / raw seafood (especially in Busan seafood markets / street food). November is cold, but indoor heating can cause rapid dehydration if vomiting / diarrhea.

**Before travel:** Talk to your doctor at least 2 weeks before 31 Oct. Ask for:
- **Prescription anti-diarrheal** (e.g., loperamide / Imodium) — if appropriate for your health.
- **Prescription anti-emetic** (e.g., ondansetron / Zofran) — for severe nausea / vomiting.
- **Electrolyte powder / packets** (e.g., Pedialyte / oral rehydration salts) — carry enough for both travelers for 3–5 days.
- **Antibiotics for traveler's diarrhea** — some doctors prescribe a standby course (e.g., azithromycin / rifaximin) for high-risk trips; ask if this is appropriate for you.

**Protocol if symptoms start:**
1. **Stop eating solid food.** Drink only clear liquids (water, oral rehydration solution, diluted juice, clear broth). Avoid alcohol, caffeine, dairy.
2. **Use oral rehydration immediately.** Mix electrolyte packets with bottled water. Drink small amounts frequently (every 10–15 minutes).
3. **Rest.** Stay in hotel / near bathroom. If one traveler is sick, the other should handle transport / food / communication — do not both get sick.
4. **When to call 119 / go to hospital:**
   - Severe dehydration (very dark urine / no urine for 8+ hours, dizziness, rapid heartbeat, dry mouth / sunken eyes, confusion).
   - High fever (>39°C / 102°F) with diarrhea / vomiting.
   - Blood or black color in stool / vomit.
   - Severe abdominal pain that doesn't improve.
   - Symptoms lasting >48 hours with no improvement.
   - You cannot keep any fluids down for >12 hours.
5. **Medical card:** Show `checklists/medical-alert-card.md` to doctor; show `checklists/translation-card.md` for symptoms.
6. **Insurance:** Call your travel insurance 24/7 claim line if hospitalization is needed; ask 1330 or hotel for nearest international clinic.

**Prevention:**
- Eat at busy, popular restaurants (high turnover = fresh food).
- Avoid raw / undercooked seafood, raw eggs, unpasteurized dairy, street food with unknown sourcing.
- Drink **bottled water** (or boiled / filtered); avoid ice unless from a reliable source.
- Wash hands before eating (carry hand sanitizer with >60% alcohol — available at any convenience store).

## Scenario E — Crowd Crush / Stampede / Panic in Dense Area

**Risk:** Itaewon / Hongdae (weekend nights), Myeongdong market, Busan Haeundae events (if any in Nov — check VISITKOREA S9), subway rush hour, CSAT test-site morning crowds.

**Prevention / awareness:**
- Avoid bottlenecked alleys / narrow corridors in crowds.
- If you feel pressure / can't move freely, **move to the edge of the crowd / find an exit immediately** — don't wait.
- Don't wear headphones / don't look at your phone in dense crowds — stay aware.
- If one of you starts feeling panic / dizziness, move to an open space / near a police officer (in uniform — safe to ask directions).

**If caught in a crowd crush or panic:**
1. **Move with the crowd — do not fight the current.** Fighting causes more injuries.
2. **Protect your head / neck / chest.** Cross arms over chest; keep elbows out slightly to create space.
3. **Find a wall / pillar / fixed structure** to lean against if you can't move.
4. **Don't stop in the middle** — you risk being trampled. Keep moving toward exits / open areas.
5. If you fall: **curl into fetal position; protect head with arms** — try to get up quickly; ask for help from those near you.
6. Call **112** when safe; ask 1330 if you need translation help; notify US Embassy if you or your companion is injured / traumatized.

## Scenario F — Hotel Fire / Smoke / Building Evacuation

**Prevention:** At check-in, ask front desk: "Where is the nearest fire exit from my room?" Confirm the route; note if elevator is disabled during fire (use stairs). Check that smoke detectors work in room.

**If fire alarm / smoke in building:**
1. **Leave immediately.** Use stairs — never elevator. Feel door handle — if hot, do not open; seal door with wet towel / clothing; call 119 from inside.
2. **Go to pre-agreed meeting point** (outside, away from building, near other travelers / hotel staff). Confirm both travelers are safe.
3. **Do not re-enter** for belongings / phones / passports — only if staff confirm it's safe and you have someone watching you.
4. **Call 119** from outside; ask for fire / rescue; inform hotel management; ask concierge if you need alternative accommodation (hotel should arrange at their expense if they are at fault — but for safety, confirm with staff).
5. **Notify CA emergency contact** once safe; use backup phone / hotel phone / ask staff to call for you.

## Scenario G — Financial Emergency (Lost Cards / Cash / Need Emergency Wire)

**Before travel:**
- Write down bank international collect numbers (call your bank before 31 Oct; ask for international collect / 24-hour line).
- Carry at least **one backup credit/debit card** in a separate bag / pocket from your primary cards.
- Carry **₩50,000–100,000 cash** per traveler in separate locations (wallet + travel pouch + hidden in bag).
- Confirm with CA emergency contact: they can wire money via **Western Union / MoneyGram** to a major station / bank near your hotel if needed; know the nearest location before you leave.

**If cards lost / stolen / frozen:**
1. Call bank using international number (from backup phone / hotel phone / public phone).
2. Ask bank to freeze / cancel cards; ask for emergency replacement if they can mail to Korea (usually 3–7 days — not fast enough for a 22 Nov trip; better to rely on backup card + cash + wire).
3. Use **backup card**; use **cash**; ask hotel / concierge for temporary help (most major hotels can help with emergency cash / card replacement or direct billing to your room if you can provide card info over phone).
4. If you need an emergency wire: ask CA contact to send via Western Union / MoneyGram to a major bank / station near your hotel (Seoul Station / Busan Station / Daejeon Station have branches / agents). You will need your passport + receipt number.
5. If you have travel insurance that covers lost/stolen baggage / cards, call claim line; keep police report (if theft) and bank notification records.

## Scenario H — Transport Emergency (Taxi Accident / KTX Delay / Missed Connection / Lost in City)

**Taxi accident / unsafe driver:**
1. Call **112** (police) if injury / serious accident.
2. Note taxi number / license plate / driver info / time / location.
3. Don't agree to pay unofficial fees; use Kakao T (app) to get fare estimate; ask for receipt (택시 영수증); take photo of meter / receipt.
4. If injured: go to nearest hospital (119 ambulance if serious); show `checklists/medical-alert-card.md`; call 1330 for nearest international clinic.

**KTX delay / missed connection (especially 8 Nov / 13 Nov / 20 Nov):**
1. Go to station customer service / information desk (English available at major stations).
2. Use **Korail / LetsKorail app** or ask staff for next available seat.
3. If you miss your connection and need a hotel / transport: ask station staff / 1330 / hotel concierge for help; use backup plan (hotel near station, taxi, or night if needed).
4. Build **3-hour buffers** on all inter-city travel days; do not schedule non-refundable events on those days.

**Lost in city / can't find hotel / language barrier with taxi:**
1. Show taxi driver your **hotel address written in Korean** (save in notes / print on paper).
2. If lost / confused: ask any police officer (in uniform — usually safe) or go to nearest major station / major hotel / convenience store (CU / GS25 — staff often speak basic English).
3. Call **1330** for directions / translation help; ask for English-speaking operator.
4. Use **Naver Maps / KakaoMap** (offline maps downloaded in advance) to show driver where you want to go.

## Scenario I — Air Quality Emergency / Fine Dust Spike (Nov — possible)

**Check:** AirKorea (`https://www.airkorea.or.kr`, S13) each morning — if AQI is yellow / orange / red, wear KF94 / N95 masks (available at convenience stores / pharmacies).

**If AQI spikes suddenly:**
- Close windows; turn on air purifier if hotel has one; avoid outdoor exercise / long walks; stay indoors with air conditioning / filtration.
- If someone has asthma / respiratory issues: use rescue medication; seek medical help if severe symptoms (wheezing / difficulty breathing / chest tightness).
- If you must go outside: wear mask; avoid areas near highways / construction; keep windows closed in taxi / subway.
- Monitor AirKorea daily; if red / very unhealthy: plan indoor activities (museums / shopping / restaurants with good ventilation).
