# 4. Health, Medications & Insurance

Sources: CDC [S6], U.S. Embassy [S2][S4], MFDS [S11].

## Vaccinations & routine health (CDC) [S6]
Be current on **routine vaccines** before travel:
- MMR, Tdap, varicella, polio, and a current-season flu shot.

CDC travel-vaccine guidance for South Korea:
| Vaccine | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| **Hepatitis A** | Recommended for most travelers (food/water risk) |
| **Typhoid** | Recommended for most travelers |
| **Hepatitis B** | Consider (blood/body-fluid risk) |
| **Japanese encephalitis** | Higher risk **May–Oct**; lower for a November trip, but consider if rural outdoor activities |
| **Rabies** | Consider if hiking/extended rural stay or animal contact |

- **No mandatory vaccinations** to enter Korea, and **no COVID-19 test or
  quarantine requirement** currently. Re-check the CDC page close to
  departure. [S6]

## Food & water safety [S6]
- Tap water in Korea is generally treated and considered potable, but most
  visitors drink bottled water. Safe to drink bottled/boiled water.
- Choose reputable, busy restaurants. Eat fully cooked food.
- Korea is famous for seafood — be cautious with **raw** shellfish/raw fish
  (hwe) if you have a sensitive stomach.

## Air quality / fine dust (미세먼지) — relevant to November
- **Yellow/fine dust** can spike in late fall/winter. Check daily air-quality
  data on **AirKorea (airkorea.or.kr)** [S13].
- Pack **KF94 or N95 masks** if anyone is sensitive to particulates.
- On high fine-dust days, limit long outdoor periods and keep windows closed.

## Medicines — critical rules ⚠️ [S2][S4][S11]
Korea has **strict** drug laws. **U.S. citizenship offers no protection**
under Korean law, and penalties can include arrest/deportation.

### Never bring (even with a prescription)
- **Marijuana, CBD oil, hemp / cannabis derivatives** — strictly prohibited,
  even on a layover, even with a U.S. prescription. Do not bring.
- **Opiates / narcotics** (e.g., codeine, tramadol, oxycodone),
  **amphetamines** (e.g., Adderall), **benzodiazepines** (e.g., Xanax,
  Valium) — generally prohibited; if medically essential you must obtain
  **advance import approval from Korea's Ministry of Food & Drug Safety
  (MFDS)** *before* travel. This takes time; contact MFDS at
  `narcotics@korea.kr` / +82-43-719-2813 and allow ≥2 weeks. [S4][S11]
- **ADHD meds** (methylphenidate/Ritalin) — require advance MFDS approval.
- Some **cold/allergy decongestants** containing **pseudoephedrine** are
  controlled in Korea — check the MFDS permitted list before packing any.

### Allowed with proper documentation
For **non-narcotic, non-amphetamine** prescription medications you may carry
up to **~6 bottles / ~3-month supply for personal use** if you bring:
1. **Original prescription(s)**
2. A **letter from your doctor** stating the medical condition
3. A **statement** about the medicines being brought

Keep all medication in **original labeled packaging**.
The **Korean Customs Service at Incheon has final authority** over what is
admitted. [S4]

### Action items (well before 1 Nov 2026)
- [ ] Review every medication (Rx + OTC) against the MFDS restricted list.
- [ ] Get original prescriptions + doctor's letters for any controlled/
      stimulant medication and submit MFDS import application if required.

## Travel insurance
- Strongly recommended. Get a policy covering **medical care, emergency
  evacuation, trip interruption, and lost baggage**.
- Note your insurer's **24/7 emergency line** and keep the policy number +
  claims number in the contact sheet (`03-emergency-contacts.md`).
- Confirm your California health plan doesn't cover abroad (most don't) so you
  know you need the travel policy.

## If you get sick or injured in Korea
- Minor: pharmacies (약국, *yak-guk*) sell OTC items; a pharmacist can advise.
- Moderate/serious: call **119** for an ambulance, or go to a large hospital
  with an international clinic (see `03`). Hospitals accept walk-ins and
  payment; keep receipts for insurance claims.
- **1330** can help locate English-speaking medical facilities.
