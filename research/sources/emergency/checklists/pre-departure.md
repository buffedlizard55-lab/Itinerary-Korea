# Pre-Departure Checklist
Run this **2–3 weeks before 31 Oct 2026** and again in the final week.

## Documents & entry
- [ ] U.S. passports valid for the full stay (≥6 months best practice)
- [ ] No K-ETA needed — exemption runs through 31 Dec 2026 (re-verify)
- [ ] **Submit free e-Arrival Card** within 72 h of landing → e-arrivalcard.go.kr
- [ ] Return/onward ticket + accommodation addresses on hand
- [ ] 3 copies of docs (carry-on, main bag, secure cloud) — see 02
- [ ] Enroll in **STEP** → step.state.gov

## Health & meds
- [ ] Up to date on routine vaccines (MMR, Tdap, flu)
- [ ] Review every Rx/OTC against MFDS restricted list (04)
- [ ] Get original prescriptions + doctor's letters for any medication
- [ ] MFDS advance approval if bringing any controlled/stimulant med (allow ≥2 weeks)
- [ ] **Nothing containing cannabis/CBD** packed
- [ ] Travel insurance active; save 24/7 emergency line + policy number

## Gear & packing
- [ ] Layers for 2–12 °C weather; warm jacket, scarf, gloves, beanie
- [ ] KF94/N95 masks (fine dust)
- [ ] Basic first-aid kit + personal meds
- [ ] Universal adapter (Type C/F, 220V)
- [ ] Power bank, offline maps (Naver/Kakao) downloaded
- [ ] T-money card (buy on arrival) or plan to buy immediately

## Money & connectivity
- [ ] Notify bank of travel; carry 2 payment methods + some KRW
- [ ] eSIM / travel SIM or pocket Wi-Fi arranged
- [ ] Confirm plan for late-night arrival transport (AREX/night bus/taxi) — 07

## Traffic/holiday awareness
- [ ] Note **CSAT 19 Nov** — build in time; avoid ~1 PM flights that day (06)
- [ ] Confirm no holiday conflicts (none in 1–22 Nov window)
