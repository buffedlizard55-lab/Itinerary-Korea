## ⛩️ Seoul Route A: Historic Core & Palaces
**Focus:** Royal heritage, hanok alleyways, UNESCO kimchi museum & street food
**Distance:** ~7 km walk + subway · **Pace:** Full day (09:30 – 21:00)
**Subway start:** Line 3 Gyeongbokgung Stn (Exit 5) · **Subway end:** Lines 2/4/5 Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Stn

### 🚶 Step-by-Step Itinerary
- **09:30 AM — Gyeongbokgung Palace & Royal Guard Ceremony**
  - Enter Gyeongbokgung Palace ([royal.khs.go.kr](https://royal.khs.go.kr); ₩3,000 adult, **free if wearing hanbok**; closed Tuesdays). Catch the official **Royal Guard Changing Ceremony at 10:00 AM** at Gwanghwamun Gate.
- **11:30 AM — Museum Kimchikan (Insadong)**
  - Walk 10 min to Insadong-gil. Visit Museum Kimchikan ([kimchikan.com](http://www.kimchikan.com); ₩5,000 admission, 40-min hands-on kimchi class ~₩16,000, closed Mondays).
- **01:00 PM — Lunch & Bukchon Hanok Village Walk**
  - Walk up through Anguk to Bukchon Hanok Village and Samcheong-dong. Grab lunch (traditional kalguksu or samgyetang). Explore preserved Joseon-era hanok alleys and craft shops.
- **03:00 PM — Changdeokgung Palace & Secret Garden (Huwon)**
  - Walk to Changdeokgung Palace ([cdg.go.kr](https://www.cdg.go.kr); ₩3,000 palace + ₩5,000 Secret Garden; closed Mondays). Enjoy peak autumn foliage in the Secret Garden (**English tour ~14:30 or 15:30**; book online 6 days prior at 10:00 AM).
- **05:30 PM — Gwangjang Market Feast**
  - Walk 15 min south to Gwangjang Market ([kwangjangmarket.co.kr](http://www.kwangjangmarket.co.kr); open daily ~09:00–22:00). Feast on *bindaetteok* (mung bean pancakes ~₩5,000), *mayak gimbap* (~₩3,000), and *yukhoe* (beef tartare ~₩19,000) with makgeolli.
- **07:30 PM — Cheonggyecheon Stream Walk to DDP Light Show**
  - Walk along Cheonggyecheon stream to Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP). Watch the free nightly **DDP Dream in Light show** ([ddp.or.kr](https://ddp.or.kr); hourly 18:00–22:00, ~25 min).

<a name="seoul-route-b"></a>
## 🏙️ Seoul Route B: Skyline, River & Hip Vibes
**Focus:** Modern architecture, River cruise, Seongsu cafes & Seoul Sky observatory
**Distance:** ~6 km walk + subway · **Pace:** Full day (10:00 – 21:30)
**Subway start:** Line 5 Yeouinaru Stn (Exit 1) · **Subway end:** Lines 2/8 Jamsil Stn

### 🚶 Step-by-Step Itinerary
- **10:00 AM — Yeouido & The Hyundai Seoul ALT.1**
  - Stroll Yeouido Park and explore The Hyundai Seoul ([thehyundai.com](https://www.thehyundai.com)). Visit the ALT.1 gallery for the *BANKSY: Still Here* exhibition (through Nov 3; ₩18,000 adult) or rooftop indoor garden *Sound Forest*.
- **01:00 PM — Han River Cruise or River Bus**
  - Walk to Yeouido Dock for an Eland Han River Cruise ([elandcruise.com](https://www.elandcruise.com); ~₩20,000–30,000) or ride the commuter Han River Bus ([hgbus.co.kr](https://www.hgbus.co.kr)) along the riverbank.
- **03:00 PM — Seongsu-dong Cafe Street & Pop-Up Shops**
  - Take Subway Line 2 to Seongsu Station. Explore Seongsu-dong Cafe Street ([visitseoul.net](https://english.visitseoul.net)) — visit Daerim Warehouse red-brick gallery cafe, Seongsu Yeonbang, and hip fashion pop-ups.
- **06:00 PM — Jamsil Seokchon Lake Foliage Walk**
  - Take Line 2 to Jamsil Station. Stroll around Seokchon Lake Park surrounding Lotte World Magic Island for peak autumn maple reflections.
- **07:30 PM — Seoul Sky (Lotte World Tower 117F–123F)**
  - Ascend the 555m Lotte World Tower via the 600m/min Sky Shuttle elevator to Seoul Sky ([seoulsky.lotteworld.com](https://seoulsky.lotteworld.com); ₩31,000 adult). Stand on the 478m glass-floor Sky Deck and admire 360° night panoramas of Seoul.

<a name="seoul-route-c"></a>
## 🚠 Seoul Route C: Namsan Heights & Culture
**Focus:** Myeongdong street food, Mt. Namsan foliage cable car, N Seoul Tower & Itaewon art
**Distance:** ~5 km walk + cable car/bus · **Pace:** Half to full day (11:00 – 20:30)
**Subway start:** Line 4 Myeongdong Stn (Exit 3) · **Subway end:** Line 6 Hangangjin Stn (Exit 1)

### 🚶 Step-by-Step Itinerary
- **11:00 AM — Myeongdong Shopping & Kalguksu**
  - Explore Myeongdong shopping street. Enjoy lunch at Myeongdong Kyoja (famous Michelin-bib-gourmand chopped noodles & dumplings).
- **01:00 PM — Namsan Orumi & Namsan Cable Car**
  - Walk to Namsan Tunnel 3 entrance and ride the free inclined glass elevator (*Namsan Orumi*). Board the Namsan Cable Car ([cablecar.co.kr](https://www.cablecar.co.kr); ₩15,000 adult round-trip / ₩12,000 one-way) soaring over autumn foliage to the top of Mt. Namsan.
- **02:30 PM — N Seoul Tower Observatory & Love Locks Plaza**
  - Visit the Love Locks Plaza and ascend N Seoul Tower Observatory ([nseoultower.co.kr](https://www.nseoultower.co.kr/visit/use2.asp); official adult **₩29,000** / child & senior **₩23,000**; hours 10:00–22:30 weekdays / 10:00–23:00 weekends, last entry 30 min before close). Check the air quality via the tower's evening LED illumination color scheme.
- **04:30 PM — Scenic Foliage Hike Down Namsan Park to Itaewon**
  - Hike down the wooden stair paths through Namsan Park's maple forest (or take Yellow Bus 01) toward Itaewon / Hannam-dong.
- **06:00 PM — Leeum Museum of Art & Hannam Dining**
  - Visit Leeum Museum of Art ([leeumhoam.org](https://www.leeumhoam.org); Hannam-dong). Explore postwar installations (*Inside Other Spaces* exhibition through Nov 29). End the evening with dinner and drinks in Itaewon/Hannam-dong.

<a name="busan-route-a"></a>
## 🌊 Busan Route A: Coastal Icons & Spa
**Focus:** Ocean views, world-class jjimjilbang spa, BEXCO & Gwangalli drone show
**Distance:** Subway Line 2 corridor · **Pace:** Full day (09:30 – 21:30)
**Subway start:** Line 2 Haeundae Stn (Exit 5) · **Subway end:** Line 2 Gwangan Stn (Exit 3 or 5)

### 🚶 Step-by-Step Itinerary
- **09:30 AM — Haeundae Beach & Dongbaek Island Coastal Walk**
  - Walk Haeundae Beach and follow the coastal wooden boardwalk around Dongbaek Island ([english.visitbusan.net](https://english.visitbusan.net)) past APEC Nuri Maru House and lighthouse views of Gwangan Bridge.
- **12:00 PM — Shinsegae Centum City & Spa Land Relaxation**
  - Take Line 2 to Centum City. Enjoy lunch at Shinsegae Food Hall. Spend 3 hours unwinding at **Spa Land** ([department.shinsegae.com](https://department.shinsegae.com); ~₩20,000–25,000) — natural hot spring baths, 13 themed saunas, and outdoor footbaths.
- **03:30 PM — BEXCO / G-STAR Expo or Busan Cinema Center**
  - Walk across the street to BEXCO ([bexco.co.kr](https://www.bexco.co.kr); host of G-STAR gaming expo Nov 19–22) or visit the futuristic Busan Cinema Center ([biff.kr](https://www.biff.kr)), home of the Busan International Film Festival.
- **06:00 PM — Gwangalli Beach Raw Fish / Grilled Clam Dinner**
  - Take Line 2 to Gwangan Station and walk to Gwangalli Beach. Enjoy fresh seafood or pork국밥 (*dwaeji gukbap*) at a beachfront restaurant overlooking illuminated Gwangan Bridge.
- **07:00 PM / 09:00 PM — Gwangalli M Drone Light Show**
  - Watch the free **Gwangalli M Drone Light Show** ([gwangallimdrone.co.kr](https://www.gwangallimdrone.co.kr); Saturdays 19:00 & 21:00 in winter/Nov) featuring 1,000+ synchronized LED drones over the sea. *(Note: On Sat Nov 7, replaced by the massive Busan Fireworks Festival).*

<a name="busan-route-b"></a>
## 🚢 Busan Route B: Old Town & Harbor Heritage
**Focus:** Cultural hillside village, sea cable car, historic fish market & harbor tower
**Distance:** ~6 km walk + bus/cable car · **Pace:** Full day (09:30 – 21:00)
**Subway start:** Line 1 Toseong Stn (Exit 6) · **Subway end:** Line 1 Nampo Stn (Exit 1 or 7)

### 🚶 Step-by-Step Itinerary
- **09:30 AM — Gamcheon Culture Village**
  - Take Line 1 to Toseong Stn, then local bus Saha 1-1 to Gamcheon Culture Village ([english.visitbusan.net](https://english.visitbusan.net)). Walk through colorful terraced hillside alleys, murals, and Little Prince photo spots.
- **12:00 PM — Songdo Marine Cable Car**
  - Take a short taxi/bus to Songdo Beach. Ride the Songdo Marine Cable Car ([busanaircruise.co.kr](http://busanaircruise.co.kr); ₩17,000 adult round-trip / ₩22,000 glass-floor Crystal Cabin) soaring over the ocean to Songdo Sky Park & Suspension Bridge.
- **02:30 PM — Jagalchi Fish Market & BIFF Square**
  - Take bus/subway to Jagalchi Station. Explore Jagalchi Market — Korea's largest seafood market. Pick fresh fish downstairs and have it cooked upstairs. Walk across to BIFF Square for famous *ssat씨앗 hotteok* (seed-stuffed sweet pancakes ~₩2,000).
- **05:30 PM — Gwangbok-ro Escalators & Yongdusan Park**
  - Walk along Gwangbok-ro fashion street and ride the covered outdoor escalators up to Yongdusan Park. See the Statue of Admiral Yi Sun-sin and Citizens' Bell.
- **07:00 PM — Busan Tower (Diamond Tower) Night View**
  - Ascend Busan Tower Observatory ([bisco.or.kr](https://www.bisco.or.kr); ₩12,000 adult, **free with Visit Busan Pass**). Enjoy 360° views of Busan Harbor, Yeongdo Bridge, and the interactive fireworks window projection mapping show.

<a name="daejeon-route"></a>
## 🧪 Daejeon Route: Science, Hot Springs & Heritage Cafes
**Focus:** 1920s railway hanok cafes, legendary bakery pilgrimage, hot springs & Expo science tower
**Distance:** ~5 km walk + subway/bus · **Pace:** Full day (10:00 – 20:30)
**Subway start:** Daejeon KTX Station (East Exit) · **Subway end:** Line 1 Yuseong Spa Stn / Expo Park

### 🚶 Step-by-Step Itinerary
- **10:00 AM — Sojaedong Cafe Street (Daejeon Stn East Exit)**
  - Arrive at Daejeon Station via KTX (50 min from Seoul). Walk 3 minutes out East Exit to **Sojaedong Cafe Street** ([daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)). Sip specialty tea at *Pungryu* (bamboo garden hanok teahouse) inside restored 1920s railway worker heritage houses.
- **11:30 AM — Sungsimdang Bakery & Cake Boutique**
  - Walk through Daejeon Station West Exit to Sungsimdang Bakery ([sungsimdang.co.kr](http://www.sungsimdang.co.kr); est. 1956). Buy hot *튀김소보로 (fried soboro)* and visit *Sungsimdang Cake Boutique* & *Culture Space* next door.
- **01:30 PM — Yuseong Hot Springs Footbath Park**
  - Take Daejeon Subway Line 1 from Daejeon Stn to Yuseong Spa Stn. Enjoy a free outdoor natural thermal footbath at Yuseong Footbath Park ([daejeontour.co.kr](https://daejeontour.co.kr)).
- **03:30 PM — Expo Science Park, National Science Museum & Shinsegae Art Space 193**
  - Take bus/taxi 10 min to Expo Science Park. Visit the National Science Museum ([science.go.kr](https://www.science.go.kr); free main hall) and ascend Shinsegae Art Space 193 Observatory ([shinsegae.com](https://www.shinsegae.com); ₩15,000 adult) on floor 38-39 for Ólafur Elíasson's media art gallery over Gapcheon river.
- **06:30 PM — Hanbit Tower Observatory & Night Media Facade / Musical Fountain**
  - Visit Hanbit Tower Observatory ([djto.kr](https://www.djto.kr); ₩2,500 adult). Watch the free evening **Media Facade light show** projected on the 93m tower and **Musical Fountain** performance over Hanbit Plaza *(anchor site for Wine EXPO Nov 6–8 and Noodle Festival Nov 7–9)*.

<a name="cheonan-route"></a>
## 🥮 Cheonan Route: Heritage & Walnut Pastries
**Focus:** National independence history, original 1934 walnut pastries & stadium sports
**Distance:** ~4 km walk + local bus · **Pace:** Half to full day (10:00 – 17:30)
**Subway start:** Cheonan-Asan KTX Stn / Cheonan Stn · **Subway end:** Cheonan General Stadium

### 🚶 Step-by-Step Itinerary
- **10:00 AM — Hakwha Hodugwaja (The Original Walnut Pastry, est. 1934)**
  - Arrive at Cheonan Stn or Cheonan-Asan KTX. Visit Hakwha Hodugwaja ([hodo1934.com](https://www.hodo1934.com)) at the terminal crossroads branch. Get warm, freshly baked walnut pastries (*hodu-gwaja* ~₩10,000/box).
- **11:30 AM — Independence Hall of Korea (독립기념관)**
  - Take Bus 380 / 400 (~20 min) to Independence Hall of Korea ([i815.or.kr](https://www.i815.or.kr); free admission; Nov hours 09:30–17:00, closed Mon). Walk the expansive autumn foliage gardens, Black Eagle monument plaza, and 6 historical exhibition halls.
- **02:00 PM — Cheonan General Stadium / Yu Gwan-sun Gym Match Day**
  - Take local bus to Cheonan General Stadium precinct ([cheonancityfc.kr](https://cheonancityfc.kr) / [kovo.co.kr](https://www.kovo.co.kr)). Catch a **Cheonan City FC K League 2 match** (e.g. Sun Nov 8 vs Busan IPark at 14:00; ~₩10,000–15,000) or a **Hyundai Capital Skywalkers V-League volleyball match** at Yu Gwan-sun Gym.
