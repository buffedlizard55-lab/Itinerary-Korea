# Trip Itinerary & Night Split

**Trip:** Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026 (22 nights) · **Cities:** Seoul → Gyeongju → Busan

## Recommended split

| City | Nights | Dates | Why |
|------|--------|-------|-----|
| **Seoul** | 8 | Oct 31 – Nov 8 | Biggest city, main airport/gateway. Base for day trips (DMZ, Suwon). |
| **Gyeongju** | 6 | Nov 8 – Nov 14 | Compact heritage capital. Relaxed pace + day trips (Bulguksa, Yangdong Village). |
| **Busan** | 8 | Nov 14 – Nov 22 | Large, spread out — split between Haeundae (beach) and Nampo (old town). |

**Why this order:** Seoul is the transport hub (ICN/GMP airports, KTX hub). Taking the
KTX down to Gyeongju, then onward to Busan, flows geographically south. Gyeongju sits
between them and has a direct KTX connection to Busan.

**Alternative splits**
- *More Seoul:* 10 / 4 / 8 (Gyeongju becomes a long weekend).
- *More Gyeongju:* 8 / 7 / 7 (slower heritage immersion).

## Getting between cities

| Leg | Mode | Time |
|-----|------|------|
| Seoul → Gyeongju | KTX high-speed rail (from Seoul Station) | ~2 hr |
| Gyeongju → Busan | KTX or intercity bus | ~0.5–1.5 hr |
| (optional) Busan → Seoul | KTX | ~2.5–3 hr |

Book KTX in advance via **Korail / Let's Korail** or **Korail Talk** for discount fares.
November is low-ish shoulder season, so trains and hotels are generally more available
than spring/autumn-peak periods — but Gyeongju's best hanok properties still sell out,
so book those early.

## Autumn note (Oct 31 – Nov 22)

This is prime **fall foliage** season in Korea. Expect mild, crisp days (roughly
5–15°C) and colorful leaves at Gyeongju's Bulguksa, Seoul's palaces, and Busan's
mountains. Weather and rates are generally favorable — a good time for value.
