### Gyeongju — ⚠️ no hotel meets the station rule

**Gyeongju has no subway, and the KTX station (Singyeongju) sits ~7 miles outside the
city centre** (about a 15–25 min taxi/bus ride from the Old Town or Bomun Lake). So there is
**no hotel in Gyeongju within a 1–15 minute walk of a train/subway station.** If you want to
stay in Gyeongju, your realistic choices are:

- **Relax the station rule for Gyeongju** and take a taxi/bus from Singyeongju (≈₩10,000–12,000
  taxi, or ~₩2 bus). Almost everyone does this.
