# Busan — Hotels & Booking Guide

**Recommended:** 8 nights (Nov 14 – Nov 22). Busan is spread across two personalities —
split your stay between **Haeundae** (beach/resort) and **Nampo/Dong-gu** (old town, near
Busan Station & Jagalchi Market).

## Where to stay
- **Haeundae** — beaches, sea views, Grand Josun / L7 / Shilla Stay cluster, easy to Nampo by metro.
- **Busan Station (Dong-gu)** — best for KTX arrivals/departures, Chinatown, harbour views, ASTI hotel.
- **Nampo / Seomyeon** — markets, shopping, most "local" vibe (Jagalchi fish market, BIFF Square).

## Loyalty & deals
- **Shilla Rewards** (Shilla Stay): earn 3% on room / 1% on F&B; tier perks across
  Shilla Stay + Shilla hotels. Book on shillastay.com.
- **LOTTE Members (L7):** member rates, points, flash deals.
- **The Josun Hospitality (Grand Josun):** brand loyalty; best-rate guarantee on josun.com.
- **World of Hyatt (Park Hyatt):** points + member rates on hyatt.com.

## Rules to know
- Shilla Stay Haeundae: min. check-in age 20; guests 18+ charged as adults; no pets; no cots/extra beds.
- Grand Josun / L7: check-out 11:00 (earlier than the 12:00 norm — plan your final morning).
- Haeundae is ~35–40 min by metro/limousine from Gimhae Airport; Busan Station hotels are
  ideal on the day you arrive/leave by KTX.

## Get around
- Use the Busan Metro (Lines 1 & 2) + city buses; taxi app **Kakao T**.
- Day-trip options: Taejongdae, Gamcheon Culture Village, Beomeosa Temple, Gwangalli Beach at night.
