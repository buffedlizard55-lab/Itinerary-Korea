# Promotions, Deals & Loyalty Programs

Booking smart in Korea usually means: **join the hotel chain's free loyalty program,
book direct, and watch for periodic flash sales.** Below is a cheat-sheet for the chains
featured in this repo, with official links.

## How to save the most
1. **Book direct on the official site** for the featured hotels — that's where member
   rates, packages, and best-rate guarantees live.
2. **Join the free loyalty program before booking** (takes minutes; unlocks member-only rates).
3. **Watch flash sales:** LOTTE *Flash Deals*, Accor ALL *Private Sales*, Hilton/Shilla seasonal offers.
4. **Cross-check one OTA** (Agoda/Booking/Trip.com/KAYAK) before confirming — Korea OTAs
   sometimes beat the standard public rate, but rarely beat a member/direct rate.
5. **Book early** — especially Gyeongju hanoks and peak-foliage weekends in early November.

## Booking-platform notes
- **Agoda & Booking.com** are the most reliable OTAs for Korea and often have the best
  OTA pricing / coupons.
- **KAYAK / Google Hotels / momondo** are good comparison metasearch across OTAs.
- **Kakao T** (taxi app) and **Naver/Kakao Maps** are must-haves on the ground.
