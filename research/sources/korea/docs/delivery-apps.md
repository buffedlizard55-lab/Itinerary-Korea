## 🥇 1. Shuttle Delivery — The Easiest Option for US Tourists

Built specifically for international visitors, English speakers, and expats in Seoul, Busan, and Pyeongtaek.

### Why It Works for US Citizens
- **No Korean Phone Verification (`본인인증`):** Sign up with your US email address and US phone number.
- **100% English UI & Customer Support:** Menus, addresses, and customer chat are entirely in English.
- **US Credit Cards Accepted:** Pay instantly with your US Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or Apple Pay.
- **Zero ID Check on Delivery:** Your food is dropped off at your hotel lobby or door with no passport or ID check.

### How to Save on Shuttle (Welcome Deals)
- **Klook Discounted E-Gift Vouchers:** Before ordering, buy a Shuttle e-gift voucher on Klook in USD (often discounted 10–15% below face value) and redeem the code at checkout.
- 🔗 Official Shuttle Klook page: <https://www.klook.com/activity/120381-food-delivery-in-korea/>

## 🥈 2. Baemin (`배달의민족`) — Largest Selection via Guest Checkout (`비회원 주문`)

South Korea's #1 food delivery platform launched a **multi-language UI (English, Chinese, Japanese)** on February 4, 2026, and expanded international payment support.

### How US Citizens Bypass `본인인증` on Baemin
While registering a *full verified account* on Baemin requires a Korean mobile number with `본인인증`, **US tourists can order without an account**:
1. Download **Baemin** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. In the app settings, switch the interface language to **English**.
3. When checking out, select **Guest Checkout (`비회원 주문`)**.
4. Enter your hotel address (use Korean address string or landmark search).
5. Pay using your **US Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Alipay+, or WeChat Pay**.

### Welcome / First-Order Deals
- First-time guest orders often trigger home-screen banner coupons worth **₩10,000 off** (minimum spend ~₩15,000—a standard fried chicken order clears this easily).
- 🔗 Official App Store listing: <https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id378084485> · 🟢 [English UI launch news (Feb 2026)](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/2972/baemin-starts-in-app-support-for-english-chinese-and-japanese)

## 🚫 Why US Citizens Should Skip Coupang Eats & Yogiyo

- **Coupang Eats (`쿠팡이츠`):** While popular among Korean residents for fast delivery, account creation requires **`본인인증`** tied to a Korean mobile number and usually a domestic bank card or Wow membership.
- **Yogiyo (`요기요`):** Entirely Korean UI, requires a Korean mobile number for account creation, and international credit cards frequently fail at checkout.

## 🏨 Crucial Tips for Hotel Delivery in South Korea

1. **No Tipping Required:** Never add a tip for delivery drivers in Korea. The total shown at checkout is final.
2. **Hotel Lobby Pickup:** Most Seoul/Busan hotels do not allow delivery drivers upstairs to guest rooms. Meet your driver in the ground-floor lobby or outside the main entrance.
3. **No ID Check for Food:** Drivers will hand you your order without asking for identification. *(Important: Do not add alcohol like soju or beer to delivery orders, as alcohol delivery requires a physical ID age check).*
4. **Fresh Phone/Device Rule:** First-order app coupons require a device that has never claimed a welcome coupon before.
