## 🚫 The `#1` Trap to Avoid: Why Most Korean "App Deals" Will Reject You

Many online Korea travel blogs list famous birthday and welcome coupons like **Outback Steakhouse Boomerang (₩10,000 off)**, **Starbucks Korea (free birthday drink)**, **CJ ONE / VIPS (₩10,000 off)**, and **Happy Point / Baskin Robbins**.

**Here is the hard truth for US citizens:**
Almost every native Korean F&B membership app requires **`본인인증` (PASS mobile identity verification)** during registration. This system checks a Korean Citizen Resident Registration Number or Korean Alien Registration Card (ARC) against a domestic Korean telecom contract.
- A US passport, US phone number, or standard tourist eSIM **cannot complete `본인인증`**.
- If you try to register for the Starbucks Korea app or Outback app, you will hit a brick wall at phone verification.

**The Good News:** You do not need those apps! South Korea has an incredible ecosystem of **tourist-accessible, email-verified, KakaoTalk-based, and platform-based food deals and birthday treats** that US citizens can claim in seconds—without ever showing a physical ID.

## 🛡️ The "Zero ID Check" Guarantee at Korean Restaurants

You asked to make sure **you won't need to show ID to claim any promos or birthday rewards**.

In South Korea:
1. **Barcode / QR Code / Kiosk Redemption is 100% Anonymous:** Whether you are scanning a coupon at a self-serve kiosk (`키오스크`), showing a KakaoTalk barcode to a cashier, or presenting an e-voucher from Klook/Creatrip, **staff never ask for physical ID, passport, or proof of birthdate**.
2. **When IS an ID checked in Korea?**
   - **Buying alcohol** at a convenience store, bar, or restaurant (age verification).
   - **Tax refunds (`즉시면세` / `사후면세`)** or shopping at **Duty-Free foreign customer desks** and **Hypermarket checkouts** (requires physical passport).
   - *For food, coffee, bakery treats, delivery apps, and dining coupons, your physical ID stays in your bag.*
