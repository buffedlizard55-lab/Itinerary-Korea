## 🪙 2. Traditional Market (`전통시장`) Food Experiences

Avoid tourist-trap pricing at overcrowded markets and head to these high-value traditional market experiences:

### Tongin Market Brass Coin Lunchbox (`통인시장 엽전도시락`)
- **What it is:** Located 8 minutes from Gyeongbokgung Palace, this market lets you buy traditional brass coins (`엽전`) to build a custom lunchbox from dozens of participating market vendors.
- **Cost:** **₩10,000 per person** gets you **20 brass coins** and a 6-compartment empty lunch tray.
- **What you eat:** Famous oil tteokbokki (`기름떡볶이`), sweet and sour chicken (`닭강정`), tteokgalbi patties, pancake skewers, and japchae.
- **Key rules:** Open Tue–Sun 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM (coin sales end at 3:00 PM on weekdays, 4:00 PM weekends). **Closed every Monday and the 3rd Sunday of each month.** Leftover coins can be refunded!
- 🟢 [Tongin Market Guide](https://www.ivisitkorea.com/tongin-market/) · [2026 Video review](https://www.tiktok.com/@themukjja/video/7547317023567514887)

### Mangwon Market (`망원시장`) vs. Gwangjang Market
- **The Tourist Trap Warning:** Gwangjang Market and Myeongdong street stalls now charge inflated tourist prices (e.g., ₩15,000+ for basic pancakes or tteokbokki).
- **Where to go instead:** **Mangwon Market** (near Hongdae/Hapjeong) offers authentic local prices: handmade croquettes (`고로케`) for ₩1,500, dakgangjeong chicken boxes for ₩5,000–₩8,000, and fresh fruit juices.

## 🏪 3. Convenience Store Monthly 1+1 & 2+1 Promos (Resets Nov 1st!)

South Korea's four major convenience store chains (**CU**, **GS25**, **7-Eleven**, and **emart24**) operate standing promotional systems on drinks, snacks, instant noodles, and lunch boxes.

### How the "+1" System Works
- **1+1 (Buy One, Get One Free):** Grab two identical items with the red "1+1" shelf tag; you are only charged for one at the register.
- **2+1 (Buy Two, Get One Free):** Grab three items with the yellow "2+1" shelf tag; you are charged for two.
- **Mix & Match (`교차 선택`):** Many 2+1 tags allow mixing flavors within the same brand and price tier.
- **ID check?** Absolutely zero ID check for food and drinks. *(Note: Alcohol like beer/soju requires age verification ID).*

### ⏰ Why Your Trip Timing (Oct 31 – Nov 22) is Perfect
Convenience store promotions **reset on the 1st of every month**:
- **Night 1 (Oct 31):** You arrive on the final day of October's promotional slate—perfect for an arrival-night water and snack run.
- **Day 2 (Nov 1):** You wake up to a **brand-new November 1+1 and 2+1 promotional calendar** across every CU, GS25, and 7-Eleven in Korea!

## 🎓 4. University District (`대학가`) Student Pricing & Free Refills

Dining in university commercial districts (**Hongdae** / Hongik Univ, **Sinchon** / Yonsei, **Hyehwa** / Sungkyunkwan, **Anam** / Korea Univ, **Konkuk Univ**) saves you **20% to 30%** compared to Gangnam or Myeongdong:
- **Standing Student Prices:** Pork cutlet (`돈까스`), kimchi stew (`김치찌개`), and bibimbap sets run **₩8,000 to ₩10,000** (vs. ₩13,000+ in tourist zones).
- **Free Rice & Noodle Refills (`밥/면 무한리필`):** Many student-district eateries display signs reading **`밥 무한리필`** (unlimited rice refills) or **`사리 무한리필`** (unlimited noodle refills) at self-serve side tables. No student ID required!

## 🥐 5. Evening Bakery Clearance (8:00 PM – 9:30 PM)

Korean bakeries and department store basement food halls offer steep evening discounts to clear fresh daily inventory before closing:
- **Department Store Food Halls (The Hyundai Seoul B1, Lotte, Shinsegae):** Starting around **7:30 PM to 8:00 PM**, deli counters, sushi stands, and bakeries bundle remaining items at **20% to 40% off** (look for "3 for ₩10,000" or discounted sticker tags).
- **Franchise Bakeries (Paris Baguette, Tous Les Jours):** Individual branches often package unsold breads and pastries into discounted evening bags between **8:30 PM and 9:30 PM**.
- **How to claim:** Pick up the discounted package and pay at the register. **Zero ID check.**

## ☕ 6. Low-Cost Coffee Chains (The ₩1,500 – ₩2,000 Americano)

Instead of paying ₩4,500+ at international chains, South Korea's ubiquitous budget coffee chains serve massive 24oz iced Americanos for **₩1,500 to ₩2,000**:
- **Top chains:** **Mega Coffee (`메가커피`)**, **Compose Coffee (`컴포즈커피`)**, **Paik's Coffee (`빽다방`)**, and **The Venti (`더벤티`)**.
- **How to order:** Use the external touch-screen kiosk (`키오스크`) out front. Switch language to English, select your drink, and pay with your US Visa/Mastercard/Apple Pay. **Zero ID check.**

## 🍽️ 7. Weekday Lunch Specials (`점심 특선`)

Korean BBQ houses, hanjeongsik restaurants, and shabu-shabu spots offer **Weekday Lunch Specials (`점심 특선`)** between **11:00 AM and 2:00 PM**:
- A meal that costs ₩35,000+ per person at dinner is often served as a lunch set (meat + stew + rice + side dishes) for **₩12,000 to ₩16,000**.
- **How to claim:** Visit during weekday lunch hours and select the `점심 특선` menu. **No ID or coupon needed.**

## 🛒 8. Hypermarket Immediate Tax Refund (`즉시환급`) on Edible Gifts & Groceries

While restaurant dining is not tax-exempt, buying packaged Korean food, snacks, tea, coffee, and edible souvenirs at major hypermarkets **IS tax-exempt**:
- **Where it works:** **Lotte Mart** (Seoul Station Branch / Zettaplex), **E-Mart**, and **Homeplus**.
- **The Rule:** Spend **₩15,000 or more** (lowered from ₩30,000 in recent tax rules!) on packaged groceries/snacks to receive an **Immediate Tax Refund (`즉시환급`)** directly at the checkout register, deducting **~6% to 7%** from your bill instantly.
- 🪪 **Crucial ID Notice:** *This is the ONLY food/grocery savings avenue in Korea where you must show your physical US passport at the cashier to scan for tax exemption!*

## 🚫 Why We Ignore Telecom & Resident Card Perks

- **Korean Telecom Carrier Discounts (KT / SKT / LGU+):** Offer 5–15% off at family restaurants and bakeries, but require a domestic resident mobile subscription.
- **Korean Credit Card Points / Perks:** Offer dining discounts, but require a Korean bank account and Alien Registration Card (`ARC`).
- Because our group has **no Korean resident companion**, skip these and use the 8 standing savings avenues above!
