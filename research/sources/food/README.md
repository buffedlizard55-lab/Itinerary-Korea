## 🍁 Timing and booking checklist

* October 31 is Halloween weekend; expect crowds in Hongdae/Itaewon and reserve sought-after BBQ where possible.
* November 11 is Pepero Day; November weekends can be busy. Check whether a restaurant is closed on a regular monthly day, not just Sunday.
* Save the Korean name, phone number, address, and one backup per meal. Confirm last order, break time, and whether foreign cards are accepted.
* Recheck all facts against the linked business page, Naver/Kakao listing, and official tourism page in **October 2026**. A listing is not evidence that a restaurant will still exist.

## 🌟 Research standard

`Official` means the restaurant/group or a government tourism site. `Trusted listing` means Naver/Kakao Maps or Visit Korea/Visit Seoul/Visit Busan. We do not present review-site hours as definitive. Where a price is not published by an official source, it is explicitly labeled an estimate and should not be treated as a quote. See [sources-and-notes.md](sources-and-notes.md).
