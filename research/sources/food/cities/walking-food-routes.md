# Walking food routes — named restaurants only

The previous version of this file claimed “535 verified spots” and “1,000+ unique daily food walks.” Most of those slots were generated placeholders (`Seoul Local Cafe 5`, `Busan Harbour Ramen 2`, `Cheonan Hub BBQ 9`, …). Those names are not real businesses and were removed on **18 Aug 2026**.

Below are neighborhood walks that use **only remaining named places**. Confirm hours, last order, and whether a branch still exists on Naver Map or the official tourism page before you go.

## Seoul

### Insadong / Jongno
- Lunch: **Osegyehyang** (vegan jajang) or **Oreno Ramen Insadong**
- Tea: **Shin Old Tea House**
- Dinner: **Myeongnyun Jinsa Galbi** (AYCE) or **Imun Seolleongtang**

### Myeongdong
- Lunch: **Myeongdong Kyoja** or **Hadongkwan**
- Dinner: **Menten** or **Hong Kong Banjum 0410**

### Gwangjang / Dongdaemun
- Lunch: **Buchon Yukhoe** (Gwangjang)
- Snack: Gwangjang market stalls (bindaetteok / mayak gimbap — pick a stall on the day)
- Dinner: **Jin Ok-hwa Halmae Dakhanmari**

### Seongsu / Wangsimni
- Lunch: **Somunnan Seongsu Gamjatang**
- Dinner: **Daedo Sikdang Wangsimni**

### Hongdae / Hapjeong
- Lunch: **Oreno Ramen Main Store**
- Dinner: **Hong Kong Banjum 0410**

### Mapo / Gongdeok
- Lunch: **Sinseonggak**
- Coffee: **Fritz Coffee Company Dohwa**
- Dinner: **Mapo Jeong Daepo** or **Budnamujip**

### Itaewon / Hannam / Yongsan
- Lunch or dinner: **Nari's House** (confirm hours — typically afternoon/evening)
- Alternate: **Goryeo Samgyetang** (City Hall) if you are closer to that side of Yongsan

### Yaksu / Sindang
- Snack: **Maboklim Halmoni Tteokbokki**
- Dinner: **Geumdwaeji Sikdang** or **Yukjeon Sikdang Branch 1**

## Busan

### Busan Station / Choryang
- Lunch: **Shinbalwon** or **Maga Mandu**
- Coffee: **Brown Hands Baekje**

### Haeundae
- Lunch: **Nagahama Mangetsu**
- Dinner: **Haeundae Somunnan Amso Galbi-jip**

### Seomyeon
- Lunch: **Songjeong Samdae Gukbap** or **Gijang Sonkalguksu**
- Dinner: **Seomyeon Gaegeum Milmyeon**

### Nampo / Jagalchi
- Lunch: **Halmae Gaya Milmyeon** or **Hwaguk Banjeom**
- Snack: **Samjin Amook**
- Dinner: **Busan Jokbal** or **Jagalchi Market** (buy downstairs, cook upstairs)

## Daejeon

### Old downtown
- Bakery: **Sungsimdang Main Store**
- Lunch/dinner: **Ossi Kalguksu** or **Boksu Bunsik**

### Yuseong
- Lunch: **Sutgol Won Naengmyeon**
- Dinner: **Taepyeong Sogukbap** or **Sariwon Myeonok**

## Cheonan

Only one named restaurant survived the placeholder purge: **Dongsunwon** (Seonghwan). For Cheonan meals, treat hodu-gwaja shops and Byeongcheon sundae street as **neighborhoods to research on arrival**, not as a list of invented shop names.
